# Chapter 10: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

Pattern A starts at $5$ and adds $3$ tiles each design. Its nth term is
$3n+2$. Pattern B starts at $2$ and doubles each design. Its term at position
$n$ is $2^n$.

Compare the totals:

$$
n=1: A=5,\quad B=2
$$

$$
n=2: A=8,\quad B=4
$$

$$
n=3: A=11,\quad B=8
$$

$$
n=4: A=14,\quad B=16
$$

Pattern B first has more tiles at design $4$. For design $5$, Pattern A has
$17$ tiles and Pattern B has $32$.

## Mixed practice: choose a method and explain why

For each question, name the type of rule or calculation you will use before
working it out.

1. Continue the sequence $3,8,13,18,\ldots$ by two terms.
2. Find the 20th term of the sequence with nth term $5n-2$.
3. Is $64$ a square number, a cube number, both, or neither?
4. Write the next two triangular numbers after $15$.
5. Find the nth term of $9,13,17,21,\ldots$.
6. Find the nth term of $1,4,9,16,25,\ldots$.
7. Continue $2,6,18,54,\ldots$ by one term.
8. Work out $0.75$ of $240$.
9. Solve $4x+7=31$.
10. A sequence begins $12,7,2,-3,\ldots$. State its common difference and
    find its 10th term.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Add 5 each time: $23,28$.
2. Linear rule: $5(20)-2=98$.
3. Both: $64=8^2=4^3$.
4. Add 6, then 7: $21,28$.
5. Common difference 4, so begin with $4n$. Add 5 to match term 1: $4n+5$.
6. Square numbers, so the nth term is $n^2$.
7. Multiply by 3: $162$.
8. $0.75\times240=180$.
9. $4x=24$, so $x=6$.
10. Common difference $-5$. The 10th term is $12+9(-5)=-33$.

</details>

- find linear, quadratic, and geometric rules;

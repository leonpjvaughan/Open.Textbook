# 10.3 The nth term of a linear sequence — Practise with help

Try each item before opening its hints. Reveal the full working when you want to check your reasoning.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Find the common difference: $6,10,14,\ldots$.
2. Evaluate $3(4)+2$.
3. Write the 3rd term of $2n+1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check your warm-up</summary>

1. $4$.
2. $14$.
3. $7$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find the nth term of $6,9,12,15,\ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The common difference is $3$, so start with $3n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

The first term is 6; $3(1)=3$, so add 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rule is $3n+3$.

</details>
</details>
</details>


**2.** Find the 12th term of $4n-1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Substitute $n=12$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

$4(12)-1=48-1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The 12th term is $47$.

</details>
</details>
</details>


**3.** Find the nth term of $14,10,6,2,\ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The common difference is $-4$, so start with $-4n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

At position 1 this gives $-4$; add 18 to reach 14.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rule is $-4n+18$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

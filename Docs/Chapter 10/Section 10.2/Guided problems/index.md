# 10.2 Special sequences — Practise with help

Try each item before opening its hints. Reveal the full working when you want to check your reasoning.

## Warm-up: remember

1. What is $6^2$?
2. Continue $3,6,9,\ldots$.
3. What is $2^3$?

<summary>Check your warm-up</summary>

1. $36$.
2. $12$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Write the first five square numbers.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Square the positions 1 to 5.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

$1^2=1$, $2^2=4$, $3^2=9$, $4^2=16$, $5^2=25$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$1,4,9,16,25$.

</details>
</details>
</details>


**2.** Find the next two triangular numbers: $1,3,6,10,\ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the next row size each time.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

$10+5=15$, then $15+6=21$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The next terms are $15,21$.

</details>
</details>
</details>


**3.** Continue $4,7,11,18,\ldots$ as a Fibonacci sequence.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the previous two terms.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

$11+18=29$, then $18+29=47$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The next terms are $29,47$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

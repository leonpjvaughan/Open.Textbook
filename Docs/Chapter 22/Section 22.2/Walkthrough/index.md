# 22.2 Adding and subtracting vectors — Learn

**Prerequisites:** column vectors and directed movement from section 22.1.

**Objectives:** add and subtract vectors; multiply a vector by a scalar; show these operations in diagrams and columns.

## Add vectors component by component

When two movements happen in sequence, add their horizontal changes and add
their vertical changes. This is vector addition.

**Example 1.** Work out $\begin{pmatrix}3\\-2\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-5\\4\end{pmatrix}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{pmatrix}3\\-2\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-5\\4\end{pmatrix}
&=\begin{pmatrix}3+(-5)\\-2+4\end{pmatrix} \\
&=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\2\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

The combined movement is 2 left and 2 up.

## Subtract vectors and multiply by a scalar

Subtracting a vector means adding its opposite. Multiplying by a **scalar**—a
number that scales a vector—multiplies each component.

**Example 2.** Work out $2\begin{pmatrix}1\\3\end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix}4\\-2\end{pmatrix}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2\begin{pmatrix}1\\3\end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix}4\\-2\end{pmatrix}
&=\begin{pmatrix}2\\6\end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix}4\\-2\end{pmatrix} \\
&=\begin{pmatrix}2-4\\6-(-2)\end{pmatrix} \\
&=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\8\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

> **Watch out.** The lower component is $6-(-2)=8$, not $4$. Subtracting a
> negative number increases the result. Check by adding the opposite vector.

## Progress check

The chapter route to $C$ is $\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}$. The other route to $C$ is
$\mathbf{v}+\mathbf{u}$. Component addition gives the same result in either
order, so both routes describe the same displacement.

## You should now be able to

- add and subtract vectors by operating on matching components;
- multiply each component by a scalar;
- interpret a negative component as movement in the opposite direction;
- represent a vector calculation with a diagram or a column.

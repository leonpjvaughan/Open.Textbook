# 22.1 Vector notation and column vectors — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Point $A$ has coordinates $(2,1)$. Move it 3 right and 2 down. What are the new coordinates?
2. What is the horizontal change from $x=4$ to $x=-1$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $(5,-1)$: add 3 to the horizontal coordinate and subtract 2 from the vertical coordinate.
2. $-1-4=-5$, so the change is 5 units left.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Point $P=(-2,3)$ is translated by $\begin{pmatrix}4\\-5\end{pmatrix}$. Find its image.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>
Add the top component to the horizontal coordinate and the lower component to the vertical coordinate.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>
The new coordinates are $(-2+4,\ 3-5)$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>
$(2,-2)$, because $-2+4=2$ and $3-5=-2$.
</details>

**2.** Find the vector from $A=(3,-2)$ to $B=(-1,4)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>
Subtract the start coordinates from the end coordinates.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>
The changes are $-1-3$ horizontally and $4-(-2)$ vertically.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>
$\overrightarrow{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}-4\\6\end{pmatrix}$: move 4 left and 6 up.
</details>

**3.** Describe the translation represented by $\begin{pmatrix}-3\\-2\end{pmatrix}$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>
Read the top entry as horizontal movement and the lower entry as vertical movement.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>
Both entries are negative, so both movements are in the negative directions.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>
Move 3 units left and 2 units down. This is a 2D translation vector.
</details>

**4.** An arrow starts at $(-1,2)$ and ends at $(2,0)$. Write its column vector.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>
Find end minus start for each coordinate.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>
Horizontal change $=2-(-1)$; vertical change $=0-2$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>
$\begin{pmatrix}3\\-2\end{pmatrix}$.
</details>

**5.** Draw $\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\end{pmatrix}$ as an arrow from the origin on squared paper.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>
Start at $(0,0)$, then move right before moving up.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>
Mark the endpoint $(2,3)$ and draw an arrow from the origin.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>
The arrow runs from $(0,0)$ to $(2,3)$. Any parallel arrow with the same direction and length represents the same vector.
</details>

**6.** In the chapter problem, write the vector from $A$ to $C$ using the side vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>
Follow the route from $A$ to $B$, then from $B$ to $C$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>
The route is $\mathbf{u}$ followed by $\mathbf{v}$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>
$\overrightarrow{AC}=\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}$. We will calculate such sums in section 22.2.
</details>

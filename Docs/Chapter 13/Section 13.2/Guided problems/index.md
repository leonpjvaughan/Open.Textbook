# 13.2 Trigonometric graphs — Practise with help

Try each question first. Reveal support in order when you need it.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $\sin 30^\circ$.
2. Work out $\cos 60^\circ$.
3. Find the gradient through $(0,0)$ and $(2,4)$.
4. Factorise $x^2-4$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Answers</summary>

$1/2$; $1/2$; $2$; $(x-2)(x+2)$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Sketch $y=\sin x$ from $0^\circ$ to $360^\circ$ using its quarter-turn values.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Mark the values at $0^\circ,90^\circ,180^\circ,270^\circ,360^\circ$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The heights in order are $0,1,0,-1,0$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Plot $(0,0),(90,1),(180,0),(270,-1),(360,0)$ and join smoothly. The period is $360^\circ$.
</details>

**2.** Solve $\sin x=0$ for $0^\circ\leq x\leq360^\circ$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Find where the curve crosses the horizontal axis, including both endpoints.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The sine curve has height zero at the start, halfway point and end of the cycle.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$x=0^\circ,180^\circ,360^\circ$.
</details>

**3.** State the vertical asymptotes of $y=\tan x$ from $-180^\circ$ to $180^\circ$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Tangent is undefined where cosine is zero.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Cosine is zero at $-90^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ in this interval.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The asymptotes are $x=-90^\circ$ and $x=90^\circ$. The branches approach these lines without reaching them.
</details>

**4.** Solve $\cos x=0$ for $0^\circ\leq x\leq360^\circ$ by describing the intersections.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Look where the cosine curve meets the horizontal axis.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The curve crosses at the quarter-turn and three-quarter-turn points.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The intersections occur at $x=90^\circ$ and $x=270^\circ$.
</details>

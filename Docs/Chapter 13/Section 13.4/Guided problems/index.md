# 13.4 The equation of a circle and tangents — Practise with help

Try the questions before opening the support. Reveal each level in order.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Find the gradient between $(0,0)$ and $(2,6)$.
2. Work out $5^2$.
3. State the positive square root of $49$.
4. Solve $x^2=25$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Answers</summary>

$3$; $25$; $7$; $x=-5$ or $x=5$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find the centre and radius of $x^2+y^2=16$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Compare with $x^2+y^2=r^2$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The centre is $(0,0)$ and $r^2=16$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The centre is $(0,0)$. The radius is $\sqrt{16}=4$.
</details>

**2.** Check whether $(3,0)$ is on the circle $x^2+y^2=9$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Substitute both coordinates into the left side.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Calculate $3^2+0^2$ and compare it with $9$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$3^2+0^2=9$. The equation is true, so $(3,0)$ lies on the circle.
</details>

**3.** A radius has gradient $3$. Find the tangent gradient.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Perpendicular gradients are negative reciprocals.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Choose a number that multiplies by $3$ to give $-1$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The tangent gradient is $-1/3$, since $3\times(-1/3)=-1$.
</details>

**4.** Find the tangent equation at $(0,5)$ to $x^2+y^2=25$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The radius to $(0,5)$ is vertical. A perpendicular line is horizontal.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

A horizontal line through the point keeps $y$ fixed at $5$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The tangent equation is $y=5$.
</details>

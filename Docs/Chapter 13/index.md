# Chapter 13: Other functions and graphs

## The chapter problem: reading a changing signal

A sensor records a repeating signal. Its graph crosses the middle value while rising, reaches a peak, falls through the middle, reaches a low point, then repeats.

**First contact:** Predict what comes next. Sketch a curve or describe it. No calculation is needed yet.

## By the end of this chapter you will be able to

- sketch cubic, reciprocal, exponential and trigonometric graphs;
- read intercepts, periods, asymptotes and intersections;
- describe translations, reflections and stretches of graphs;
- use circle equations and perpendicular gradients to find tangents.

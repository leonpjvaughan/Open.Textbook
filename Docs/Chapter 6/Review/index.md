# Chapter 6: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

The account starts with £600 and earns 4% compound interest each year. The multiplier is 1.04, so after two years:

$$
600 × 1.04^2 = £648.96
$$

The balance is £648.96, which is more than £640. The estimate was close enough to predict the direction, and the exact method gives the balance.

## Mixed practice — choose the method first

For each question, name the method before calculating. Use a percentage of an amount, percentage change, reverse percentage, simple interest or compound change.

1. Work out 30% of 250.
2. Increase £75 by 8%.
3. A price after a 20% discount is £96. Find the original price.
4. Find the simple interest on £400 at 3% for 5 years.
5. Find the compound amount for £500 at 4% for 3 years.
6. A value changes from 80 to 92. Work out the percentage increase.
7. A machine worth £6,000 loses 10% of its value yearly for two years. Find its value.
8. Which method would you use for 15% of 360, and why?
9. A £200 value increases by 10%, then decreases by 10%. Find the final value and compare it with £200.
10. Use a ratio from Chapter 5: a drink mix uses 2 parts syrup to 5 parts water. What percentage of the mixture is syrup?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. 75.
2. £81.
3. £120, because £96 is 80% of the original.
4. £60 interest.
5. £562.43 to the nearest penny.
6. 15% increase.
7. £4,860.
8. For example, 10% plus 5%: 36 + 18 = 54.
9. £198, which is £2 less. The multipliers are 1.1 and 0.9.
10. $2/7 × 100\% ≈ 28.6\%$.

</details>

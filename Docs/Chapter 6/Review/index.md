# Chapter 6: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

The account starts with £600 and earns 4% compound interest each year. The multiplier is 1.04, so after two years:

$$
600 × 1.04^2 = 648.96
$$

The balance is £648.96, which is more than £640. The estimate was close enough to predict the direction, and the exact method gives the balance.

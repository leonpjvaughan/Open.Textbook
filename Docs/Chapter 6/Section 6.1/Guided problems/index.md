# 6.1 Percentage of an amount — Practise with help

Try each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write 25% as a decimal.
2. Work out 10% of 90.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check your answers</summary>

1. 0.25. 2. 9.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find 15% of 200.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Split 15% into 10% and 5%.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

10% is 20 and 5% is 10, so 15% is 30.

</details>

**2.** Find 32% of £450.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find 30% and 2%, then add.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

30% is £135; 2% is £9; total £144.

</details>

**3.** Find 7.5% of 160.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use 10% minus 2.5%.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

10% is 16 and 2.5% is 4, so the answer is 12.

</details>

# 20.8 The area of any triangle — Learn

**Prerequisites:** triangle area, sine ratios, and matching sides to angles.

**Objectives:** find the area of a triangle from two sides and their included angle. This teaches DfE G23.

When perpendicular height is unknown, use $\text{Area}=\frac12 ab\sin C$. The angle $C$ must lie between the known sides $a$ and $b$.

**Example 1.** Find the area when two sides are $8$ cm and $5$ cm, with included angle $30^\circ$.

$$\text{Area}=\frac12(8)(5)\sin30^\circ$$

$$\text{Area}=20\times\frac12$$

$$\text{Area}=10\text{ cm}^2$$

**Example 2.** A triangle has sides $7$ cm and $9$ cm with included angle $40^\circ$. Find its area to 3 significant figures.

$$\text{Area}=\frac12(7)(9)\sin40^\circ$$

$$\text{Area}\approx20.25\text{ cm}^2$$

$$\text{Area}=20.3\text{ cm}^2\text{ (3 significant figures)}$$

> **Watch out.** The angle must be between the two given sides. If it is opposite one of them, mark the included angle before using the formula.

## Progress check

For the garden rectangle, use base times perpendicular height for its area. For a slanted triangle, the sine factor supplies the perpendicular height.

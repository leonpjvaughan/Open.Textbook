# 20.7 The cosine rule — Learn

**Prerequisites:** cosine, square roots, and rearranging equations.

**Objectives:** use the cosine rule to find a side or angle in a non-right-angled triangle. This teaches DfE G22.

The **cosine rule** finds a side from two sides and their included angle: $a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A$. It can also find an angle by rearranging the formula.

**Example 1.** Two sides are $5$ cm and $7$ cm, with included angle $60^\circ$. Find the opposite side $a$.

$$a^2=5^2+7^2-2(5)(7)\cos60^\circ$$

$$a^2=25+49-70\left(\frac12\right)$$

$$a=\sqrt{39}\approx6.24\text{ cm}$$

**Example 2.** Find angle $A$ when the opposite side is $8$ cm and the other sides are $5$ cm and $6$ cm.

$$8^2=5^2+6^2-2(5)(6)\cos A$$

$$\cos A=\frac{25+36-64}{60}=-0.05$$

$$A=\cos^{-1}(-0.05)\approx92.9^\circ$$

> **Watch out.** The angle in the formula must sit between the two known sides. Check the diagram before substituting values.

# 17.2 Plans and elevations — Practise with help

Attempt each item before opening its help. Recall ideas from earlier chapters first.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Name a shape with four right angles.
2. What does a scale of $1$ cm to $2$ m mean?
3. What angle is a right angle?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. A rectangle (a square is also correct).
2. Each centimetre on the drawing represents $2$ m in real life.
3. $90^\circ$.
</details>

## Try these with help

**1.** A cuboid is 6 cm wide, 4 cm deep and 3 cm high. Describe its plan.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A plan is viewed from above.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The plan records width and depth, not height.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full answer</summary>

A rectangle measuring 6 cm by 4 cm.
</details>
</details>

**2.** A solid has a $4$ cm by $2$ cm front. Which dimensions appear in its front elevation?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A front elevation shows width and height.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The front elevation is 4 cm by 2 cm.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full answer</summary>

The front elevation shows 4 cm width and 2 cm height.
</details>
</details>

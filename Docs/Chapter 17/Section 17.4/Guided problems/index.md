# 17.4 Loci — Practise with help

Attempt each item before opening its help. Recall ideas from earlier chapters first.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Name a shape with four right angles.
2. What does a scale of $1$ cm to $2$ m mean?
3. What angle is a right angle?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. A rectangle (a square is also correct).
2. Each centimetre on the drawing represents $2$ m in real life.
3. $90^\circ$.
</details>

## Try these with help

**1.** Describe the locus of points 5 cm from point $A$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use a fixed radius centred at $A$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

A fixed distance from a point traces a circle.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full answer</summary>

A circle centred at $A$ with radius 5 cm.
</details>
</details>

**2.** State the shortest distance from a point to a line.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Think about the angle made with the line.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The shortest segment meets the line at a right angle.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full answer</summary>

The perpendicular distance, measured at $90^\circ$.
</details>
</details>

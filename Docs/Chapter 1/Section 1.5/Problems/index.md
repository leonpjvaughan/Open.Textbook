# 1.5 Rounding and estimation — Practise

1. Round $7\,284$ to the nearest (a) $10$, (b) $100$, (c) $1000$.

2. Round $12.78$ to 1 decimal place.

3. Round $0.916$ to 2 decimal places.

4. Round $63\,500$ to 2 significant figures.

5. Round $0.020\,45$ to 3 significant figures.

6. Estimate $59 \times 31$ by rounding each number to 1 significant figure.

7. Estimate $497 \div 5$.

8. A jug holds $1.85$ litres. Round this to the nearest tenth of a litre and to the
   nearest litre.

9. A journey takes $3$ hours $47$ minutes. Round this to the nearest hour.

10. A cyclist travels $24.6$ km in $1.8$ hours. Estimate the average speed by
    rounding, then say whether an exact answer of $13.7$ km/h is reasonable.

11. The stall's profit was £39.20. A poster must show the profit to the nearest
    £5. What should it say?

12. **H** A number rounds to $4.7$ to 1 decimal place. Write down the smallest and
    largest values it could have.

13. **H** Estimate $\dfrac{6.2 \times 19.8}{3.1}$ by rounding each number to 1
    significant figure.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and concise solutions</summary>

1. (a) $7\,280$ (b) $7\,300$ (c) $7\,000$.
2. $12.8$.
3. $0.92$.
4. $64\,000$ (the $3$ and $5$ round up to $64$).
5. $0.020\,5$.
6. $60 \times 30 = 1800$.
7. $500 \div 5 = 100$.
8. $1.9$ litres; $2$ litres.
9. $4$ hours.
10. $25 \div 2 = 12.5$ km/h; $13.7$ km/h is reasonable.
11. $£40$.
12. $4.65$ to just below $4.75$ (i.e. $4.65 \leq x < 4.75$).
13. $\dfrac{6 \times 20}{3} = 40$.
</details>

# 1.5 Rounding and estimation — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $8 + 3 \times 4$.
2. Round $347$ to the nearest $10$.
3. Work out $1.25 \times 4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $20$.
2. $350$.
3. $5$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Round $6\,482$ to the nearest (a) $10$, (b) $100$, (c) $1000$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the digit in the place value you are rounding to, then look at the digit on
its right.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

(a) $6\,480$. (b) $6\,500$. (c) $6\,000$.
</details>

**2.** Round $5.678$ to (a) 1 decimal place, (b) 2 decimal places.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

For 1 d.p. look at the hundredths digit; for 2 d.p. look at the thousandths digit.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

(a) $5.7$. (b) $5.68$.
</details>

**3.** Round $0.003\,49$ to 2 significant figures.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Leading zeros are not significant. The first significant figure is the $3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The first two significant figures are $3$ and $4$; the next digit is $9 \geq 5$,
so round up: $0.003\,5$.
</details>

**4.** Estimate $38 \times 21$ by rounding each number to 1 significant figure.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

$38 \approx 40$ and $21 \approx 20$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$40 \times 20 = 800$. (The exact answer is $798$.)
</details>

**5.** Round $4\,999$ to 2 significant figures and explain why it is $5\,000$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Rounding up the first two digits may carry into the next column.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The first two significant figures are $4$ and $9$; the next digit is $9$, so round
up. $49$ becomes $50$, so the answer is $5\,000$.
</details>

**6.** A shirt costs £18.99 and a pair of trousers costs £32.50. Estimate the total
cost, then work out the exact total and compare.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Round to the nearest pound for the estimate.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Estimate: $£19 + £33 = £52$. Exact: $£51.49$. The estimate is close, so the exact
answer is reasonable.
</details>

**7.** In the chapter problem, the profit was £39.20. Round it to the nearest pound
and to 1 significant figure.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Nearest pound: look at the tenths. 1 s.f.: look at the second digit.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Nearest pound: $£39$. 1 significant figure: $£40$.
</details>

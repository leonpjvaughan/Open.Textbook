# 1.4 Priority of operations and inverse operations — Practise

1. Work out $6 + 4 \times 3$.

2. Work out $(6 + 4) \times 3$.

3. Work out $30 - 2 \times 7$.

4. Work out $4^2 - 5$.

5. Work out $15 \div 3 + 2 \times 4$.

6. Work out $20 - 12 \div 4$.

7. Work out $36 \div 6 \times 2$.

8. Work out $10 + \sqrt{25}$.

9. Work out $2 \times (9 - 4)^2$.

10. I think of a number, add 6, then multiply by 3. The result is 36. Find the
    number.

11. I think of a number, square it, then subtract 5. The result is 20. Find the
    possible values.

12. Write an expression for the profit at the stall and evaluate it.

13. **H** Insert brackets to make the statement true: $2 + 3 \times 4 - 1 = 19$.

14. **H** Work out $3 + 4 \times (7 - 2^2)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and concise solutions</summary>

1. $18$.
2. $30$.
3. $16$.
4. $11$.
5. $5 + 8 = 13$.
6. $17$.
7. $6 \times 2 = 12$.
8. $15$.
9. $2 \times 5^2 = 2 \times 25 = 50$.
10. $36 \div 3 = 12$, then $12 - 6 = 6$.
11. $x^2 - 5 = 20$, so $x^2 = 25$ and $x = 5$ or $x = -5$.
12. $95 \times 1.20 + 6 \times 3.00 - (120 \times 0.65 + 8 \times 1.85) = £39.20$.
13. $(2 + 3) \times 4 - 1 = 19$.
14. $2^2 = 4$, $7 - 4 = 3$, $4 \times 3 = 12$, $3 + 12 = 15$.
</details>

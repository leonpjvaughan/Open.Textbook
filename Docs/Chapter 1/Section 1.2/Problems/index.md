# 1.2 The four operations — Problems

1. Work out $374 + 268$.

2. Work out $604 - 187$.

3. Work out $36.5 + 7.85$.

4. Work out $90 - 23.6$.

5. Work out $37 \times 24$.

6. Work out $6.4 \times 7$.

7. Work out $144 \div 12$.

8. Work out $7.2 \div 3$.

9. Work out $2.35 \times 100$.

10. A pack of 8 sandwiches costs £10.40. Work out the cost of one sandwich.

11. Three friends share a restaurant bill of £86.40 equally. How much does each
    pay?

12. In the anchor problem, work out the exact profit: total taken $£132$ minus
    the cost of the stock.

13. **H** Work out $4.8 \times 2.5$.

14. **H** Work out $15.6 \div 0.4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and concise solutions</summary>

1. $642$.
2. $417$.
3. $44.35$ (align: $36.50 + 7.85$).
4. $66.4$ ($90.0 - 23.6$).
5. $888$ ($37 \times 20 = 740$, $37 \times 4 = 148$).
6. $44.8$.
7. $12$.
8. $2.4$.
9. $235$.
10. $10.40 \div 8 = £1.30$.
11. $86.40 \div 3 = £28.80$.
12. Stock cost $120 \times 0.65 + 8 \times 1.85 = 78 + 14.80 = £92.80$, so
    profit $= 132 - 92.80 = £39.20$.
13. $4.8 \times 2.5 = 12$ (work out $48 \times 25 = 1200$, two decimal places).
14. $15.6 \div 0.4 = 156 \div 4 = 39$.
</details>

# 1.3 Negative numbers — Practise

1. Write these in order, smallest first: $4,\ -2,\ 0,\ -7,\ 1$.

2. Work out $-8 + 3$.

3. Work out $5 - 12$.

4. Work out $-6 - 4$.

5. Work out $2 - (-9)$.

6. Work out $(-7) \times 3$.

7. Work out $(-6) \times (-6)$.

8. Work out $(-36) \div 4$.

9. The temperature falls from $2\,^\circ\text{C}$ to $-5\,^\circ\text{C}$. By how
   many degrees did it fall?

10. A submarine is at $-40$ m and rises $15$ m. What is its new depth?

11. A shop's takings are £210 and its costs are £235. Work out the profit as a
    negative number and describe the result.

12. In a quiz, teams score $+3$ for a correct answer and $-1$ for a wrong answer.
    A team gets 7 correct and 4 wrong. What is its total score?

13. **H** Work out $-4 + 6 - 3 + 2 - 5$.

14. **H** The product of two negative numbers is $+36$. Give two possible pairs.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and concise solutions</summary>

1. $-7 < -2 < 0 < 1 < 4$.
2. $-5$.
3. $-7$.
4. $-10$.
5. $11$.
6. $-21$.
7. $36$.
8. $-9$.
9. $7$ degrees.
10. $-25$ m.
11. $210 - 235 = -25$, a loss of £25.
12. $7 \times 3 + 4 \times (-1) = 21 - 4 = 17$.
13. $-4 + 6 = 2$, $2 - 3 = -1$, $-1 + 2 = 1$, $1 - 5 = -4$.
14. $(-6) \times (-6)$ and $(-4) \times (-9)$, for example.
</details>

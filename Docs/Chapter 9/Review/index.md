# Chapter 9: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

Let $h$ be the number of hours. The first hall costs $18+6h$ pounds; the second costs $10+8h$ pounds.

Set the costs equal: $18+6h=10+8h$.

Subtract $10$ and $6h$: $8=2h$.

Divide by $2$: $h=4$.

At four hours, both halls cost £42. The equal point is after two hours, as you predicted or revised in the opener.

## Mixed practice: choose a method and explain why

For each item, name the method before you work. After calculating, say why that method fits.

1. Solve $5x-7=18$.
2. Solve $4x+3=2x+15$.
3. A rectangle has length $x+5$ and width $x$. Its perimeter is 46 cm. Find its sides.
4. Write and solve an inequality for values no greater than 6.
5. Solve $3-2x<11$.
<!-- tier:h -->
6. Solve $(x+1)(x-4)\geq0$.
7. Solve $x^2-2x-8<0$.
<!-- /tier:h -->
8. Chapter 1 link: estimate $19.8\times4.9$ and explain how the estimate checks an exact answer.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Inverse operations: $x=5$.
2. Collect unknown terms: $x=6$.
3. $2(x+5)+2x=46$, so $x=9$; width 9 cm and length 14 cm.
4. $x\leq6$.
5. Subtract 3: $-2x<8$; divide by $-2$ and reverse: $x>-4$.
<!-- tier:h -->
6. Roots $-1,4$; test intervals: $x\leq-1$ or $x\geq4$.
7. Factor: $(x-4)(x+2)<0$, so $-2<x<4$.
<!-- /tier:h -->
8. Estimate $20\times5=100$. The exact product is $97.02$, which is close to the estimate.
</details>

# Chapter 14: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

Let $f_A$ be Company A's fixed fee and $r_A$ its price per kilometre. The two fares give

$$
f_A+4r_A=14
$$

$$
f_A+8r_A=22
$$

Subtract the first equation from the second: $4r_A=8$, so $r_A=2$. Then $f_A+4(2)=14$, so $f_A=6$.

For Company B, let $f_B$ be its fixed fee and $r_B$ its price per kilometre.

$$
f_B+4r_B=16
$$

$$
f_B+8r_B=24
$$

Subtracting gives $4r_B=8$, so $r_B=2$. Then $f_B+4(2)=16$, so $f_B=8$.

Both companies charge £2 per kilometre. Company A has a £6 fixed fee; Company B has an £8 fixed fee. This confirms the opening prediction can be checked by comparing the rates.

## Mixed practice: choose the method and explain why

For each question, name a method before working. Give a reason for your choice.

1. Solve $x+y=12$ and $x-y=4$.
2. Solve $y=3x-2$ and $x+y=10$.
3. Find the intersection of $y=x+1$ and $y=-2x+7$.
4. Solve $y=x+2$ and $y=x^2-2x-3$.
5. Use $x_{n+1}=\frac12(x_n+6/x_n)$ with $x_0=2$ to find $x_3$.
6. Work out $3(2.5-0.7)$, then explain how inverse operations could check it.
7. A temperature rises from $-4^\circ$C by $9^\circ$C. State the new temperature.
8. Solve $2x+3=15$. Then use your value as the $x$ coordinate in $y=2x-1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Add: $2x=16$, so $x=8$, $y=4$.
2. Substitute: $3x-2+x=10$, so $x=3$, $y=7$.
3. Set equal: $x+1=-2x+7$, so $(x,y)=(2,3)$.
4. Set equal: $x+2=x^2-2x-3$, so $x^2-3x-5=0$. Thus $x=(3\pm\sqrt{29})/2$ and $y=x+2$ for each root.
5. $x_1=2.5$, $x_2=2.45$, $x_3\approx2.449$.
6. $3(1.8)=5.4$; check $5.4\div3=1.8$.
7. $-4+9=5^\circ$C.
8. $x=6$, then $y=11$.

# 25.1 Quartiles, interquartile range and box plots — Practise with help

Try each question before opening its hint. Reveal the working when you want to check.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Order $4, 9, 2, 7, 5$.
2. Find the median of $3, 6, 8, 10, 12$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Feedback</summary>

1. $2,4,5,7,9$. 2. $8$, the middle value.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find the median, lower quartile and upper quartile of
$2,4,5,7,8,10,12,14$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Split the ordered list into two equal halves. Find each half's median.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Step</summary>

The median is the mean of positions 4 and 5. The lower and upper halves each have four values.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Median $=(7+8)/2=7.5$. Lower quartile $=(4+5)/2=4.5$. Upper quartile $=(10+12)/2=11$.
</details>

**2.** Calculate the IQR for the data in question 1.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract the lower quartile from the upper quartile.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Step</summary>

Use $Q_3-Q_1=11-4.5$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The IQR is $11-4.5=6.5$.
</details>

**3.** Earlier work: find the range of $-3, 2, 6, 9$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Range means maximum minus minimum.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Step</summary>

Calculate $9-(-3)$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The range is $9-(-3)=12$.
</details>

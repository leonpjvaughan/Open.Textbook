# 21.3 Tangents and chords — Practise with help

Try each question before opening a hint. Open the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

1. How many degrees are in a right angle?
2. A triangle has angles $90^\circ$ and $36^\circ$. Find its third angle.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. $90^\circ$.
2. $180-90-36=54^\circ$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A tangent meets a radius at the point of contact. State the angle between them.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The tangent and radius are perpendicular.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write the size of a right angle.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The angle is $90^\circ$.
</details>

**2.** Tangents from $P$ touch the circle at $A$ and $B$. If $PA=9$ cm, find $PB$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Tangents from one external point have equal lengths.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Set $PB=PA$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$PB=PA=9$ cm.
</details>

**3.** A perpendicular from the centre meets a chord of length $18$ cm. Find the length of each half.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Divide $18$ by $2$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$18\div2=9$ cm for each half.
</details>

**4.** The tangent-chord angle is $39^\circ$. Find the angle in the alternate segment.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Name the theorem that connects these two angles.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The theorem says the angles are equal.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The alternate segment angle is $39^\circ$.
</details>

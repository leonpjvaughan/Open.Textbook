Attempt each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Practise with help

**1.** Reflect $(4,-2)$ in the $y$-axis.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Change the sign of the coordinate perpendicular to the mirror line.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>The $y$-axis is vertical, so keep $y=-2$ and change $x=4$ to $-4$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>Image $(-4,-2)$. The point and image have equal distances from the $y$-axis.</details>

**2.** Rotate $(3,1)$ by $90^\circ$ anticlockwise about the origin.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Use $(x,y)\mapsto(-y,x)$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Substitute $x=3$ and $y=1$ to get $(-1,3)$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>The image is $(-1,3)$ after a quarter-turn anticlockwise.</details>

**3.** Earlier topic: solve $3x=18$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Use the inverse operation.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Divide both sides by $3$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$x=6$.</details>

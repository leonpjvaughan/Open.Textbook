# Chapter 12: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

The ball reaches the ground when its height is zero:

$$
-t^2+4t+5=0
$$

Factor out $-1$, then factorise the quadratic:

$$
-(t^2-4t-5)=0
$$

$$
-(t-5)(t+1)=0
$$
So $t=5$ or $t=-1$. Time cannot be negative in this context, so the ball reaches the ground after **5 seconds**. Substitution gives $h=0$ when $t=5$.

5. Write $x^2+10x+1$ in completed-square form. State its turning point as a graph of $y$.

<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $x^2+2x-8$.
2. $(x+5)(x-2)$.
3. $(x-3)(x+3)=0$, so $x=3$ or $x=-3$.
4. $a=3,b=1,c=-2$: $x=(-1\pm\sqrt25)/6$, so $x=2/3$ or $x=-1$.
5. $(x+5)^2-24$; turning point $(-5,-24)$.
6. For example, $(x+1)(x-4)=0$; any non-zero multiple has those roots.
7. $x=4$; substitution gives $3(4)+8=20$.
8. $2^7=128$.

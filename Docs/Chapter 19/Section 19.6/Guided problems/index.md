# 19.6 Composite shapes and solids — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Find the volume of a cuboid $3\times4\times5$ cm. 2. Find the area of a $9$ cm by $6$ cm rectangle.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Check your answers</summary>

1. $60\text{ cm}^3$. 2. $54\text{ cm}^2$.
</details>

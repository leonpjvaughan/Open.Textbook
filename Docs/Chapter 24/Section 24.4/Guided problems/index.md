# 24.4 Averages from frequency tables — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

**Closed book:** Work out $3+4$ and write $0.25$ as a fraction.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Check your answers</summary>

$3+4=7$. Also, $0.25=25/100=1/4$. If either answer differs, revisit number operations and fractions before continuing.
</details>

### Question 1

Work out $6	imes4$ and $24/6$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Recall multiplication and division.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The answers are 24 and 4.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The answers are 24 and 4.
</details>

### Question 2

Values 1 and 3 have frequencies 2 and 4. Find the total frequency.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Add the frequencies.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Total frequency $=2+4=6$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Total frequency $=2+4=6$.
</details>

### Question 3

Use those values and frequencies to find the mean.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply each value by its frequency.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Mean $=(1	imes2+3	imes4)/6=14/6=7/3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Mean $=(1	imes2+3	imes4)/6=14/6=7/3$.
</details>

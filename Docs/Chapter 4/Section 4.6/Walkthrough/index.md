# 4.6 Fractions and percentages as operators — Learn

**Prerequisites:** equivalent fractions and multiplication.

**Objectives:** An operator acts on an amount. For example, $\frac{3}{5}$ of £20 means multiply £20 by $\frac{3}{5}$. A percentage is a fraction out of $100$.

## Learn the method

To find a fraction or percentage of an amount, multiply. For a percentage increase, multiply by $1+p/100$. For a decrease, multiply by $1-p/100$.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** Work out $\frac{3}{8}$ of £56.

Divide £56 by $8$ to find one eighth.

$56\div8=7$.

Multiply by $3$.

$7\times3=£21$.

**Example 2.** Increase £80 by $15\%$.

The multiplier is $1+15/100=1.15$.

$80\times1.15=£92$.

**Example 3.** A £72 price is after a $20\%$ reduction. Find the original price.

The new price is $80\%$ of the original.

$72\div0.8=£90$.

> **Watch out.** A $20\%$ reduction does not mean subtracting £20. Find $20\%$ of the price, or multiply by $0.8$. Check that the sale price is below the original.

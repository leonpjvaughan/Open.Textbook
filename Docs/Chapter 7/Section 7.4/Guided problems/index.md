# 7.4 Upper and lower bounds — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Convert $2.5$ m to cm.
2. Work out $3.6\times10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $250$ cm.
2. $36$.
</details>

## Practise with help

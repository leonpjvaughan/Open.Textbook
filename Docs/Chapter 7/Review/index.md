# Chapter 7: Review

## The chapter problem, resolved

Suppose the map uses scale $1:25\,000$, and the path measures $3.2$ cm. Its real length is

$$
3.2	imes25\,000=80\,000	ext{ cm}=800	ext{ m}=0.8	ext{ km}.
$$

If a walker takes $10$ minutes, that is $1/6$ hour. The average speed is

$$
0.8\div(1/6)=4.8	ext{ km/h}.
$$

A sample recorded as $0.0042$ kg to 2 significant figures lies from $0.00415$ kg up to, but not including, $0.00425$ kg. In standard form, the recorded value is $4.2	imes10^{-3}$ kg. These steps show how conversion, compound measures, rounding and standard form connect.

## Mixed practice: choose the method first

For each question, name the method before working.

1. Convert $2.7$ m to mm.
2. A cyclist travels $36$ km in $1.5$ hours. Find the average speed.
3. Write the interval for $5.6$ kg rounded to the nearest $0.1$ kg.
4. A length is $8.2$ cm and a width is $3.4$ cm, each to the nearest $0.1$ cm. Find the upper bound for the area.
5. Work out $(4	imes10^6)(3	imes10^{-2})$ in standard form.
6. Simplify $\sqrt{108}-\sqrt{12}$.
7. A map distance is $6$ cm at scale $1:50\,000$. Find the real distance in kilometres.
8. A force of $450$ N acts on $0.15	ext{ m}^2$. Find the pressure.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $2700$ mm.
2. $36\div1.5=24$ km/h.
3. $5.55\leq m<5.65$ kg.
4. Upper bounds: $8.25$ cm and $3.45$ cm. Area upper bound $=28.4625	ext{ cm}^2$.
5. $12	imes10^4=1.2	imes10^5$.
6. $6\sqrt3-2\sqrt3=4\sqrt3$.
7. $6	imes50\,000=300\,000$ cm $=3$ km.
8. $450\div0.15=3000$ Pa.
</details>

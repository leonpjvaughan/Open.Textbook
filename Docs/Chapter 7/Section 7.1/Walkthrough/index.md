# 7.1 Units of measure and conversions — Learn

## Before you begin

You should know decimal place value and how to multiply and divide by 10.

## Objectives

- Convert between metric units for length, mass, capacity and area.
- Use scale drawings and map scales to find real distances and lengths.
- Read and use bearings measured clockwise from north.

## Metric conversions

The prefixes kilo-, centi- and milli- mean $1000$, one hundredth and one thousandth. A conversion changes the number and its unit together.

$$
1\text{ km}=1000\text{ m},\quad 1\text{ m}=100\text{ cm},\quad 1\text{ kg}=1000\text{ g},\quad 1\text{ litre}=1000\text{ ml}
$$

For area, square the length conversion: $1\text{ m}^2=10\,000\text{ cm}^2$.

**Example 1.** Convert $3.6$ km to metres.

$$
3.6\times1000=3600
$$

So $3.6$ km is $3600$ m. Convert back by dividing by $1000$.

**Example 2.** Convert $2.4\text{ m}^2$ to $\text{cm}^2$.

$$
1\text{ m}^2=100\times100\text{ cm}^2
$$

$$
2.4\times10\,000=24\,000
$$

So the area is $24\,000\text{ cm}^2$.

> **Watch out.** A square unit has two length dimensions. Multiplying by $100$ gives the wrong area conversion; use $100^2$.

## Scale drawings

A scale gives the ratio between a drawing length and a real length in matching units. A scale of $1:50\,000$ means $1$ cm represents $50\,000$ cm, or $500$ m.

**Example 3.** A map uses scale $1:50\,000$. Two places are $4.2$ cm apart. Find their real distance in kilometres.

$$
4.2\times50\,000=210\,000\text{ cm}
$$

$$
210\,000\div100\,000=2.1\text{ km}
$$

## Bearings

A bearing is an angle measured clockwise from north. Write it with three digits, such as $065^\circ$. The north line is the starting direction.

**Example 4.** A bearing of $125^\circ$ turns $125^\circ$ clockwise from north. Its reverse bearing is $125^\circ+180^\circ=305^\circ$.

## You should now be able to

- convert metric lengths, masses, capacities and areas;
- use a scale ratio to find a real distance or drawing length;
- measure and write a three-figure bearing from north.

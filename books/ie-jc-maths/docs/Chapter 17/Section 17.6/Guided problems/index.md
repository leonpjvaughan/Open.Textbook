# 17.6 The general form ax + by + c = 0 — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Work these from memory. They are the skills this section uses.

1. Make $y$ the subject of $2y=4x+6$.
2. Work out $3\times2+4-10$.
3. Solve $5x-10=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $y=2x+3$. Divide every term by 2.
2. $0$. Multiply first: $3\times2=6$, then $6+4-10=0$.
3. $x=2$. Add 10, then divide by 5.

Rearranging a formula is the same work as changing a line from one form to
another.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Write the line $y=2x+3$ in the form $ax+by+c=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Move every term to the left so the right-hand side is 0.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$0=2x-y+3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The general form is $2x-y+3=0$.

Subtract $y$ from both sides: $0=2x-y+3$. Check with $(0,3)$: $0-3+3=0$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Write the line $y=-3x+1$ in the form $ax+by+c=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add $3x$ to both sides and subtract 1.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$3x+y-1=0$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The general form is $3x+y-1=0$.

Check with $(0,1)$: $0+1-1=0$. Check with $(1,-2)$: $3-2-1=0$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Write the line $2x+3y-6=0$ in the form $y=mx+c$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Move the $x$ term and the constant to the right, then divide by 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$3y=6-2x$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The line is $y=-\tfrac{2}{3}x+2$.

$$y=2-\frac{2}{3}x$$

The slope is $-\tfrac{2}{3}$ and the intercept is 2. Check with $x=3$:
$-\tfrac{2}{3}\times3+2=0$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Find the slope of the line $3x-y+4=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the rule slope $=-\frac{a}{b}$, or rearrange for $y$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$-\frac{3}{-1}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The slope is $3$.

$$-\frac{a}{b}=-\frac{3}{-1}=3$$

Check by rearranging: $y=3x+4$, so the slope is 3.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Find the $y$-intercept of the line $2x+5y-10=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Set $x=0$, because the $y$-axis is the line $x=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$5y-10=0$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The $y$-intercept is $2$.

$$5y=10 \qquad y=2$$

The line crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,2)$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Find the $x$-intercept of the line $4x+y-8=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Set $y=0$, because the $x$-axis is the line $y=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$4x-8=0$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The $x$-intercept is $2$.

$$4x=8 \qquad x=2$$

The line crosses the $x$-axis at $(2,0)$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Does the point $(1,4)$ lie on the line $x+2y-9=0$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Substitute $x=1$ and $y=4$, and see whether the result is 0.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$1+2\times4-9$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Yes, the point lies on the line.

$$1+8-9=0$$

A result of 0 means the point fits the equation of the line.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** The park main path is $y=\tfrac{3}{4}x+\tfrac{5}{4}$. Write it in the form
$ax+by+c=0$ with whole-number coefficients.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply every term by 4 to clear the fractions.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$4y=3x+5$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The general form is $3x-4y+5=0$.

$$4y=3x+5 \qquad 3x-4y+5=0$$

Check the gate $G(1,2)$: $3-8+5=0$. Check the kiosk $K(9,8)$: $27-32+5=0$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Make $y$ the subject of $3y=6x-9$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide every term by 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$y=\frac{6x-9}{3}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$y=2x-3$$

Dividing every term by 3 is the same step used to turn a general form into
$y=mx+c$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

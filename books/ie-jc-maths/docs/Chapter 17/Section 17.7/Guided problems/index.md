# 17.7 Graphs in context and rate of change — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Work these from memory. They are the skills this section uses.

1. A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. Find its average speed.
2. Work out $45\div9$.
3. On a distance-time graph, what does a flat line mean?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $60$ km/h. Divide the distance by the time.
2. $5$. This is the same division a rate uses.
3. It means the object is not moving, because the distance is not changing.

A rate is a division of two changes, and a flat segment has a rate of 0.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A walker covers 180 m in 3 minutes at a steady pace. Find the rate of change.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the change in distance by the change in time.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{180}{3}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rate is $60$ m per minute.

$$\frac{180}{3}=60$$

The walker moves 60 m every minute.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A straight-line graph passes through $(0,0)$ and $(4,12)$. Find its rate of
change.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The rate is the slope: change in $y$ divided by change in $x$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{12-0}{4-0}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rate is $3$.

$$\frac{12}{4}=3$$

The vertical amount rises by 3 for each unit across.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A straight-line graph passes through $(0,5)$ and $(4,17)$. Find its rate of
change and its starting value.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The rate is the slope. The starting value is the $y$-intercept.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{17-5}{4-0} \qquad c=5$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rate is $3$ and the starting value is $5$.

$$\frac{12}{4}=3$$

The graph meets the $y$-axis at $(0,5)$, so the starting value is 5. The equation
is $y=3x+5$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A distance-time graph is flat from $t=2$ to $t=5$ minutes, at a distance of
20 m. Find the rate of change during those 3 minutes.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The distance does not change.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{20-20}{5-2}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rate is $0$ m per minute.

$$\frac{0}{3}=0$$

A flat segment means the person is resting, so the speed is 0.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Two lines are drawn on one distance-time graph. Line A goes through $(0,0)$
and $(3,15)$. Line B goes through $(0,0)$ and $(5,15)$. Which line shows the
faster speed?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find both rates and compare them.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{15}{3} \qquad \frac{15}{5}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Line A is faster. Its rate is $15\div3=5$, and the rate of line B is
$15\div5=3$. Both reach 15 m, but line A does it in 3 s rather than 5 s. Line A is
steeper.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** A straight-line graph passes through $(0,30)$ and $(6,6)$. Find its rate of
change.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The amount falls, so the rate is negative.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{6-30}{6-0}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rate is $-4$.

$$\frac{-24}{6}=-4$$

The amount falls by 4 for each step to the right, so the graph goes down.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** A park keeper walks 10 m in the first 2 minutes, then 24 m in the next 4
minutes. Find the rate of change in each part.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide each distance by its own time.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{10}{2} \qquad \frac{24}{4}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rates are $5$ m per minute and then $6$ m per minute.

$$\frac{10}{2}=5 \qquad \frac{24}{4}=6$$

The second part of the graph is steeper, so the keeper walks faster in the second
part.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**8.** A train travels 150 km in 3 hours. Find its average speed.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Speed is distance divided by time.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{150}{3}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The average speed is $50$ km/h.

$$\frac{150}{3}=50$$

Speed is a rate of change, and the slope of a distance-time graph gives the same
number.

</details>
</details>
</details>

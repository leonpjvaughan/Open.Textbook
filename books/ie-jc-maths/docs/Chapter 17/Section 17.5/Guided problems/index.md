# 17.5 The equation of a line — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Work these from memory. They are the skills this section uses.

1. Solve $2x+1=9$.
2. Work out $5-2\times3$.
3. Solve $x+4=7$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $x=4$. Subtract 1 to get $2x=8$, then divide by 2.
2. $-1$. Multiply first: $2\times3=6$, then $5-6=-1$.
3. $x=3$. Subtract 4 from both sides.

Finding the intercept uses the same steps as solving an equation.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A line has slope 2 and passes through $(0,4)$. Write its equation in the
form $y=mx+c$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A point on the $y$-axis gives the intercept straight away.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$m=2 \qquad c=4$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The equation is $y=2x+4$. The point $(0,4)$ is on the $y$-axis, so the intercept is
4. Check it: at $x=0$, $y=4$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A line has slope 3 and passes through $(1,2)$. Write its equation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use $c=y-mx$ with the point and the slope.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$c=2-3\times1$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The equation is $y=3x-1$.

$$c=2-3=-1$$

Check it with the point: $3\times1-1=2$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A line has slope $-1$ and passes through $(2,5)$. Write its equation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Substitute carefully, because the slope is negative.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$c=5-(-1)\times2$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The equation is $y=-x+7$.

$$c=5-(-1)\times2=5+2=7$$

Subtracting a negative slope adds. Check it: $-2+7=5$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A line passes through $(0,2)$ and $(4,10)$. Find its equation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the slope first, then use a point to find the intercept.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$m=\frac{10-2}{4-0}=2 \qquad c=2-2\times0$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The equation is $y=2x+2$.

$$m=\frac{8}{4}=2 \qquad c=2$$

The point $(0,2)$ is on the $y$-axis, so the intercept is 2. Check with the other
point: $2\times4+2=10$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** A line passes through $(1,1)$ and $(4,7)$. Find its equation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The slope is $(7-1)\div(4-1)$. Use $(1,1)$ for the intercept.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$m=\frac{7-1}{4-1}=2 \qquad c=1-2\times1$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The equation is $y=2x-1$.

$$m=\frac{6}{3}=2 \qquad c=1-2=-1$$

Check with the other point: $2\times4-1=7$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Find the point where the line $y=3x-1$ meets the line $y=x+3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

At the crossing point the two $y$-values are equal. Set the right-hand sides
equal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$3x-1=x+3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The lines meet at $(2,5)$.

$$3x-1=x+3 \qquad 2x=4 \qquad x=2$$

$$y=3\times2-1=5$$

Check with the other line: $2+3=5$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** The park main path passes through the gate $G(1,2)$ with slope
$\tfrac{3}{4}$. Find its equation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use $c=y-mx$ with the gate and the slope.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$c=2-\frac{3}{4}\times1$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The equation is $y=\tfrac{3}{4}x+\tfrac{5}{4}$.

$$c=2-\frac{3}{4}=\frac{8}{4}-\frac{3}{4}=\frac{5}{4}$$

Check with the kiosk $K(9,8)$:
$\tfrac{3}{4}\times9+\tfrac{5}{4}=\tfrac{27}{4}+\tfrac{5}{4}=8$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**8.** Solve $3x-2=13$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add 2 to both sides first, then divide.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$3x=15$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$x=5$$

Add 2 to both sides to get $3x=15$, then divide by 3. Finding an intercept uses the
same two steps.

</details>
</details>
</details>

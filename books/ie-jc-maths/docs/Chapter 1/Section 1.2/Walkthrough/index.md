# 1.2 Adding and subtracting integers — Learn

**Prerequisites:** the number systems from Section 1.1, and the number line.

**Objectives:** add and subtract integers; handle two signs written side by side;
find the difference between two integers; work through a chain of additions and
subtractions.

By the end of this section, you can move along the number line with confidence, even
when the answer passes through zero.

## Moving along the number line

Draw a number line with 0 in the middle. The positive numbers are to the right and
the negative numbers are to the left. Adding and subtracting is a movement along
that line.

- Adding a positive number moves you to the **right**.
- Adding a negative number moves you to the **left**.
- Subtracting a positive number moves you to the **left**.
- Subtracting a negative number moves you to the **right**.

**Example 1.** Work out $-7 + 3$.

Start at $-7$ and move 3 places to the right. You pass $-6$, then $-5$, then $-4$.

$$-7 + 3 = -4$$

The answer is still negative, because 7 is larger than 3.

**Example 2.** Work out $5 - 9$.

Start at 5 and move 9 places to the left. You pass zero on the way.

$$5 - 9 = -4$$

The answer is negative, because 9 is larger than 5.

## Two signs side by side

When two signs meet, replace them with one sign. There are only two cases to learn.

$$+(-) \text{ becomes } - \qquad -(-) \text{ becomes } +$$

The second one catches people out. Subtracting a negative number is the same as
adding, because you move to the right twice: once to remove the subtraction and once
for the negative.

**Example 3.** Work out $-3 + (-8)$.

The signs are $+(-)$, which becomes $-$.

$$-3 + (-8) = -3 - 8 = -11$$

**Example 4.** Work out $6 - (-9)$.

The signs are $-(-)$, which becomes $+$.

$$6 - (-9) = 6 + 9 = 15$$

**Example 5.** Work out $-2 - (-7)$.

$$-2 - (-7) = -2 + 7 = 5$$

## Investigate moving along the number line

**Predict.** Before you move the controls, predict which way the hops will go when you
subtract a negative number. Write your prediction down before you touch anything.

:::figure integer-line

**Test.** Start at $-3$ and set the second number to $-8$. Follow the hops one place at
a time. Then change the operation to subtract and watch the hops again. Try starting at
$4$ and subtracting $6$, so that the answer passes through zero.

**Explain.** Write two or three sentences. Say why subtracting a negative number moves
you to the right, and what the hops show that the digits alone do not.

## The difference between two numbers

The **difference** between two numbers is the distance between them on the number
line. You find it by subtracting the smaller from the larger, or by counting the gap
through zero.

**Example 6.** Work out the difference between $-6$ and $4$.

From $-6$ to 0 is 6, and from 0 to 4 is 4. The gap is $6 + 4 = 10$. You can also write
it as a subtraction:

$$4 - (-6) = 4 + 6 = 10$$

## A chain of additions and subtractions

When a calculation has several additions and subtractions, work from left to right.
They are equally strong, so the order you read them is the order you do them.

**Example 7.** Work out $12 - 15 + 7$.

$$12 - 15 = -3 \qquad -3 + 7 = 4$$

> **Watch out.** Two numbers with the same sign push in the same direction, so the
> amounts add. The common error is to treat $-4 - 6$ as $-4 + 6 = 2$. It is not:
> both numbers are negative, so you move left twice.
>
> $$-4 - 6 = -10$$
>
> **Re-check:** say the movement out loud. "Start at $-4$, move 6 to the left." That
> lands you at $-10$, not 2.

## Progress check

Return to the chapter problem. The class fund holds €620. The bus deposit of €150 and
the centre deposit of €70 are paid from it.

1. Work out the fund balance after both deposits.
2. The whole bus is then paid early for €480. Work out the new balance.
3. The balance is negative. Write one sentence saying what that means for the class
   fund.

Keep the balance you found. In Section 1.3 you will see the contributions coming in
and the balance climbing back above zero.

## You should now be able to

- add and subtract integers by moving along the number line;
- replace two signs written side by side with a single sign;
- find the difference between two integers;
- work through a chain of additions and subtractions from left to right;
- explain what a negative balance means.

# Chapter 1: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

**The class trip budget.** The bus costs €480, the centre costs €14 for each person,
and lunch costs €6.50 for each person. 35 people are going. The class fund holds €620,
and the bus deposit of €150 and the centre deposit of €70 have already been paid.

Work through it in the order the chapter taught.

**Step 1. The total cost.** Write the trip as one calculation and use the order of
operations.

$$480 + 35 \times 14 + 35 \times 6.50$$

The two multiplications go first.

$$35 \times 14 = 490 \qquad 35 \times 6.50 = 227.50$$

$$480 + 490 + 227.50 = 1197.50$$

**The trip costs €1197.50.**

**Step 2. The class fund.** The two deposits have left the fund.

$$620 - 150 - 70 = 400$$

The fund has €400 left. At the moment the whole bus is paid, the balance drops to
$400 - 480 = -80$: the fund is €80 overdrawn until the contributions arrive.

**Step 3. The money still to find.**

$$1197.50 - 620 = 577.50$$

**Step 4. The share for each person.**

$$577.50 \div 35 = 16.50$$

**Each of the 35 people pays €16.50.**

**Step 5. Check that the money works out.** Multiply the share back up.

$$35 \times 16.50 = 577.50$$

That is the money still to find, so the budget balances. The trip costs €1197.50, the
fund pays €620, and the 35 contributions cover the rest exactly.

One more check is free. The distributive property says the two per-person costs could
have been added first: $35 \times (14 + 6.50) = 35 \times 20.50 = 717.50$, and
$480 + 717.50 = 1197.50$. Both routes agree.

## Mixed practice — choose the method first

Work through these without notes. For each one, decide **which idea applies** and say
why in a few words. The idea might be the number system, a sign rule, the order of
operations, a property, or a surd rule.

1. Put $-3$, $0$, $4.5$ and $7$ in order from smallest to largest.
2. Write $0.35$ as a fraction in its simplest form.
3. Work out $-8 + 3 - (-5)$.
4. Work out $(-7) \times 4$ and $(-7) \times (-4)$. Say which sign rule you used.
5. Work out $16 \div (2 + 6) \times 3$.
6. Work out $5 + 3 \times (8 - 2)^2$.
7. Work out $25 \times 39 + 25 \times 61$ by taking out the common factor.
8. Work out $12 \times 99$ using the distributive property.
9. The temperature at 6 a.m. is $-6$ degrees. It rises 4 degrees by noon, then falls
   9 degrees by midnight. Work out the midnight temperature.
10. A shop takes €1240 on Saturday and €1085 on Sunday. Its costs are €950 and €880.
    Work out the profit for the weekend as one calculation.

## What you can now do

- say whether a number is natural, an integer or rational, and write a decimal as a
  fraction;
- add and subtract integers, including two signs written side by side, and read a
  balance that has gone below zero;
- multiply and divide integers and state the sign of the answer first;
- work out a calculation with brackets, indices and a fraction bar in the correct
  order;
- use the commutative, associative and distributive properties to choose a quicker
  route;
<!-- tier:h -->
- simplify a surd, add like surds, and estimate a square root that is not a whole
  number.

## Thinking back

At the start of the chapter you predicted whether the trip would cost more or less
than €1000, and you wrote down which calculation you would need first. Look back at
that prediction now.

The trip costs €1197.50, so it is above €1000. The calculation needed both
multiplication and addition, and the order of operations decided which went first.

You also wrote about a place in your own week where a negative number appears, or
where a total has to be right. Take that example and work it out now, with the four
operations. If the answer surprises you, that is worth writing down: it usually means
a sign, or the order of the operations, was not what you expected.

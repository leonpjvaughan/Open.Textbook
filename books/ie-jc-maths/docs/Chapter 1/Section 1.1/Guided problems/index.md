# 1.1 The number systems ℕ, ℤ and ℚ — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $8 \times 7$.
2. How many tenths are there in $0.6$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $56$.
2. There are 6 tenths, because $0.6 = \frac{6}{10}$. The first place after the
   decimal point counts tenths.

Reading a decimal in tenths and hundredths is what lets you turn it into a fraction,
which is the main job of this section.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Write down the natural numbers between 5 and 11.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The natural numbers are the counting numbers. Which counting numbers sit strictly
between 5 and 11?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Start at 6 and stop at 10.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$6, 7, 8, 9, 10$$

The word *between* leaves out both ends, so 5 and 11 are not in the list.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Which of $-8$, $0$, $3$ and $7$ are integers?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

An integer is a whole number, and it may carry a minus sign.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

All four are whole numbers. Does the minus sign on $-8$ take it out of the set?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

All four are integers. The integers contain the negatives, zero and the positives.

$-8$ is an integer, but it is not a natural number, because the natural numbers start
at 1.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Write $0.4$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The 4 is in the tenths place, so start with $\frac{4}{10}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Divide the top and the bottom by 2.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$0.4 = \frac{4}{10} = \frac{2}{5}$$

The fraction $2/5$ is in its simplest form, because 2 and 5 share no factor other
than 1.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Write $1.25$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Two decimal places means hundredths. Start with $\frac{125}{100}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Divide the top and the bottom by 25.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1.25 = \frac{125}{100} = \frac{5}{4}$$

As an improper fraction the answer is $5/4$. It is also correct to write it as
$1\frac{1}{4}$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Write $\frac{3}{5}$ as a decimal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A fraction is a division. Work out $3 \div 5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Multiply the top and the bottom by 2 to get tenths: $\frac{6}{10}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$0.6$$

$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{6}{10}$, and six tenths is $0.6$. The decimal stops, which
happens when the denominator is built from 2s and 5s.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Is $12$ a rational number? Explain your answer.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Can you write 12 as a fraction of two integers?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write it over 1.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Yes. $12 = \frac{12}{1}$, and 12 and 1 are integers. Every integer is rational, so 12
is rational as well as natural.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Put $-5$, $2$, $-1$ and $0$ in order from smallest to largest.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Sketch a number line. The further left a number sits, the smaller it is.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The two negatives are the smallest, and $-5$ is further left than $-1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$-5, -1, 0, 2$$

Zero sits between the negatives and the positives. Of two negative numbers, the one
with the larger digit is the smaller number.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** A submarine is at a depth of $-30$ metres. It rises 20 metres. Is its new
depth a natural number, an integer, or neither?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Rising means moving up, towards zero. Work out $-30 + 20$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$-30 + 20 = -10$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The new depth is $-10$ metres, which is an integer. It is not a natural number,
because it is negative. The submarine is still below the surface.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from earlier work

**9.** Write down the value of the digit 4 in the number 2471.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Which place is the 4 in — units, tens, hundreds or thousands?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The digits are 2 thousands, 4 hundreds, 7 tens and 1 unit.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The 4 is in the hundreds place, so its value is $400$.

Place value is the idea behind the whole of this chapter. It is why $0.4$ means four
tenths and why $-20$ means twenty below zero.

</details>
</details>
</details>

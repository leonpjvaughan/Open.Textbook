# Chapter 1: Number systems and the four operations

The number systems, the four operations on them, and the properties those operations obey.

## The chapter problem

**The class trip budget.** Your year group is planning a trip. You will work with the numbers on the quotes — totals, deposits, per-person shares and a few negative balances — and by the end of the chapter you can say what the trip really costs and whether the money works out.

**Where it is used:** Budgets, quotes, invoices, and any calculation you check twice.

**Your turn to connect it:** Where in your own week do you use a negative number, or need to be sure a total is right?

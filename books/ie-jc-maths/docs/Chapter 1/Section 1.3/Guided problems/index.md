# 1.3 Multiplying and dividing integers — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $6 \times 7$.
2. Work out $48 \div 8$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $42$.
2. $6$, because $6 \times 8 = 48$.

Division undoes multiplication, so you can always check a division by multiplying
back. That check works with negative numbers too, which is the main idea of this
section.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Work out $(-3) \times 5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

One sign is negative and the other is positive. What sign will the answer have?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Different signs give a negative answer. Work out $3 \times 5$ and attach the sign.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(-3) \times 5 = -15$$

Three groups of 5 is 15, and the minus sign makes it $-15$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Work out $(-6) \times (-4)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Two negatives. Is the answer positive or negative?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Same signs give a positive answer. Work out $6 \times 4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(-6) \times (-4) = 24$$

Two negatives make a positive. You can check with the pattern from the Learn page:
each step the first number drops, the answer rises by 4.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Work out $9 \times (-2)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The signs are different, so the answer is negative.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$9 \times 2 = 18$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$9 \times (-2) = -18$$

A positive times a negative is negative. The order of the two numbers does not change
the sign.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Work out $(-42) \div 6$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Different signs give a negative answer. What is $42 \div 6$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$42 \div 6 = 7$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(-42) \div 6 = -7$$

Check by multiplying back: $(-7) \times 6 = -42$, which is the number you started
with.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Work out $(-56) \div (-8)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Two negatives in a division. What sign does the answer have?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Same signs give a positive answer. What is $56 \div 8$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(-56) \div (-8) = 7$$

Check: $7 \times (-8) = -56$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Work out $(-2) \times 5 \times (-3)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Count the minus signs first. Is the count odd or even?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

There are two minus signs, which is even, so the answer is positive. Work left to
right.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(-2) \times 5 = -10 \qquad -10 \times (-3) = 30$$

The answer is $30$. The two negatives paired up.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Work out $(-3)^2$ and $(-3)^3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write each power out as a product. How many minus signs are there in each?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$(-3)^2$ has two minus signs, and $(-3)^3$ has three.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(-3)^2 = 9 \qquad (-3)^3 = -27$$

An even index gives a positive answer, and an odd index keeps the minus sign.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** The temperature falls by 4 degrees each hour for 6 hours. Work out the total
change in temperature.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A fall is a negative change. You need 6 groups of $-4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$6 \times (-4)$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The total change is $-24$ degrees.

$$6 \times (-4) = -24$$

The answer is negative because the temperature fell. If it had risen, the change
would have been positive.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Work out $-9 + 4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

This is an addition, not a multiplication. Start at $-9$ and move 4 to the right.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$-9 + 4$ passes $-8$, $-7$, $-6$, $-5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$-9 + 4 = -5$$

The answer is still negative, because 9 is larger than 4. Compare this with
$(-9) \times 4 = -36$: the sign rules of multiplication are not the rules of
addition.

</details>
</details>
</details>

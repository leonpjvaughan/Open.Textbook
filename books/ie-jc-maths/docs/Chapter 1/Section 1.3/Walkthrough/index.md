# 1.3 Multiplying and dividing integers — Learn

**Prerequisites:** adding and subtracting integers from Section 1.2, and the
multiplication tables.

**Objectives:** multiply and divide integers; predict the sign of an answer; work with
powers of negative numbers; know why division by zero is not allowed.

By the end of this section, you can work out any product or quotient of two integers
and say the sign before you calculate.

## Multiplication is repeated addition

Multiplying is a short way of adding equal groups. The product $4 \times 3$ means four
groups of 3.

$$4 \times 3 = 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12$$

The same idea works with negatives. The product $(-4) \times 3$ means four groups of
$-3$, which is a total of $-12$.

$$(-4) \times 3 = (-3) + (-3) + (-3) + (-3) = -12$$

## The sign rules

There are four cases, and only two of them give a negative answer.

| Product or quotient | Sign of the answer | Example |
|---|---|---|
| positive with positive | positive | $4 \times 3 = 12$ |
| positive with negative | negative | $4 \times (-3) = -12$ |
| negative with positive | negative | $(-4) \times 3 = -12$ |
| negative with negative | positive | $(-4) \times (-3) = 12$ |

The two signs are the same: the answer is positive. The two signs are different: the
answer is negative.

## Why two negatives make a positive

Look at what happens when the first number in a product drops by 1 each time.

$$\begin{aligned}
3 \times (-4) &= -12\\
2 \times (-4) &= -8\\
1 \times (-4) &= -4\\
0 \times (-4) &= 0
\end{aligned}$$

Each time the first number falls by 1, the answer rises by 4. The pattern has to
continue, so the next line is:

$$(-1) \times (-4) = 4$$

That is why the product of two negatives is positive. The pattern leaves no other
possibility.

## Division

Division undoes multiplication. If you know the product, you can find the missing
factor.

$$(-9) \times 4 = -36 \qquad \text{so} \qquad (-36) \div 4 = -9$$

The sign rules are exactly the same as for multiplication. A negative divided by a
negative gives a positive.

$$(-48) \div (-6) = 8 \qquad \text{because} \qquad 8 \times (-6) = -48$$

## More than two numbers

Work from left to right. You can also count the minus signs first: an odd number of
them gives a negative answer, and an even number gives a positive answer.

**Example 1.** Work out $7 \times (-2) \times 3$.

There is one minus sign, so the answer is negative.

$$7 \times (-2) = -14 \qquad -14 \times 3 = -42$$

**Example 2.** Work out $(-2) \times (-3) \times (-4)$.

There are three minus signs, so the answer is negative.

$$(-2) \times (-3) = 6 \qquad 6 \times (-4) = -24$$

## Powers of negative numbers

A power is a repeated multiplication, so the sign rules apply again.

$$(-2)^3 = (-2) \times (-2) \times (-2) = -8$$

An **odd** index keeps the minus sign, because one negative is left unpaired. An
**even** index gives a positive answer, because the negatives pair up.

$$(-2)^2 = (-2) \times (-2) = 4$$

## Dividing by zero

You cannot divide by zero. The question $6 \div 0$ asks "what number times 0 gives
6?", and no number does. So the answer is undefined, and the calculation stops.

> **Watch out.** The brackets in a power change the answer. $(-2)^2$ means
> $(-2) \times (-2)$, which is $4$. But $-2^2$ means the negative of $2^2$, which is
> $-4$. The index applies only to the 2 unless a bracket says otherwise.
>
> **Re-check:** write the power out in full as a product before you decide the sign.
> That removes the guesswork.

## Progress check

Return to the chapter problem. The centre charges €14 for each of 35 people, and the
fund is €80 overdrawn before the contributions arrive.

1. Work out the total cost at the centre.
2. The money still to find is €577.50, shared equally by 35 people. Work out each
   person's share.
3. The fund balance is $-80$ euros. Each of the 35 people then pays their share into

## You should now be able to

- multiply and divide integers and state the sign of the answer first;
- explain why the product of two negative numbers is positive;
- work out a product with several negative factors;
- work out a power of a negative number, with and without brackets;
- explain why division by zero is not defined.

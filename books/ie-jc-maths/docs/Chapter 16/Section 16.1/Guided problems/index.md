# 16.1 Perimeter and area of rectangles and triangles — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $7\times8$.
2. Write 2.5 m in centimetres.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $56$. The multiplication facts are the same ones you use in every area formula.
2. $250$ cm. There are 100 cm in 1 m, so multiply by 100.

Both skills return in this section. A perimeter adds lengths, and an area multiplies
two lengths. Check the unit before you start.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A rectangle is 8 cm long and 3 cm wide. Find its perimeter.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Perimeter is the distance around the edge. Add the length and the width, then double.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2(8+3)$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The perimeter is $22$ cm.

$$2(8+3)=2\times11=22$$

The unit is centimetres, because a perimeter is a length.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A rectangle is 15 m long and 4 m wide. Find its area.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Area is the surface inside the rectangle. Multiply the length by the width.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$15\times4$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The area is $60$ m².

$$15\times4=60$$

The unit is square metres, because an area is measured in squares.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A triangle has a base of 12 cm and a perpendicular height of 7 cm. Find its area.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A triangle is half of a rectangle. Use half of base times height.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\tfrac{1}{2}\times12\times7$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The area is $42$ cm².

$$\tfrac{1}{2}\times12\times7=6\times7=42$$

Halve the base first, which makes the multiplication quicker.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A triangle has an area of 36 m² and a base of 9 m. Find its perpendicular height.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Put the numbers you know into $A=\tfrac{1}{2}\times\text{base}\times\text{height}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$36=\tfrac{1}{2}\times9\times h \qquad 36=4.5h$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The height is $8$ m.

$$h=36\div4.5=8$$

Check it: $\tfrac{1}{2}\times9\times8=36$ m², which matches the area in the question.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** A rectangle has an area of 72 cm² and a length of 9 cm. Find its width.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The area is length times width. Divide the area by the length.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$72\div9$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The width is $8$ cm.

$$72\div9=8$$

Check by multiplying: $9\times8=72$ cm².

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** A square has sides of 6 m. Find its perimeter and its area.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A square has four equal sides. The perimeter adds them, and the area multiplies two
of them.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$P=4\times6 \qquad A=6\times6$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The perimeter is $24$ m and the area is $36$ m².

$$4\times6=24 \qquad 6\times6=36$$

The two answers have different units. A perimeter is a length, and an area is a
surface.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** The chapter plot is a rectangle 10 m long and 6 m wide. Find its perimeter.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The plot has two sides of 10 m and two sides of 6 m.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2(10+6)$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The perimeter is $32$ m.

$$2(10+6)=2\times16=32$$

This is the length of fence needed to go right around the plot.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** The triangular bed in the chapter plot has two shorter sides of 4 m and 3 m,
meeting at a right angle. Find its area.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The two shorter sides meet at a right angle, so one is the base and the other is the
height.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\tfrac{1}{2}\times4\times3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The bed has an area of $6$ m².

$$\tfrac{1}{2}\times4\times3=2\times3=6$$

This is the area you will subtract from the plot in Section 16.3.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Work out $1.4\times0.8$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply the digits as whole numbers first, then place the decimal point.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$14\times8=112$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1.4\times0.8=1.12$$

There is one digit after the decimal point in each number, so the answer has two
digits after the decimal point. This skill returns when you multiply decimal lengths
to find an area.

</details>
</details>
</details>

# 2.4 Fractional indices and roots — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $5^0$.
2. Write $2^{-3}$ as a fraction, and work out its value.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $1$. Any non-zero number to the power zero is 1.
2. $\frac{1}{8}$. A negative index gives a reciprocal, so
   $2^{-3} = \frac{1}{2^3} = \frac{1}{8}$.

Both answers come from Section 2.3. This section keeps the same rules and changes
the index from a whole number to a fraction. A negative fractional index uses both
ideas at once.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Work out $25^{\frac12}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

An index of $\frac12$ means the square root.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$25^{\frac12} = \sqrt{25}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$25^{\frac12} = \sqrt{25} = 5$$

Check it: $5^2 = 25$, so 5 is the number whose square is 25.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Work out $64^{\frac13}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

An index of $\frac13$ means the cube root. Which number cubed gives 64?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$64^{\frac13} = \sqrt[3]{64}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$64^{\frac13} = \sqrt[3]{64} = 4$$

Check it: $4^3 = 64$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Work out $81^{\frac14}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The denominator 4 tells you to take the fourth root.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Which number raised to the power 4 gives 81?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$81^{\frac14} = \sqrt[4]{81} = 3$$

Check it: $3^4 = 81$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Work out $8^{\frac23}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The denominator is the root and the numerator is the power. Take the root first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$8^{\frac23} = \left(8^{\frac13}\right)^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$8^{\frac23} = \left(8^{\frac13}\right)^2 = 2^2 = 4$$

The cube root of 8 is 2, and then $2^2 = 4$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Work out $16^{\frac34}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Take the fourth root first, then raise the result to the power 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$16^{\frac34} = \left(16^{\frac14}\right)^3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$16^{\frac34} = \left(16^{\frac14}\right)^3 = 2^3 = 8$$

The fourth root of 16 is 2, and $2^3 = 8$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Work out $4^{-\frac12}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The negative index gives a reciprocal. Deal with the reciprocal first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$4^{-\frac12} = \dfrac{1}{4^{\frac12}}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4^{-\frac12} = \frac{1}{4^{\frac12}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

The square root of 4 is 2, and the negative index moves it underneath a 1. The
answer is positive.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Work out $27^{-\frac23}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write the reciprocal first, then work out the positive fractional power.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$27^{-\frac23} = \dfrac{1}{27^{\frac23}}$, and
$27^{\frac23} = \left(27^{\frac13}\right)^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$27^{-\frac23} = \frac{1}{\left(27^{\frac13}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{3^2} = \frac{1}{9}$$

The cube root of 27 is 3, and $3^2 = 9$. The negative index then gives
$\frac{1}{9}$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** Estimate $2^{\frac12}$ to 3 decimal places, and check your answer by
squaring it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

$2^{\frac12}$ is the square root of 2, which sits between 1 and 2.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Try 1.4 and 1.5. Square each one to see which is closer to 2.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$2^{\frac12} \approx 1.414$$

Check it: $1.414^2 = 1.999396$, which is very close to 2. The exact value carries
on forever, so 1.414 is a rounded value.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Simplify $\dfrac{3^7\times3^2}{3^6}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the indices on top, then subtract the index underneath.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$3^7\times3^2 = 3^9$, so the fraction is $3^9\div3^6$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{3^9}{3^6} = 3^3 = 27$$

The two laws used here are the product law and the quotient law from Section 2.2.

</details>
</details>
</details>

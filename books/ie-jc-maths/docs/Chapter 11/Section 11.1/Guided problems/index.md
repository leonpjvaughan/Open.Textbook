# 11.1 Adding and subtracting algebraic expressions — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write an expression for 4 more than $x$.
2. What is the coefficient of $y$ in $9y$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $x+4$. The 4 is added to $x$.
2. The coefficient is 9. The coefficient is the number that multiplies the letter.

A term is a part joined by a plus or a minus sign, and its coefficient is the number
in front. Both ideas return in every question on this page.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Simplify $6x + 3x$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The two terms are like terms. Add the coefficients.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$6 + 3 = 9$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$6x + 3x = 9x$$

The letter part does not change. There are 6 copies of $x$ and 3 more, which is 9
copies of $x$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Simplify $8a - 5a + 2a$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

All three terms are like terms. Work through them from left to right.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$8 - 5 = 3 \qquad 3 + 2 = 5$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$8a - 5a + 2a = 5a$$

The coefficients are 8, $-5$ and 2, and they add to 5.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Simplify $4x + 3y + 2x + 5y$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Collect the $x$ terms together and the $y$ terms together. They are different letter
parts, so they stay apart.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$4x + 2x = 6x \qquad 3y + 5y = 8y$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$6x + 8y$$

The $x$ terms and the $y$ terms cannot be added to each other, because the letters
differ.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Simplify $(2x+3)+(5x+4)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Adding a bracket adds each term inside it, so the brackets can be removed.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2x + 3 + 5x + 4$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$7x + 7$$

The $x$ terms give $2x+5x=7x$, and the numbers give $3+4=7$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Simplify $(6a+5)-(2a+3)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The minus sign changes the sign of both terms in the second bracket.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$6a + 5 - 2a - 3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4a + 2$$

The $a$ terms give $6a-2a=4a$, and the numbers give $5-3=2$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Simplify $(9x+4)-(3x-7)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The $-7$ is inside the second bracket, and the minus sign in front changes it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$9x + 4 - 3x + 7$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$6x + 11$$

The $x$ terms give $9x-3x=6x$, and the numbers give $4+7=11$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Simplify $2(x+3)+4(x+1)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply out each bracket first, and then collect the like terms.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2x + 6 + 4x + 4$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$6x + 10$$

The $x$ terms give $2x+4x=6x$, and the numbers give $6+4=10$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** Simplify $5(x+2)-3(x-1)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The minus sign applies to the whole second bracket, so the $-1$ becomes $+1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$5x + 10 - 3x + 3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$2x + 13$$

The $x$ terms give $5x-3x=2x$, and the numbers give $10+3=13$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from earlier work

**9.** A rectangle is $x$ metres long and 4 metres wide. Write an expression for its
perimeter, and simplify it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

From Section 10.1: the perimeter is twice the length plus twice the width.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2x + 2\times4$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$2x + 8$$

The two lengths give $2x$, and the two widths give 8. The terms are not like terms, so
they stay apart.

</details>
</details>
</details>

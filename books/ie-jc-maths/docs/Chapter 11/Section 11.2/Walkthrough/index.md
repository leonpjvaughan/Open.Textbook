# 11.2 Multiplying out a single bracket — Learn

**Prerequisites:** collecting like terms from Section 11.1, and the distributive
property from Section 1.5.

**Objectives:** multiply out a bracket by a number; multiply out a bracket by a letter
or a term such as $2x$; multiply out a bracket with a minus sign in front; expand two
brackets and collect the like terms.

By the end of this section, you can write an expression with one bracket as an
expression without brackets.

## The distributive property

Multiplying out a bracket, or **expanding**, uses the distributive property.

$$a(b+c) = ab + ac$$

The number outside the bracket multiplies **every** term inside it.

**Example 1.** Expand $3(x+4)$.

$$3\times x + 3\times4 = 3x + 12$$

**Example 2.** Expand $5(2x-3)$.

$$5\times2x - 5\times3 = 10x - 15$$

**Example 3.** Expand $x(x+5)$.

$$x\times x + x\times5 = x^2 + 5x$$

Remember that $x\times x = x^2$.

**Example 4.** Expand $2x(3x-4)$.

$$2x\times3x - 2x\times4 = 6x^2 - 8x$$

## A minus sign in front

A minus sign in front of a bracket is the same as multiplying by $-1$. Every sign
inside the bracket changes.

**Example 5.** Expand $-(x+3)$.

$$-1\times x - 1\times3 = -x - 3$$

**Example 6.** Expand $-2(3x-5)$.

$$-2\times3x + 2\times5 = -6x + 10$$

The $-2$ times the $-5$ is $+10$.

## Expand and simplify

When two brackets are added or subtracted, expand each one and then collect the like
terms.

**Example 7.** Expand and simplify $3(x+2)+4(x+5)$.

$$3x + 6 + 4x + 20$$

$$7x + 26$$

**Example 8.** Expand and simplify $5(x+3)-2(x+1)$.

$$5x + 15 - 2x - 2$$

$$3x + 13$$

**Example 9.** Expand and simplify $2(3x-1)-3(2x+4)$.

$$6x - 2 - 6x - 12$$

$$-14$$

The $x$ terms cancel, so the answer is a number.

> **Watch out.** The number outside the bracket multiplies every term inside. From
> $4(x+3)$, the common error is to write $4x+3$, multiplying only the first term. The
> correct expansion is $4x+12$.
>
> **Re-check:** put a number in for $x$ and compare. With $x=1$: $4(1+3)=16$, and
> $4(1)+12=16$. The wrong form gives $4+3=7$, which does not match.

## Progress check

Return to the chapter problem. The garden plot is $(x+5)$ metres long and $(x+2)$
metres wide.

1. Write the perimeter of the plot as $2(x+5)+2(x+2)$.
2. Expand and simplify to find the perimeter.

In Section 11.3 you will multiply two brackets together to find the area of the plot.

## You should now be able to

- multiply out a bracket by a number;
- multiply out a bracket by a letter or by a term such as $2x$;
- multiply out a bracket with a minus sign in front;
- expand two brackets and collect the like terms.

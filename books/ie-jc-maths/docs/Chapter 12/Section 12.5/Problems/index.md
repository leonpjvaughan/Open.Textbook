# 12.5 Linear inequalities — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Solve an inequality as you would an equation. Turn the sign around when you multiply or
divide by a negative number, and keep it the same when the number is positive. Say
whether the endpoint is included.

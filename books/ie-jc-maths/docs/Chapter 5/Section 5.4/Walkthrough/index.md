# 5.4 Fractions, decimals and percentages — Learn

**Prerequisites:** equivalent fractions from Section 5.1, and multiplying and
dividing by 100.

**Objectives:** change a fraction to a decimal; change a decimal to a fraction;
change a fraction or decimal to a percentage; change a percentage to a fraction or a
decimal.

By the end of this section, you can move between the three forms and order a mixed
list of them.

## A fraction is a division

The bar of a fraction is a division sign. The fraction $\frac{3}{4}$ means
$3\div4$. That is the bridge between fractions and decimals.

**Example 1.** Write $\frac{3}{4}$ as a decimal.

$$3\div4=0.75$$

A quarter is $0.25$, and three of them make $0.75$.

**Example 2.** Write $\frac{1}{3}$ as a decimal, correct to 2 decimal places.

$$1\div3=0.333\ldots$$

The threes never stop. The third decimal digit is 3, which is less than 5, so the
answer is $0.33$ to 2 decimal places.

## A decimal is a fraction

Every decimal has a place value, and that tells you the denominator.

$$0.6=\frac{6}{10} \qquad 0.25=\frac{25}{100} \qquad 0.125=\frac{125}{1000}$$

Then simplify the fraction.

**Example 3.** Write $0.6$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

$$0.6=\frac{6}{10}=\frac{3}{5}$$

Divide the top and the bottom by 2.

**Example 4.** Write $0.125$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

$$0.125=\frac{125}{1000}=\frac{1}{8}$$

The highest common factor of 125 and 1000 is 125.

## Percentages

**Per cent** means "per hundred". So a percentage is a fraction with denominator 100.

$$60\%=\frac{60}{100}$$

To change a fraction or a decimal to a percentage, multiply by 100. To change a
percentage to a decimal, divide by 100.

**Example 5.** Write $\frac{3}{5}$ as a percentage.

$$\frac{3}{5}\times100=60$$

So $\frac{3}{5}=60\%$. You can also write $\frac{3}{5}$ with the denominator 100:
$\frac{3}{5}=\frac{60}{100}$.

**Example 6.** Write $45\%$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

$$45\%=\frac{45}{100}=\frac{9}{20}$$

Divide the top and the bottom by 5.

**Example 7.** Write $80\%$ as a decimal.

$$80\div100=0.8$$

Dividing by 100 moves every digit two places to the right of the decimal point.

**Example 8.** Write $\frac{7}{20}$ as a percentage.

$$\frac{7}{20}\times100=35$$

So $\frac{7}{20}=35\%$. Because $20\times5=100$, you can also write the fraction as
$\frac{35}{100}$.

**Example 9.** Write $\frac{5}{8}$ as a percentage.

$$\frac{5}{8}\times100=62.5$$

A percentage does not have to be a whole number.

## A table to remember

| Fraction | Decimal | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| $\frac{1}{2}$ | $0.5$ | $50\%$ |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ | $0.25$ | $25\%$ |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ | $0.75$ | $75\%$ |
| $\frac{1}{5}$ | $0.2$ | $20\%$ |
| $\frac{1}{10}$ | $0.1$ | $10\%$ |
| $\frac{1}{3}$ | $0.333\ldots$ | $33.3\%$ |

## Ordering a mixed list

To order fractions, decimals and percentages together, write them all in the same
form. Decimals are usually the quickest.

**Example 10.** Write $0.7$, $\frac{2}{3}$ and $65\%$ in order, smallest first.

As decimals they are $0.70$, $0.666\ldots$ and $0.65$. In order:

$$65\%, \quad \frac{2}{3}, \quad 0.7$$

> **Watch out.** The digits of a fraction are not its decimal digits. From
> $\frac{3}{5}$, the common error is to write $0.35$ by putting the 3 and the 5 after
> the decimal point. The fraction is a division, so $\frac{3}{5}=3\div5=0.6$.
>
> **Re-check:** multiply the decimal by the denominator. $0.6\times5=3$, which is the
> numerator, so $0.6$ is correct. And $0.35\times5=1.75$, which is not 3, so $0.35$
> is wrong.

## Progress check

Return to the chapter problem. One jug of punch holds $\frac{3}{2}$ litres, and
$\frac{3}{4}$ of a litre of it is orange juice.

1. Write the fraction of the jug that is orange juice:
   $\frac{3}{4}\div\frac{3}{2}$.
2. Write your answer as a decimal.
3. Write it as a percentage. What share of every jug is orange juice?

You will use percentages again in Section 5.5, when the price of the juice changes.

## You should now be able to

- change a fraction to a decimal by dividing the numerator by the denominator;
- change a decimal to a fraction using place value, and simplify it;
- change a fraction or decimal to a percentage by multiplying by 100;
- change a percentage to a fraction or a decimal, and order a mixed list of them.

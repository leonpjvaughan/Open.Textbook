# 5.4 Fractions, decimals and percentages — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $3\div4$.
2. Write $\frac{2}{5}$ with the denominator 100.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $0.75$. The bar of a fraction is a division sign, so this is how a fraction
   becomes a decimal.
2. $\frac{40}{100}$. The denominator was multiplied by 20, so the numerator is
   multiplied by 20 as well.

A percentage is a fraction with denominator 100, so the second answer is $40\%$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Write $\frac{7}{10}$ as a decimal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the numerator by the denominator.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$7\div10$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$7\div10=0.7$$

The denominator is 10, so the answer has one decimal place.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Write $0.25$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The last digit is in the hundredths place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$0.25=\frac{25}{100}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{25}{100}=\frac{1}{4}$$

Divide the top and the bottom by 25. You can check the answer: one quarter is
$0.25$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Write $\frac{2}{5}$ as a percentage.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply the fraction by 100.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{2}{5}\times100$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{2}{5}\times100=40$$

So $\frac{2}{5}=40\%$. Check it another way: $\frac{2}{5}=0.4$, and $0.4\times100=40$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Write $30\%$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A percentage is a fraction with denominator 100.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$30\%=\frac{30}{100}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{30}{100}=\frac{3}{10}$$

Divide the top and the bottom by 10. Three tenths is $0.3$, and $0.3=30\%$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Write $\frac{1}{8}$ as a decimal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide 1 by 8. You may need to write 1 as $1.000$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$1\div8$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1\div8=0.125$$

Check it by multiplying: $0.125\times8=1$. The answer stops after three decimal
places, so no rounding is needed.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Write $\frac{9}{20}$ as a percentage.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply by 100, or write the fraction with the denominator 100.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{9}{20}\times100$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{9}{20}\times100=45$$

So $\frac{9}{20}=45\%$. Because $20\times5=100$, the fraction is also
$\frac{45}{100}$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Write $\frac{1}{2}$, $0.45$ and $55\%$ in order, smallest first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write all three as decimals.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{1}{2}=0.5 \qquad 0.45=0.45 \qquad 55\%=0.55$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$0.45, \quad \frac{1}{2}, \quad 55\%$$

The decimals are $0.45$, $0.50$ and $0.55$. Writing all three in the same form is
what makes the order clear.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** One jug of punch holds $\frac{3}{2}$ litres, and $\frac{3}{4}$ of a litre of it
is orange juice. What percentage of the jug is orange juice?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the orange juice by the whole jug, then multiply by 100.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{3}{4}\div\frac{3}{2}=\frac{3}{4}\times\frac{2}{3}=\frac{1}{2}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{1}{2}=0.5=50\%$$

Half of every jug is orange juice. The other half is the apple juice and the
sparkling water together.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Work out $\frac{2}{3}$ of 15.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Look back at Section 5.3. The word "of" means multiply.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$15\div3=5$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

One third of 15 is 5, so two thirds is $2\times5=10$.

Check it by multiplying: $\frac{2}{3}\times15=\frac{30}{3}=10$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

# 6.3 Direct proportion — Learn

**Prerequisites:** ratio notation from Section 6.1, and multiplying and dividing
decimals.

**Objectives:** recognise when two quantities are directly proportional; use the
unitary method to find a missing value; decide whether a pair of values is in
direct proportion.

By the end of this section, you can find the value of one unit and scale it to any
number of units.

## Two quantities that grow together

Two quantities are in **direct proportion** when they change by the same factor.
Double one and the other doubles. Halve one and the other halves.

The cost of pens is a good example. Each pen costs the same, so 10 pens cost twice
as much as 5 pens. The cost and the number of pens grow together.

The constant that links them is the **value of one unit**: the price of a single
pen. Find that, and every other question follows from it.

## The unitary method

The method has two steps.

1. Find the value of **one** unit.
2. Multiply by the number of units you want.

**Example 1.** Three pens cost 1.50 euro. Find the cost of seven pens.

**Step 1.** Find the cost of one pen.

$$1.50\div3 = 0.50$$

One pen costs 0.50 euro.

**Step 2.** Multiply by 7.

$$0.50\times7 = 3.50$$

Seven pens cost 3.50 euro.

Check the size of the answer. Seven pens cost more than three pens, and 3.50 is
more than 1.50, so the answer is plausible. You can also see that 7 is between 6
and 9, and the cost of 6 pens would be 3 euro, so 3.50 euro sits where it should.

**Example 2.** Five kilograms of apples cost 6 euro. Find the cost of eight
kilograms.

$$6\div5 = 1.20$$

One kilogram costs 1.20 euro.

$$1.20\times8 = 9.60$$

Eight kilograms cost 9.60 euro.

The value of one unit is the bridge between the two amounts. Once you have it, any
other quantity is one multiplication away.

## Scaling a recipe

The same method works for any pair of directly proportional quantities, not only
for money.

**Example 3.** A recipe for 4 people uses 300 g of rice. How much rice is needed
for 6 people?

$$300\div4 = 75$$

One person needs 75 g.

$$75\times6 = 450$$

Six people need 450 g.

The answer is bigger than 300 g, which it must be, because 6 people eat more than
4 people.

## The school tour

**Example 4.** On the school tour, 48 packed lunches cost 168 euro. Find the cost
of one packed lunch, and the cost of 60 packed lunches.

$$168\div48 = 3.50$$

One packed lunch costs 3.50 euro.

$$3.50\times60 = 210$$

Sixty packed lunches cost 210 euro.

Every lunch has the same price, so the total cost is directly proportional to the
number of lunches. The price of one lunch is the constant that links them.

## How to tell direct proportion from a table

Two quantities are directly proportional when the value of one unit is the same
every time. Divide each pair and compare.

**Example 5.** A shop sells 2 bars for 1.20 euro and 5 bars for 3.20 euro. Is the
cost directly proportional to the number of bars?

$$1.20\div2 = 0.60 \qquad 3.20\div5 = 0.64$$

The cost of one bar is 0.60 euro in the first offer and 0.64 euro in the second.
The two values are different, so the cost is **not** directly proportional to the
number of bars. The shop charges a little more per bar in the larger pack.

## Investigate direct and inverse

**Predict.** Before you move the controls, predict which of the two graphs is a straight
line.

:::figure proportion-curves

**Test.** The figure draws direct proportion and inverse proportion on one pair of axes.
Set the value of one unit and the number of units, and watch both graphs.

**Explain.** Write two or three sentences. Say what happens to the value in direct
proportion when the number of units doubles, and what happens in inverse proportion.
Use the words "straight line" and "curves down".

> **Watch out.** Direct proportion is about multiplying, not adding. If 3 pens
> cost 1.50 euro, 4 pens do not cost 1.50 euro plus 1 euro. Find the value of one
> pen and multiply: 4 pens cost $4\times0.50=2$ euro.

> **Watch out.** Check that the two quantities really do grow together before you
> use this method. If you are told that 4 workers take 6 hours, that is not direct
> proportion: more workers means **less** time. That idea is the next section.

## Progress check

Back to the school tour. Each packed lunch costs 3.50 euro.

Find the total cost of 48 packed lunches. Then find the cost of 60 packed lunches,
and write one sentence saying what happened to the total when the number of lunches
went up by a quarter.

## You should now be able to

- say whether two quantities are directly proportional, and give a reason;
- find the value of one unit by dividing;
- scale the value of one unit up to any number of units by multiplying;
- check that your answer is the right size compared with the numbers you started
  with.

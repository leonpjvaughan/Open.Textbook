# 6.5 Absolute and relative comparison — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

For each question, decide first whether it wants an absolute change or a relative
one. Write the unit for an absolute change, and the percentage sign for a relative
one. The two are not interchangeable.

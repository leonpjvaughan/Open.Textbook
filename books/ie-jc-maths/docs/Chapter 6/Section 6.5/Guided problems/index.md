# 6.5 Absolute and relative comparison — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write 0.25 as a percentage.
2. Work out $80-60$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $25\%$. Multiply a decimal by 100 to change it to a percentage.
2. $20$.

You will need both in this section. The subtraction finds an absolute change, and
the multiplication by 100 turns a relative change into a percentage.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** A price rises from 20 euro to 23 euro. Find the absolute increase and the
percentage increase.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract to find the absolute increase, then divide by the original value.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$23-20=3$, and $3\div20=0.15$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The absolute increase is 3 euro. The relative increase is
$3\div20=0.15$, which is 15%.

Divide by 20, the original price. The absolute increase keeps the unit of euro;
the percentage has no unit.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A price falls from 50 euro to 40 euro. Find the absolute decrease and the
percentage decrease.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract, then divide by the value you started with.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$50-40=10$, and $10\div50=0.2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The absolute decrease is 10 euro, and the percentage decrease is
$10\div50=0.2=20\%$.

Divide by 50, the original price, not by 40. Dividing by the new value would
describe the change against the wrong starting point.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A town's population rises from 5000 to 5500. Find the percentage increase.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the increase in people first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$5500-5000=500$, and $500\div5000=0.1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The percentage increase is 10%. An increase of 500 people sounds large, but
against a town of 5000 it is one part in ten.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A jacket costs 80 euro and is reduced by 25%. Find the new price.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find 25% of 80 euro first, then subtract.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$80\times25\div100=20$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The reduction is 20 euro, so the new price is $80-20=60$ euro.

A 25% reduction means you pay 75% of the price, and $80\times0.75=60$ euro as
well. Both routes give the same answer.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Which is the bigger increase, 4 euro on 16 euro or 4 euro on 40 euro? Give
the percentage increase for each.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The absolute increase is the same in both cases, so compare the percentages.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$4\div16=0.25$ and $4\div40=0.1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

4 euro on 16 euro is the bigger increase. It is $25\%$, while 4 euro on 40 euro is
$10\%$.

Both increases are 4 euro, so only the percentage can tell them apart. The same
absolute change is a larger share of a smaller starting amount.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** A worker's pay rises from 10 euro an hour to 11.50 euro an hour. Find the
percentage increase.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the increase in euro, then divide by the original hourly rate.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$11.50-10=1.50$, and $1.50\div10=0.15$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The percentage increase is 15%. The increase of 1.50 euro an hour is 15 parts in
every 100 of the old rate.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Last year the coach for the school tour cost 450 euro. This year it costs
480 euro. Find the absolute increase and the percentage increase, correct to 1
decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract, then divide by 450.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$480-450=30$, and $30\div450=0.0666\ldots$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The absolute increase is 30 euro. The percentage increase is
$0.0666\ldots=6.7\%$ to 1 decimal place.

The fraction $30\div450$ simplifies to $1\div15$, so the exact percentage is
$6\frac{2}{3}\%$. The coach is more expensive, but by less than a tenth of last
year's price.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** A shop increases a price by 20%, and the new price is 54 euro. Find the
original price.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The new price is 120% of the original. What do you divide by?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Divide by 1.2: $54\div1.2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The original price is 45 euro.

Check it: 20% of 45 euro is 9 euro, and $45+9=54$ euro. Do not take 20% off 54
euro, which would give 43.20 euro. A percentage rise is measured from the original
price, so undoing it means dividing by 1.2.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**9.** In Class A, 12 of 16 students walk to school. In Class B, 20 of 25 students
walk to school. Which class has the higher proportion of walkers?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find each proportion as a percentage.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$12\div16=0.75$ and $20\div25=0.8$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Class B has the higher proportion. Class A has $12\div16=75\%$ walkers, and Class
B has $20\div25=80\%$.

Class B has more walkers in total, and it also has the higher proportion. The
proportion is what answers the question, because the two classes are different
sizes.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**10.** Write the ratio $16:20$ in its simplest form.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide both parts by the highest common factor of 16 and 20.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The highest common factor is 4.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$16:20 = 4:5$$

A ratio and a proportion are closely related. If 16 out of 20 students walk to
school, the ratio of walkers to the whole group is $16:20=4:5$, and the proportion
who walk is $\frac{16}{20}=80\%$. Dividing the first part by the second turns a
ratio into a proportion.

</details>
</details>
</details>

# 6.2 Dividing a quantity in a given ratio — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Several of these are word problems. Before you calculate, write down the total
number of parts and the value of one part. Then check your shares by adding them
back to the whole. A share that does not fit the check is wrong, however
reasonable it looks.

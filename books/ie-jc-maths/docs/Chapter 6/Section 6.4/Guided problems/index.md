# 6.4 Inverse proportion — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Three pens cost 1.50 euro. Find the cost of seven pens.
2. Work out $4\times6$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $3.50$ euro. One pen costs $1.50\div3=0.50$ euro, and $0.50\times7=3.50$ euro.
   This is direct proportion: more pens cost more.
2. $24$.

Both answers are used in this section. The multiplication in question 2 is the
kind of product that stays constant in an inverse proportion question. The direct
proportion in question 1 is what you must not use here.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Five workers build a wall in 8 hours. How long would four workers take?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the constant product first: workers times hours.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$5\times8=40$ worker-hours, and $40\div4=10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Four workers take 10 hours. Fewer workers means more time, so the answer is bigger
than 8 hours, which is the check to make.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A journey takes 5 hours at 60 km/h. How long does it take at 75 km/h?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The distance is the same for both speeds. Find it first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$60\times5=300$ km, and $300\div75=4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The journey takes 4 hours at 75 km/h. Going faster shortens the time, and the
distance of 300 km does not change.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Eight taps fill a tank in 15 minutes. How long would six taps take?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the constant product: taps times minutes.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$8\times15=120$ tap-minutes, and $120\div6=20$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Six taps take 20 minutes. Fewer taps fill the tank more slowly, so the time is
longer than 15 minutes.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Six people share a prize equally and receive 30 euro each. How much would
each receive if ten people shared the same prize?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the total value of the prize first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$6\times30=180$ euro, and $180\div10=18$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Each of ten people receives 18 euro. The prize does not change, so sharing it
between more people gives each person less.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** A car takes 3 hours to make a journey at 80 km/h. How long does the same
journey take at 60 km/h?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the distance first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$80\times3=240$ km, and $240\div60=4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The journey takes 4 hours at 60 km/h. The speed is lower this time, so the time is
longer than 3 hours. Check the direction before you write the answer down.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Four pumps empty a tank in 9 hours. How long would six pumps take?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply the pumps by the hours.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$4\times9=36$ pump-hours, and $36\div6=6$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Six pumps take 6 hours. More pumps empty the tank faster, so the time is shorter
than 9 hours.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** The coach for the school tour costs 480 euro. If 32 students go, each pays
15 euro. How much does each pay if 40 students go?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The coach cost is the constant. Check the product first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$32\times15=480$, and $480\div40=12$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Each of 40 students pays 12 euro. More students means less each, because the same
480 euro is shared between more people.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** Eight machines take 15 minutes to complete a job. How many machines are
needed to finish the same job in 12 minutes?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the constant product, then divide by the new time.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$8\times15=120$ machine-minutes, and $120\div12=10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Ten machines are needed. This question asks for the number of machines rather than
the time, but the constant product is found in the same way: multiply the pair you
know, then divide by the value you are given.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**9.** Four workers take 9 hours to finish a job, and six workers take 6 hours.
Show that the time is inversely proportional to the number of workers.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find workers times hours for each pair and compare.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$4\times9=36$ and $6\times6=36$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Both products are 36, so the time is inversely proportional to the number of
workers. Inverse proportion means the product of the two quantities stays the
same. When the number of workers goes up by half, from 4 to 6, the time falls by a
third, from 9 hours to 6.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**10.** Five kilograms of apples cost 6 euro. Find the cost of eight kilograms.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the cost of one kilogram first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$6\div5=1.20$ euro for one kilogram.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1.20\times8 = 9.60$$

Eight kilograms cost 9.60 euro.

This is direct proportion: more kilograms cost more, and the cost per kilogram
stays the same. Compare it with question 1 of this section. There, more workers
meant less time, so the product stayed the same instead of the value of one unit.

</details>
</details>
</details>

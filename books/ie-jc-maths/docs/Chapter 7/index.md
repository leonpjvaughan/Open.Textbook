# Chapter 7: Money and financial mathematics

Bills, VAT, profit and loss, mark-up and margin, income tax, net pay, and compound interest.

## The chapter problem

**The first payslip.** You will take a gross wage and work all the way down to what actually reaches a bank account — deductions, tax, and the difference between mark-up and margin on the way. By the end of the chapter you can read a payslip and say where each figure came from.

**Where it is used:** Payslips, bills, savings, borrowing and buying.

**Your turn to connect it:** Where would understanding interest or a payslip help you or someone you know in the next two years?

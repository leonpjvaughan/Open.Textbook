# Chapter 7: Money and financial mathematics

Money questions look different from the maths in the rest of this book. They are
not. A price with VAT, a wage with tax and a loan with interest are all
percentages in a setting. In this chapter you learn to read the numbers on a
payslip, a receipt and a bill, and to say where each one came from.

## The chapter problem

**The first payslip.** You start your first full-time job in a phone shop. You are
paid €15.00 an hour for a 40-hour week, so your gross pay is €600.00 a week. Your
payslip shows the income tax taken, the tax credit, the PRSI, and the net pay that
reaches your bank account.

The shop also sells a phone case it buys for €4.00. The price on the shelf includes
VAT, so the shop's profit is not the difference between the shelf price and €4.00.

Before you calculate anything, write down a prediction. Do you expect the net pay
to be nearer €400, €500 or €600 a week? A prediction costs nothing, and it gives
you a number to check your final answer against.

By the end of the chapter you can read every line of the payslip, and explain why
the shop's profit on the case is not the shelf price minus €4.00. You will return
to this payslip in every section, and you will solve it on the Review page.

## By the end of this chapter you can

- work out a profit or a loss, and write it as a percentage of the cost price;
- tell a mark-up from a margin, and work out each one;
- add VAT to a price, and take VAT off a price that already includes it;
- read a payslip: work out gross pay, income tax, a tax credit, PRSI and net pay;
- work out simple interest and compound interest, and say which is larger;
- compare two offers by working out the price of one unit;
- change an amount from one currency to another, and back again.

## Where it is used

Payslips, bills, savings, borrowing and buying. Every adult money decision rests
on one of the ideas in this chapter: what a wage is worth after tax, what a loan
costs over time, and whether one offer is better value than another. A payslip and
a bank statement are read with the four operations you already know.

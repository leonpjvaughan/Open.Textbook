# 7.4 Income tax and net pay — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Use the model from this section: $20\%$ on the first €800.00 of weekly pay, $40\%$
above it, a weekly tax credit of €60.00, and PRSI at $4\%$ of gross pay.

A tax credit reduces the tax, and it never makes the tax negative. Work out the
tax before the credit first.

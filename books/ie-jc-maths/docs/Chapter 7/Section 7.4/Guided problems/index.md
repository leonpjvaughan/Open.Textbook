# 7.4 Income tax and net pay — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $0.20\times600$.
2. Work out $0.04\times600$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $120$. Multiplying by $0.20$ finds $20\%$ of the amount.
2. $24$. Multiplying by $0.04$ finds $4\%$ of the amount.

A rate becomes a decimal, and the decimal multiplies the pay.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A worker is paid €15.00 an hour for 40 hours. Work out the gross pay.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Gross pay is the hourly rate multiplied by the hours.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$15.00\times40$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$15.00\times40=600.00$$

The gross pay is €600.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A worker's gross pay is €600.00. Work out the income tax before the credit,
at $20\%$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

All of €600.00 is below the €800.00 cut-off, so the whole amount is taxed at
$20\%$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$600.00\times0.20$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$600.00\times0.20=120.00$$

The income tax before the credit is €120.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A worker's income tax before the credit is €120.00, and the tax credit is
€60.00. Work out the income tax.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Take the credit off the tax.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$120.00-60.00$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$120.00-60.00=60.00$$

The income tax is €60.00. The credit reduces the tax, and it does not change the
gross pay.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A worker's gross pay is €600.00, and the PRSI rate is $4\%$. Work out the
PRSI.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

PRSI is a percentage of gross pay.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$600.00\times0.04$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$600.00\times0.04=24.00$$

The PRSI is €24.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** A worker's gross pay is €600.00, the income tax is €60.00, and the PRSI is
€24.00. Work out the net pay.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract both deductions from the gross pay.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$600.00-60.00-24.00$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$600.00-60.00-24.00=516.00$$

The net pay is €516.00. Check it: $516.00+60.00+24.00=600.00$, so the payslip
balances.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** A worker's gross pay is €1,000.00. Work out the income tax before the
credit, using $20\%$ on the first €800.00 and $40\%$ above it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Split the pay into €800.00 and €200.00.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$800.00\times0.20 \qquad 200.00\times0.40$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$800.00\times0.20=160.00 \qquad 200.00\times0.40=80.00$$

$$160.00+80.00=240.00$$

The income tax before the credit is €240.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** A worker's gross pay is €1,000.00. The income tax before the credit is
€240.00, the credit is €60.00, and the PRSI is $4\%$ of gross pay. Work out the net
pay.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the income tax, then the PRSI, then subtract both from the gross pay.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$240.00-60.00=180.00$$

$$1,000.00\times0.04=40.00$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The income tax is €180.00, and the PRSI is €40.00. The net pay is

$$1,000.00-180.00-40.00=780.00$$

which is €780.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** A worker's gross pay is €900.00. Work out the income tax before the credit,
using $20\%$ on the first €800.00 and $40\%$ above it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The amount above the cut-off is $900.00-800.00=100.00$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$800.00\times0.20 \qquad 100.00\times0.40$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$800.00\times0.20=160.00 \qquad 100.00\times0.40=40.00$$

$$160.00+40.00=200.00$$

The income tax before the credit is €200.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**9.** A worker's gross pay is €250.00. The income tax before the credit is
$250.00\times0.20=50.00$, and the tax credit is €60.00. Work out the income tax
and the net pay. The PRSI rate is $4\%$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The credit is larger than the tax before the credit. What happens to the tax?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The tax cannot go below zero, so the income tax is €0.00. Now work out
$250.00\times0.04$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The income tax is €0.00, because the €60.00 credit covers the €50.00 tax and an
unused credit is not paid out. The PRSI is $250.00\times0.04=10.00$, which is
€10.00. The net pay is

$$250.00-10.00=240.00$$

which is €240.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**10.** Increase €40.00 by $15\%$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Look back at Section 5.5. Multiply by $1.15$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$40.00\times1.15$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$40.00\times1.15=46.00$$

The new amount is €46.00. A percentage increase and a tax rate both multiply an
amount by a number slightly larger than 1.

</details>
</details>
</details>

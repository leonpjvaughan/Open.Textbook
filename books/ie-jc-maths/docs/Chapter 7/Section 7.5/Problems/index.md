# 7.5 Simple and compound interest — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Decide which kind of interest the question asks for. Simple interest is the same
every year. Compound interest grows, because the interest joins the balance.

Where a compound total is not a whole number of cent, round it at the end, and
only once.

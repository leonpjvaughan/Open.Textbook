# 7.5 Simple and compound interest — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $1.05\times1.05$.
2. Work out $2000\times0.05$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $1.1025$. This is $1.05^2$, the multiplier for two years at $5\%$ compound
   interest.
2. $100$. This is $5\%$ of €2,000.00, which is the first year's simple interest.

One number is the multiplier, and the other is the interest itself.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** €2,000.00 is saved at $5\%$ simple interest for 3 years. Work out the
interest.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Simple interest is $P\times r\times t$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2000\times0.05\times3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$2000\times0.05\times3=300$$

The interest is €300.00. It is the same €100.00 for each of the 3 years.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** €2,000.00 is saved at $5\%$ simple interest for 3 years. Work out the total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the interest to the principal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2000+300$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$2000+300=2300$$

The total is €2,300.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** €2,000.00 is saved at $5\%$ compound interest for 2 years. Work out the
total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Compound interest uses $A=P(1+r)^t$, so work out $1.05^2$ first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2000\times1.05^2$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1.05^2=1.1025$$

$$2000\times1.1025=2205$$

The total is €2,205.00. The first year adds €100.00, and the second year adds
€105.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** €500.00 is saved at $4\%$ simple interest for 2 years. Work out the
interest and the total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the interest, then add it to the principal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$500\times0.04\times2$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$500\times0.04\times2=40$$

The interest is €40.00, which is €20.00 for each year. The total is
$500+40=540$, which is €540.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** €500.00 is saved at $4\%$ compound interest for 2 years. Work out the
total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Work out $500\times1.04^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$500\times1.04^2$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1.04^2=1.0816$$

$$500\times1.0816=540.80$$

The total is €540.80. Compound interest gives 80 cent more than the simple total
of €540.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** €1,000.00 is saved at $3\%$ compound interest for 3 years. Work out the
total, correct to the nearest cent.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Work out $1.03^3$ first, then multiply.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$1.03^3=1.092727$$

$$1000\times1.092727$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1000\times1.092727=1092.727$$

The total is €1,092.727, which rounds to €1,092.73.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** €1,000.00 is saved at $3\%$ simple interest for 3 years. Work out the
interest and the total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The interest is the same €30.00 for each year.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$1000\times0.03\times3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1000\times0.03\times3=90$$

The interest is €90.00. The total is $1000+90=1090$, which is €1,090.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** €2,000.00 is saved at $5\%$ compound interest for 10 years. Work out the
total, correct to the nearest cent.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Work out $1.05^{10}$ first. Use the power key on a calculator.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$1.05^{10}=1.628894\ldots$$

$$2000\times1.628894\ldots$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$2000\times1.628894\ldots=3257.789\ldots$$

The total is €3,257.79, correct to the nearest cent.

Over 10 years at simple interest the total would be
$2000+2000\times0.05\times10=3000$ euro, which is €3,000.00. Compound interest
gives €257.79 more.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**9.** €1,200.00 is saved at $6\%$ simple interest for 1 year. Work out the
interest.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

One year means $t=1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$1200\times0.06\times1$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1200\times0.06\times1=72$$

The interest is €72.00. After one year, simple and compound interest give the same
€72.00, because there is no earlier interest for the second year to grow.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**10.** Simplify $2^3\times2^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Look back at Section 2.2. Add the indices when the bases are the same.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$2^{3+2}=2^5$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$2^3\times2^2=2^5=32$$

The indices add because the bases match. A power like $1.05^t$ in compound
interest uses the same notation.

</details>
</details>
</details>

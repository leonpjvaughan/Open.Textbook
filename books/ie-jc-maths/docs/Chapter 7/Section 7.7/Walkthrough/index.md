# 7.7 Currency conversion — Learn

**Prerequisites:** multiplying and dividing by a decimal, and rounding to two
decimal places from Section 4.1.

**Objectives:** change euro to another currency using an exchange rate; change
another currency back to euro; use the same rate in both directions; round money
to the nearest cent.

By the end of this section, you can move an amount from one currency to another,
and back again, without losing your place.

## The exchange rate

An **exchange rate** says what one currency is worth in another. The rate
€1 = 1.09 US dollars means that one euro buys 1.09 US dollars.

The rate is a ratio, so it can be used in both directions. The direction decides
whether you multiply or divide.

## From euro to another currency

To change euro to US dollars, multiply by the rate.

$$\text{US dollars} = \text{euro}\times1.09$$

**Example 1.** Change €200.00 to US dollars at €1 = 1.09 US dollars.

$$200.00\times1.09 = 218.00$$

€200.00 is 218.00 US dollars.

**Example 2.** Change €50.00 to US dollars at €1 = 1.09 US dollars.

$$50.00\times1.09 = 54.50$$

€50.00 is 54.50 US dollars.

**Example 3.** Change €80.00 to pounds at €1 = 0.85 pounds.

$$80.00\times0.85 = 68.00$$

€80.00 is £68.00.

## From another currency back to euro

To change US dollars to euro, divide by the rate.

$$\text{euro} = \frac{\text{US dollars}}{1.09}$$

**Example 4.** Change 218.00 US dollars to euro at €1 = 1.09 US dollars.

$$218.00\div1.09 = 200.00$$

218.00 US dollars is €200.00, which returns to where you started.

**Example 5.** Change 100.00 US dollars to euro at €1 = 1.09 US dollars, correct
to the nearest cent.

$$100.00\div1.09 = 91.7431\ldots$$

That rounds to €91.74.

**Example 6.** Change £68.00 to euro at €1 = 0.85 pounds.

$$68.00\div0.85 = 80.00$$

£68.00 is €80.00.

> **Watch out.** The direction decides whether you multiply or divide. To go from
> euro to another currency, multiply. To come back to euro, divide. Check with a
> rough number: at a rate near 1, the two amounts should be close. If €200.00
> becomes 218.00 US dollars, then 218.00 US dollars must come back to about
> €200.00.
>
> **Re-check:** convert the answer back with the other operation. Multiplying and
> then dividing by the same rate returns the amount you started with.

## A commission

A bank may charge a **commission**, which is a percentage taken for the exchange.
The commission is usually taken from the euro amount before the exchange.

**Example 7.** You change €200.00 at €1 = 1.09 US dollars, and the bank charges a
$2\%$ commission. Work out how many US dollars you receive.

The commission is $200.00\times0.02=4.00$. You exchange the rest.

$$200.00-4.00 = 196.00$$

$$196.00\times1.09 = 213.64$$

You receive 213.64 US dollars. Without the commission you would have received
218.00 US dollars, so the commission costs 4.36 US dollars.

## Progress check

Return to the chapter problem. The shop sells the phone case at a shelf price of
€7.38. An American website sells the same case at 8.00 US dollars.

1. Change 8.00 US dollars to euro at €1 = 1.09 US dollars, correct to the nearest
   cent.
2. Compare that with €7.38. Which is cheaper?
3. The website adds 2.50 US dollars for delivery. Change the delivery charge to
   euro, correct to the nearest cent.
4. Work out the total in euro, and say which offer is cheaper now.

The Review brings the payslip, the shop's pricing and these offers together.

## You should now be able to

- change euro to another currency by multiplying by the exchange rate;
- change another currency back to euro by dividing by the exchange rate;
- round a converted amount to the nearest cent;
- work out a commission as a percentage of the euro amount, and take it off
  before the exchange;
- check a conversion by converting the answer back.

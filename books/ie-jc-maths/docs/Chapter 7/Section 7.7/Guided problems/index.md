# 7.7 Currency conversion — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $200\times1.09$.
2. Round $91.7431$ to two decimal places.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $218$. Multiplying by a number a little over 1 makes the answer a little
   larger.
2. $91.74$. Look at the third decimal place, which is 3, so round down.

The first is leaving euro, and the second is writing money to the nearest cent.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Change €200.00 to US dollars at €1 = 1.09 US dollars.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

To leave euro, multiply by the rate.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$200.00\times1.09$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$200.00\times1.09=218.00$$

€200.00 is 218.00 US dollars.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Change €50.00 to US dollars at €1 = 1.09 US dollars.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply €50.00 by 1.09.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$50.00\times1.09$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$50.00\times1.09=54.50$$

€50.00 is 54.50 US dollars.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Change 100.00 US dollars to euro at €1 = 1.09 US dollars. Give the answer
correct to the nearest cent.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

To come back to euro, divide by the rate.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$100.00\div1.09$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$100.00\div1.09=91.7431\ldots$$

That rounds to €91.74.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Change 218.00 US dollars to euro at €1 = 1.09 US dollars.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by 1.09.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$218.00\div1.09$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$218.00\div1.09=200.00$$

218.00 US dollars is €200.00, which is where question 1 started.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Change €80.00 to pounds at €1 = 0.85 pounds.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply by the rate, which is less than 1 here.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$80.00\times0.85$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$80.00\times0.85=68.00$$

€80.00 is £68.00. The answer is smaller than the euro amount, because one euro
buys less than one pound.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Change £68.00 to euro at €1 = 0.85 pounds.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by 0.85.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$68.00\div0.85$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$68.00\div0.85=80.00$$

£68.00 is €80.00.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** A phone case costs 8.00 US dollars on an American website. Change it to euro
at €1 = 1.09 US dollars. Give the answer correct to the nearest cent.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the US dollar price by 1.09.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$8.00\div1.09$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$8.00\div1.09=7.3394\ldots$$

That rounds to €7.34.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** Change €150.00 to US dollars at €1 = 1.09 US dollars.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply €150.00 by 1.09.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$150.00\times1.09$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$150.00\times1.09=163.50$$

€150.00 is 163.50 US dollars.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**9.** You change €200.00 to US dollars at €1 = 1.09 US dollars, and the bank
charges a $2\%$ commission on the euro amount. Work out the commission, the amount
exchanged, and the US dollars you receive.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find $2\%$ of €200.00, take it off, then multiply by the rate.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$200.00\times0.02=4.00$$

$$200.00-4.00=196.00$$

$$196.00\times1.09$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The commission is €4.00, and the amount exchanged is €196.00. Then

$$196.00\times1.09=213.64$$

You receive 213.64 US dollars. Without the commission you would have received
218.00 US dollars, so the commission costs 4.36 US dollars.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**10.** Round $7.3394$ to two decimal places.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Look back at Section 4.1. Look at the third decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The third decimal place is 9, which is 5 or more, so round up.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$7.3394$ rounds to $7.34$. The digit after the second decimal place is 9, so the
second place goes up from 3 to 4.

This is the rounding used for every converted amount in this section.

</details>
</details>

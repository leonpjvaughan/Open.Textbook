# 7.7 Currency conversion — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Use the rate €1 = 1.09 US dollars unless a question gives another rate. To leave
euro, multiply. To come back to euro, divide.

Round every converted amount to the nearest cent.

# 7.3 VAT and bills — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write $23\%$ as a decimal.
2. Work out $100\div1.25$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $0.23$. Divide by 100.
2. $80$. Dividing by a number greater than 1 makes the answer smaller, which is
   what taking VAT off a price does.

A rate of VAT becomes a decimal before it multiplies a price.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A price is €6.00 before VAT. Work out the VAT at $23\%$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The VAT is $23\%$ of the price before VAT.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$6.00\times0.23$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$6.00\times0.23=1.38$$

The VAT is €1.38. The price before VAT is the base, not the shelf price.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A price is €6.00 before VAT. Work out the price with VAT at $23\%$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply by $1.23$ to add the VAT in one step.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$6.00\times1.23$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$6.00\times1.23=7.38$$

The price with VAT is €7.38. Check it: $6.00+1.38=7.38$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A shelf price is €24.60, including VAT at $23\%$. Work out the price before
VAT.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The shelf price is $123\%$ of the price before VAT, so divide by $1.23$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$24.60\div1.23$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$24.60\div1.23=20.00$$

The price before VAT is €20.00. Check it: $20.00\times1.23=24.60$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A price is €50.00 before VAT. Work out the VAT and the price with VAT at
$23\%$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find $23\%$ of €50.00 for the VAT, then add it on.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$50.00\times0.23 \qquad 50.00+11.50$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The VAT is $50.00\times0.23=11.50$, which is €11.50. The price with VAT is
$50.00+11.50=61.50$, which is €61.50.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** A receipt shows €73.80, including VAT at $23\%$. Work out the price before
VAT and the VAT.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by $1.23$ first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$73.80\div1.23=60.00$$

Now subtract to find the VAT.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The price before VAT is €60.00. The VAT is $73.80-60.00=13.80$, which is €13.80.

Check it: $23\%$ of €60.00 is €13.80.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** A phone bill is €30.00 before VAT. Work out the bill with VAT at $23\%$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply the bill by $1.23$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$30.00\times1.23$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$30.00\times1.23=36.90$$

The bill with VAT is €36.90. The VAT is €6.90.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** A repair bill has parts of €60.00 and labour of €40.00. VAT is added at
$23\%$. Work out the subtotal and the total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the two lines to get the subtotal, then add VAT to the subtotal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\text{subtotal} = 60.00+40.00 = 100.00$$

$$100.00\times1.23$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The subtotal is €100.00. The total is $100.00\times1.23=123.00$, which is €123.00.
The VAT is $100.00\times0.23=23.00$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** A shop's shelf price is €61.50, including VAT at $23\%$. Work out the price
before VAT.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the shelf price by $1.23$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$61.50\div1.23$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$61.50\div1.23=50.00$$

The price before VAT is €50.00. Check it: $50.00\times1.23=61.50$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**9.** A phone case has a shelf price of €7.38, including VAT at $23\%$. Work out
the price before VAT and the VAT.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by $1.23$ for the price before VAT, then subtract.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$7.38\div1.23=6.00$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The price before VAT is €6.00. The VAT is $7.38-6.00=1.38$, which is €1.38.

This is the price in the chapter problem, so the shelf price is confirmed.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**10.** Write $0.23$ as a percentage.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Look back at Section 5.4. Multiply the decimal by 100.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$0.23\times100$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$0.23\times100=23$$

So $0.23=23\%$. The VAT rate moves between a decimal and a percentage in exactly
this way.

</details>
</details>
</details>

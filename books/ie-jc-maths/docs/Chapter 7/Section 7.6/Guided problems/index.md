# 7.6 Value for money and comparing offers — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $24\div3$.
2. Which is smaller, $0.40$ or $0.38$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $8$. Dividing by a whole number gives the amount in each share.
2. $0.38$. Compare the tenths first: both are 3, so compare the hundredths, and 8
   is less than 0.

A unit price is a division, and comparing two unit prices is a decimal
comparison.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** A phone plan costs €20.00 for 10 GB. Work out the price of one GB.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the total price by the quantity.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$20.00\div10$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$20.00\div10=2.00$$

The price is €2.00 for each GB.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A phone plan costs €24.00 for 15 GB. Work out the price of one GB.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide €24.00 by 15.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$24.00\div15$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$24.00\div15=1.60$$

The price is €1.60 for each GB.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Plan A costs €20.00 for 10 GB, and Plan B costs €24.00 for 15 GB. Which
plan is better value for each GB?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the price of one GB for each plan, then compare.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$20.00\div10=2.00 \qquad 24.00\div15=1.60$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Plan B is better value. It costs €1.60 for each GB, and Plan A costs €2.00 for
each GB. Plan B has the larger total price, but each GB costs less.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A shop sells 500 g of coffee for €4.50. Work out the price of one kilogram.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A kilogram is two lots of 500 g.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$4.50\div0.5$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4.50\div0.5=9.00$$

The price is €9.00 for each kilogram. You can also double the price of the 500 g
bag: $2\times4.50=9.00$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** A shop sells 750 g of coffee for €6.00. Work out the price of one kilogram.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by the mass in kilograms, which is $0.75$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$6.00\div0.75$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$6.00\div0.75=8.00$$

The price is €8.00 for each kilogram.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Coffee costs €4.50 for 500 g in one bag, and €6.00 for 750 g in another.
Which bag is better value for each kilogram?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Compare €9.00 for each kilogram with €8.00 for each kilogram.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$4.50\div0.5=9.00 \qquad 6.00\div0.75=8.00$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The 750 g bag is better value. It costs €8.00 for each kilogram, and the 500 g
bag costs €9.00 for each kilogram.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** A shop sells three phone cases for €21.00. Work out the price of one case.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide €21.00 by 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$21.00\div3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$21.00\div3=7.00$$

The price is €7.00 for each case.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** A 2-litre bottle of juice costs €3.60, and a 1.5-litre bottle costs €2.85.
Work out the price of one litre for each, and say which is better value.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide each price by the number of litres.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$3.60\div2 \qquad 2.85\div1.5$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$3.60\div2=1.80 \qquad 2.85\div1.5=1.90$$

The 2-litre bottle is better value, because it costs €1.80 for each litre and the
1.5-litre bottle costs €1.90 for each litre.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**9.** A pack of 12 pens costs €4.80, and a pack of 20 pens costs €7.60. Work out
the price of one pen for each pack, and say which pack is better value.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide each price by the number of pens.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$4.80\div12 \qquad 7.60\div20$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4.80\div12=0.40 \qquad 7.60\div20=0.38$$

The 20-pack is better value. It costs 38 cent for each pen, and the 12-pack costs
40 cent for each pen.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**10.** Five kilograms of rice cost €8.00. Find the cost of twelve kilograms.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Look back at Section 6.3. Find the cost of one kilogram first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$8.00\div5=1.60$$

Now multiply by 12.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

One kilogram costs $8.00\div5=1.60$, which is €1.60. Twelve kilograms cost
$1.60\times12=19.20$, which is €19.20.

The unit price is the same idea as value for money, used to reach a new total.

</details>
</details>
</details>

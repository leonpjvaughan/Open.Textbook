# Chapter 18: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

**How tall is the school building?**

You stand 12 m from the foot of the building. The distance in a straight line to
the top is 15 m, and the angle of elevation is 37°.

**Method 1: Pythagoras' theorem.** The 15 m line is the hypotenuse, and the height
is $h$.

$$h^2 = 15^2-12^2 = 225-144 = 81$$

$$h = \sqrt{81} = 9$$

**Method 2: the tangent ratio.** The height is opposite the 37° angle, and 12 m
is adjacent to it.

$$\tan 37^\circ = \frac{h}{12}$$

$$h = 12\tan 37^\circ = 12\times0.7536\ldots = 9.04\ldots$$

**The building is about 9 m tall.** The two methods agree, and they should: both
use the same right-angled triangle. The small difference, 9.00 m against 9.04 m,
comes from rounding the measured angle of 37°.

The second acute angle of the triangle is the angle at the top. Subtract the
elevation from 90°.

$$90^\circ - 36.9^\circ = 53.1^\circ$$

The two acute angles are 36.9° and 53.1°, and they add to 90°. Every section of
this chapter met this one triangle: the theorem for the sides, the ratios for the
angle, the inverse ratios for the check, and the name for the angle above you.

## Mixed practice — choose the method first

Work through these without notes. For each one, decide **which idea applies** and
say why in a few words. The idea might be Pythagoras' theorem, a ratio, an inverse
ratio, an area, a volume, a percentage, a ratio or an equation.

1. A right-angled triangle has legs of 8 cm and 15 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
2. A right-angled triangle has an angle of 30° and a hypotenuse of 12 m. Find the
   opposite side.
3. A right-angled triangle has an opposite side of 5 m and an adjacent side of
   12 m. Find the angle, to one decimal place.
4. A ladder is 10 m long and its foot is 6 m from a wall. How far up the wall does
   it reach?
5. From a point 30 m from a tower, the angle of elevation to the top is 28°. Find
   the height of the tower, to one decimal place.
6. A rectangle is 8 m by 6 m. Find the length of a diagonal.
7. Work out 35% of 240.
8. Solve $4x+7=31$.
9. A circle has a radius of 7 cm. Find its area in terms of $\pi$.
10. A right-angled triangle has an opposite side of 9 cm and a hypotenuse of
    15 cm. Find the angle, to one decimal place.
11. From the top of a cliff 60 m high, the angle of depression to a boat is 20°.
    Find the horizontal distance to the boat, to one decimal place.
12. Divide €84 in the ratio $3:4$.
13. A cuboid is 6 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm. Find its volume.
14. A right-angled triangle has a hypotenuse of 25 m and one leg of 24 m. Find the
    other leg.
15. A ramp rises at an angle of 15° and is 5 m long. Find the height it rises, to
    one decimal place.
16. A right-angled triangle has legs of 3 m and 4 m. Find the smaller acute angle,
    to one decimal place.

<summary>Answers and concise solutions</summary>

1. $17$ cm. $8^2+15^2=64+225=289$, and $\sqrt{289}=17$.
2. $6$ m. $12\sin 30^\circ=12\times0.5=6$.
3. $22.6$°. $\tan\theta=\frac{5}{12}=0.4166\ldots$, and
   $\tan^{-1}(0.4166\ldots)=22.619\ldots$°.
4. $8$ m. The ladder is the hypotenuse: $10^2-6^2=100-36=64$, and $\sqrt{64}=8$.
5. $16.0$ m. $30\tan 28^\circ=30\times0.5317\ldots=15.951\ldots$, which is $16.0$ m
   to one decimal place.
6. $10$ m. The diagonal is the hypotenuse: $8^2+6^2=64+36=100$, and
   $\sqrt{100}=10$.
7. $84$. Ten per cent of 240 is 24, so 30% is 72 and 5% is 12. Add them:
   $72+12=84$.
8. $x=6$. Subtract 7 from both sides to get $4x=24$, then divide by 4.
9. $49\pi$ cm². $A=\pi r^2=\pi\times7^2=49\pi$.
10. $36.9$°. $\sin\theta=\frac{9}{15}=0.6$, and $\sin^{-1}(0.6)=36.869\ldots$°.
11. $164.8$ m. $d=\frac{60}{\tan 20^\circ}=\frac{60}{0.3639\ldots}=164.848\ldots$,
    which is $164.8$ m to one decimal place.
12. €36 and €48. There are $3+4=7$ parts, and $84\div7=12$. Then $3\times12=36$
    and $4\times12=48$.
13. $72$ cm³. $6\times4\times3=72$.
14. $7$ m. $25^2-24^2=625-576=49$, and $\sqrt{49}=7$.
15. $1.3$ m. $5\sin 15^\circ=5\times0.2588\ldots=1.294\ldots$, which is $1.3$ m to
    one decimal place.
16. $36.9$°. $\tan\theta=\frac{3}{4}=0.75$, and $\tan^{-1}(0.75)=36.869\ldots$°.

## What you can now do

- state Pythagoras' theorem and use it to find a missing side of a right-angled
  triangle;
- label the opposite, the adjacent and the hypotenuse for a chosen angle;
- write the sine, cosine and tangent ratios, and use them to find a missing side;
- use the inverse ratios to find a missing angle;
- draw and solve a triangle for an angle of elevation or depression;
- choose between Pythagoras' theorem and a trigonometric ratio, and say why.

## Thinking back

At the start of the chapter you wrote down one place where someone needs to work
out a distance they cannot measure directly. Look back at what you wrote.

Take that example now. Decide which of the two methods it needs, and say why in a
sentence. If the situation gives two lengths, use Pythagoras. If it gives a length
and an angle, use a ratio. Then write the equation you would solve, even if you do
not know the numbers.

That decision is the real skill of this chapter. The arithmetic is short, and the
choice of method is what tells you which arithmetic to do.

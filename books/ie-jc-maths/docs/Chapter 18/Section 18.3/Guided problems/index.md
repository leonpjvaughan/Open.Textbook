# 18.3 The trigonometric ratios — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $6\div10$ as a decimal.
2. Work out $8\div10$ as a decimal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $0.6$. Dividing by 10 moves the digits one place to the right.
2. $0.8$. The same rule gives a decimal in one step.

Each ratio in this section is one side divided by another, so these divisions are
the whole calculation.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** A right-angled triangle has an opposite side of 6 cm, an adjacent side of
8 cm and a hypotenuse of 10 cm. Write down $\sin\theta$, $\cos\theta$ and
$\tan\theta$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use SOH CAH TOA. Sine is opposite over hypotenuse.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\sin\theta=\frac{6}{10} \qquad \cos\theta=\frac{8}{10} \qquad
\tan\theta=\frac{6}{8}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\sin\theta=\frac{3}{5} \qquad \cos\theta=\frac{4}{5} \qquad
\tan\theta=\frac{3}{4}$$

Each fraction can be simplified by dividing the top and the bottom by the same
number.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A right-angled triangle has an opposite side of 5 cm and a hypotenuse of
13 cm. Write down $\sin\theta$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Sine is the opposite side over the hypotenuse.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$\sin\theta=\frac{5}{13}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\sin\theta=\frac{5}{13}$. The fraction does not simplify, because 5 and 13 have no
common factor other than 1.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A right-angled triangle has an adjacent side of 12 cm and a hypotenuse of
13 cm. Write down $\cos\theta$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Cosine is the adjacent side over the hypotenuse.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$\cos\theta=\frac{12}{13}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\cos\theta=\frac{12}{13}$. The side next to the angle is 12 cm, and the long side
is 13 cm.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A right-angled triangle has an opposite side of 9 cm and an adjacent side
of 12 cm. Write down $\tan\theta$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Tangent is the opposite side over the adjacent side.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$\tan\theta=\frac{9}{12}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\tan\theta=\frac{9}{12}=\frac{3}{4}$. Divide the top and the bottom by 3.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Use a calculator to find $\sin 30^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Check that the calculator is in degree mode first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Press sin, then 30, then equals.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\sin 30^\circ = 0.5$. This is an exact value, not a rounded one.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Use a calculator to find $\cos 60^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Degree mode, then the cos key.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Press cos, then 60, then equals.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\cos 60^\circ = 0.5$. Notice that $\sin 30^\circ$ and $\cos 60^\circ$ are equal,
because 30° and 60° are the two acute angles of the same triangle.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Use a calculator to find $\tan 45^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A 45° angle sits in an isosceles right-angled triangle, where the opposite and
adjacent sides are equal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Press tan, then 45, then equals.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\tan 45^\circ = 1$. The opposite and adjacent sides are equal, so their ratio is 1.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** The chapter problem. The angle of elevation to the top of the building is
37°. The height is opposite that angle, and the 12 m along the ground is adjacent
to it. Which ratio uses those two sides?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Look for the ratio that mentions the opposite and the adjacent sides.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Tangent is opposite over adjacent.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Tangent. $\tan 37^\circ=\frac{\text{height}}{12}$, so the height is
$12\tan 37^\circ$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Solve $5x=35$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The 5 multiplies $x$. Undo it with division.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Divide both sides by 5.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$x=7$. Check it in the original equation: $5\times7=35$.

Rearranging comes back in Section 18.4, where the unknown sits inside a ratio.

</details>
</details>

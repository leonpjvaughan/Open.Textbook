# Chapter 18: Pythagoras and trigonometry

Pythagoras' theorem, the trigonometric ratios, and solving right-angled triangles.

## The chapter problem

**How tall is the school building?.** You cannot climb it, so you will measure it from the ground — using an angle and a distance. By the end of the chapter you can choose between Pythagoras and a ratio, and say why you chose it.

**Where it is used:** Surveying, construction, navigation, and any height or distance you cannot reach.

**Your turn to connect it:** Where would being able to work out an unmeasurable distance be useful?

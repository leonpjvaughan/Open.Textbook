# 18.6 Angles of elevation and depression — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $\tan 30^\circ$, correct to three decimal places.
2. Work out $20\times0.577$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $0.577$. The calculator shows $0.5773\ldots$, and the fourth decimal digit is 3,
   so the third digit stays.
2. $11.54$. Work out $20\times577=11540$, then move the decimal point.

These two steps are one elevation problem: find the ratio, then multiply by the
distance.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** A person stands 20 m from a tree. The angle of elevation to the top is 30°.
Find the height of the tree, to one decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The height is opposite the 30° angle, and 20 m is adjacent to it. Use tangent.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$h = 20\tan 30^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$h = 20\times0.5773\ldots = 11.547\ldots$$

The tree is about 11.5 m tall.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** From the top of a cliff 40 m above the sea, the angle of depression to a boat
is 25°. Find the horizontal distance to the boat, to one decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The height is opposite the 25° angle, and the distance is adjacent to it. The
unknown is on the bottom of the fraction.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$\tan 25^\circ=\frac{40}{d}$, so $d=\frac{40}{\tan 25^\circ}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$d = \frac{40}{0.4663\ldots} = 85.78\ldots$$

The boat is about 85.8 m from the cliff.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A person stands 15 m from a tower. The angle of elevation to the top is 40°.
Find the height of the tower, to one decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use tangent with the adjacent side 15 m.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$h = 15\tan 40^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$h = 15\times0.8390\ldots = 12.586\ldots$$

The tower is about 12.6 m tall.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** From the top of a tower 12 m high, the angle of depression to a point on the
ground is 35°. Find the horizontal distance, to one decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The height is opposite the angle, and the distance is adjacent to it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$d=\frac{12}{\tan 35^\circ}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$d = \frac{12}{0.7002\ldots} = 17.137\ldots$$

The point is about 17.1 m from the tower.

</details>
</details>
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$h = 50\tan 22^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

**6.** A kite is flying on a string 30 m long. The string makes an angle of 60°
with the ground. Find the height of the kite, to one decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The string is the hypotenuse, so use sine.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$h = 30\sin 60^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$h = 30\times0.8660\ldots = 25.980\ldots$$

The kite is about 26.0 m above the ground.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** A flagpole stands on level ground. From a point 8 m away, the angle of
elevation to the top is 55°. Find the height of the flagpole, to one decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use tangent with the adjacent side 8 m.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$h = 8\tan 55^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$h = 8\times1.4281\ldots = 11.425\ldots$$

The flagpole is about 11.4 m tall.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** The chapter problem. You stand on the ground and look up to the top of the
school building. What is the name for the angle between the horizontal ground and
your line of sight?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The object is above you.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

An angle measured up from the horizontal is an angle of elevation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

It is an angle of elevation, because the top of the building is above your eye
level. An angle down to something below you is an angle of depression.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Round 11.547 to one decimal place.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Look at the second decimal digit.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The digit after the first decimal is 4, which is less than 5.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$11.5$. The hundredths digit is 4, so the tenths digit stays at 5.

Elevation and depression answers are often decimals, so rounding is part of the
work.

</details>
</details>

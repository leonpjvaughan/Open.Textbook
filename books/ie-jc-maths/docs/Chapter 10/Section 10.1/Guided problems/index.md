# 10.1 Generating and interpreting expressions — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $4 + 3 \times 5$.
2. A pattern starts $3, 7, 11, 15$. What is added each time, and what is the next
   term?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $19$. Multiplication goes first: $3\times5=15$, and then $4+15=19$.
2. Each term is 4 more than the one before, so the next term is $19$. A pattern
   with a constant step gives an expression of the form $an+b$.

Both skills appear in this section. The order of operations decides the value of
an expression, and the step of a pattern is the coefficient of the letter.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** A bag of apples costs €3. Write an expression for the cost of $b$ bags.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Each bag costs the same amount. Which operation repeats the €3 for every bag?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The cost is €3 multiplied by the number of bags.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$3b$$

The letter $b$ stands for the number of bags. With $b=4$ the cost is
$3\times4=12$ euro.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Write an expression for a number $n$ with 6 added to it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The words say that 6 is added. Which operation adds?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Start with the number $n$ and add 6.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$n+6$$

With $n=10$ the value is $10+6=16$. Adding 6 to a number gives an answer 6 more
than the number.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A number $y$ is multiplied by 5, and then 2 is subtracted. Write an
expression.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Two operations happen in order. The multiplication happens first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Multiplying $y$ by 5 gives $5y$. Then subtract 2.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$5y-2$$

With $y=3$ the value is $5\times3-2=15-2=13$. The subtraction comes after the
multiplication, so the $-2$ sits outside the $5y$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A taxi charges €4 to start and €2 for each kilometre. Write an expression
for the fare for $k$ kilometres.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

One of the charges happens once. The other happens for every kilometre.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The €4 is the constant, and the €2 multiplies $k$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4+2k$$

With $k=10$ the fare is $4+2\times10=4+20=24$ euro. The €4 does not change with
the distance, and the $2k$ does.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Write an expression for the perimeter of a square with side $s$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

How many sides does a square have, and how long is each one?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The perimeter is the side added four times.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4s$$

Adding $s$ four times is the same as multiplying it by 4. With $s=6$ cm the
perimeter is $4\times6=24$ cm.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** In the expression $7n+4$, name the coefficient of $n$ and the constant.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The coefficient is the number multiplying the letter. The constant is the term
with no letter.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Look at $7n$ first, and then at the $4$ on its own.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The coefficient of $n$ is $7$, because $7n$ means $7\times n$.

The constant is $4$, because it is the term with no letter in it.

With $n=2$ the value is $7\times2+4=14+4=18$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** A pattern of tiles uses 5 tiles in figure 1, 8 tiles in figure 2, and 11
tiles in figure 3. Write an expression for the number of tiles in figure $n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the step first. That step is the coefficient of $n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Each figure adds 3 tiles, so the expression starts with $3n$. What must you add
to $3n$ to get 5 when $n=1$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$3n+2$$

With $n=1$ the value is $3\times1+2=5$, which matches figure 1. Check figure 3:
$3\times3+2=11$, which matches.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** Write an expression for each of these: 5 less than a number $n$, and half
of a number $n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

"Less than" reverses the order. "Half of" means divided by 2.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Start with $n$ and take 5 away for the first one. Divide $n$ by 2 for the second.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

5 less than $n$ is $n-5$. With $n=12$ the value is $12-5=7$.

Half of $n$ is $\frac{n}{2}$. With $n=12$ the value is $6$.

The order matters in the first one: $5-n$ would give $-7$ when $n=12$, which is a
different value.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** A pattern has general term $4n+1$. Find the 10th term.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The general term turns the figure number into the value. Put $n=10$ into it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$4\times10+1$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4\times10+1=40+1=41$$

The 10th term is 41. Multiply before you add, so the $4n$ is worked out first.

</details>
</details>
</details>

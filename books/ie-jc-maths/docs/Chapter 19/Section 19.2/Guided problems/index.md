# 19.2 Axial symmetry and reflection — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Translate the point $(2,1)$ by the vector $\begin{pmatrix}3\\2\end{pmatrix}$.
2. Find the vector that maps $(1,1)$ to $(4,5)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $(5,3)$. Add the vector to the coordinates: $2+3=5$ and $1+2=3$.
2. $\begin{pmatrix}3\\4\end{pmatrix}$. Subtract the start from the finish:
   $4-1=3$ and $5-1=4$.

A reflection also moves points, but it flips them over a line rather than sliding
them. The next questions use the same coordinate skills.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Reflect the point $(2,4)$ in the $x$-axis.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The $x$-axis is the mirror line, so the $y$-coordinate changes sign.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The image is $(2,-4)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(2,4)\to(2,-4)$$

The point is 4 units above the axis, and the image is 4 units below. The
$x$-coordinate stays the same.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Reflect the point $(-3,1)$ in the $y$-axis.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The $y$-axis is the mirror line, so the $x$-coordinate changes sign.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The image is $(3,1)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(-3,1)\to(3,1)$$

The point is 3 units left of the axis, and the image is 3 units right. The
$y$-coordinate stays the same.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Reflect the point $(1,5)$ in the line $y=x$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The line $y=x$ runs at 45°, so the coordinates swap.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Swap 1 and 5.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(1,5)\to(5,1)$$

The image is $(5,1)$. The point and its image are the same distance from the
line $y=x$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Reflect the point $(5,2)$ in the line $x=3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The point is 2 units right of the line, so the image is 2 units left.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The image is $(2\times3-5,\ 2)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(5,2)\to(2\times3-5,\ 2)=(1,2)$$

The image is $(1,2)$. The $y$-coordinate does not change, because the mirror line
is vertical.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Reflect the point $(2,5)$ in the line $y=1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The point is 4 units above the line, so the image is 4 units below.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The image is $(2,\ 2\times1-5)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(2,5)\to(2,\ 2\times1-5)=(2,-3)$$

The image is $(2,-3)$. The $x$-coordinate does not change, because the mirror
line is horizontal.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** A triangle has vertices $(1,2)$, $(3,2)$ and $(1,4)$. Reflect the triangle
in the $x$-axis and write down the image vertices.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Each vertex changes the sign of its $y$-coordinate.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$(1,2)\to(1,-2)$ and $(3,2)\to(3,-2)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(1,2)\to(1,-2)\qquad(3,2)\to(3,-2)\qquad(1,4)\to(1,-4)$$

The image vertices are $(1,-2)$, $(3,-2)$ and $(1,-4)$. The image is congruent to
the object, but it hangs below the $x$-axis.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** The chapter problem. The crest has a shaded triangle with vertices
$M(2,2)$, $A(4,0)$ and $B(4,4)$. Reflect the triangle in the line $x=2$ and write
down the image vertices.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The point $M$ lies on the mirror line, so it does not move.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$A(4,0)$ is 2 units right of the line, so its image is $(0,0)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$M(2,2)\to(2,2)\qquad A(4,0)\to(0,0)\qquad B(4,4)\to(0,4)$$

The image vertices are $(2,2)$, $(0,0)$ and $(0,4)$. This is the left shaded
triangle $MCO$, so the line $x=2$ is an axis of symmetry of the crest.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** A student reflects the point $(4,1)$ in the line $x=2$ and writes the
image as $(-4,1)$. Explain the error and give the correct image.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The image is the same distance from the line as the point, on the other side.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The point is 2 units right of the line $x=2$, so the image is 2 units left.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The student changed the sign of the $x$-coordinate, which is a reflection in the
$y$-axis. The line $x=2$ is the mirror line here. The correct image is $(0,1)$,
because the point is 2 units from the line and the image is 2 units on the other
side: $2\times2-4=0$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Translate the point $(0,3)$ by the vector
$\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\end{pmatrix}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the vector to the coordinates.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The image is $(0-2,\ 3+1)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(0,3)\to(0-2,\ 3+1)=(-2,4)$$

The image is $(-2,4)$. The move is 2 left and 1 up. A translation and a
reflection both give a congruent image, but only the reflection reverses the
orientation.

</details>
</details>
</details>

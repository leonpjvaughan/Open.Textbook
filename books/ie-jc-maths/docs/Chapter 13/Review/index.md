# Chapter 13: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

**The planter box.** The box must hold 0.6 m³ of soil, is 1.5 m long and 0.4 m tall,
and has no lid. Find the width it needs and the area of board for its four sides and
its base.

**Step 1. The width.** Start with the volume formula.

$$V = lwh$$

The $w$ is multiplied by $l$ and by $h$, so divide both sides by $lh$.

$$w = \frac{V}{lh}$$

Substitute the three numbers you know.

$$w = \frac{0.6}{1.5\times0.4} = \frac{0.6}{0.6} = 1$$

**The box is 1 m wide.**

**Step 2. Check the volume.** Put the width back into the volume formula.

$$V = 1.5\times1\times0.4 = 0.6$$

That is the volume the club needs, so the width is right. Since $1\ \text{m}^3=1000$
litres, the box holds 600 litres of soil.

**Step 3. The board.** The formula for the four sides and the base is

$$A = 2h(l+w)+lw$$

Substitute $l=1.5$, $w=1$ and $h=0.4$. Work out the bracket first.

$$A = 2\times0.4\times(1.5+1)+1.5\times1 = 0.8\times2.5+1.5$$

$$A = 2+1.5 = 3.5$$

**The box needs $3.5\ \text{m}^2$ of board.**

**Step 4. Check the two parts of the board.** The four sides have area
$2h(l+w)=2\times0.4\times2.5=2\ \text{m}^2$. The base has area $lw=1.5\ \text{m}^2$.
Together they give $2+1.5=3.5\ \text{m}^2$, which agrees with Step 3.

The box is 1 m wide, holds 600 litres of soil, and needs $3.5\ \text{m}^2$ of board.
Each step used one of the three sections: substituting, changing the subject, and
working with a bracket.

## Mixed practice — choose the method first

Work through these without notes. For each one, decide **which idea applies** and say
why in a few words. The idea might be the order of operations, an index, expanding a
bracket, solving an equation, substituting, or changing the subject.

1. Work out $2+3\times4$.
2. Work out $(-2)^3$.
3. Expand $4(x-3)$.
4. Solve $5x+2=17$.
5. Find $y$ when $x=5$, using $y=x^2-2x$.
6. Make $x$ the subject of $y=x-8$.
7. Make $w$ the subject of $A=lw$.
8. Make $t$ the subject of $s=\dfrac{d}{t}$.
9. Make $x$ the subject of $y=5(x-2)$.
10. Make $b$ the subject of $m=\dfrac{a+b}{2}$.
11. The volume of a cuboid is $V=lwh$. Find $h$ when $V=36\ \text{cm}^3$, $l=4$ cm and
    $w=3$ cm.
12. A phone plan costs $C=20+0.05m$ for $m$ minutes. Make $m$ the subject, and then
    find the number of minutes when $C=35$.

## What you can now do

- read a formula and say what each letter stands for and which letter is the subject;
- substitute numbers into a formula, including negative numbers, decimals and indices;
- work out the value with the order of operations and brackets;
- change the subject of a formula by doing the inverse operation to both sides;
- change the subject of a formula where the letter is inside a bracket or under a
  fraction bar;
- clear a fraction by multiplying both sides by the denominator;
- check a rearrangement by substituting numbers into it.

## Thinking back

At the start of the chapter you predicted whether the planter box would be wider or
narrower than 1 m. Look back at that prediction now.

The box is exactly 1 m wide. The volume formula gave it directly once you made $w$ the
subject, and the check confirmed it. You also wrote down a rule from your own week and
the letter you would like to find.

Take that rule now. Write it with letters, name the subject, and change the subject to
the letter you wanted. Then substitute some real numbers and check that the new form
gives the answer you expected. That is the whole of this chapter in one piece of work.

# Chapter 20: Synthetic geometry, constructions and proof

The axioms, theorems and corollaries of the shared geometry course, the constructions that go with them, and what a proof is.

## The chapter problem

**Why do the angles of a triangle add to 180°?.** You have used this fact for years. You will prove it, from the axioms and the theorems that come before it, and by the end of the chapter you can say what a proof is and what it is not.

**Where it is used:** Construction, design, and the reasoning behind every geometrical fact you use.

**Your turn to connect it:** Which geometrical fact do you use most often without being able to say why it is true?

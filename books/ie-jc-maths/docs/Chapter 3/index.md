# Chapter 3: Factors, multiples and primes

Some numbers break into smaller numbers and some do not. In this chapter you learn
how to take a number apart, and what the pieces are good for.

## The chapter problem

**The two lighthouses.** Two lighthouses flash regularly — one every 12 seconds, the
other every 18 seconds. You see them flash together. How long until they do it again?

You can answer that by the end of the chapter, and you will see why it is the same
kind of question as sharing 48 blue pens and 60 black pens into boxes with nothing
left over. One question wants the *lowest common multiple*; the other wants the
*highest common factor*. Telling them apart is most of the work.

## By the end of this chapter you can

- list the factors and multiples of a number, and test whether one number divides
  another using the divisibility rules;
- write a number as a product of its prime factors;
- find the highest common factor and the lowest common multiple of two or more
  numbers, and say which one a problem is asking for.

## Where it is used

Packing and sharing without waste, timetables and repeating events, and simplifying
fractions — which is the next chapter.

# 4.2 Estimating and checking a result — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Every estimate is checked here by the exact value it stands for. The estimate and
the exact value must agree on the size of the answer. A question that says
**estimate** wants the rounded calculation, written down as working.

# 9.2 The general term of a linear pattern — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $5\times9$.
2. Continue the pattern $2, 4, 6, 8, \ldots$ by one term.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $45$.
2. $10$. The pattern adds 2 each time.

Multiplying lets you jump along a pattern, and finding the change is the first step in
writing a rule.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find the common difference of the pattern $8, 13, 18, 23, \ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract one term from the next.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$13-8=5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The common difference is $5$. Check it again: $18-13=5$ and $23-18=5$. The same answer
every time means the pattern is linear.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Find the general term of $6, 10, 14, 18, \ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The common difference is the number that multiplies $n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The common difference is 4, so start with $4n$. The $4n$ pattern is $4, 8, 12, 16$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Each term is 2 more than the matching term of $4n$, so the general term is $4n+2$.

Check position 1: $4(1)+2=6$. Check position 3: $4(3)+2=14$. Both match.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Find the general term of $3, 10, 17, 24, \ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Work out the common difference first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$10-3=7$, so the general term starts with $7n$. The $7n$ pattern is $7, 14, 21, 28$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Each term is 4 less than the matching term of $7n$, so the general term is $7n-4$.

Check position 1: $7(1)-4=3$. Check position 2: $7(2)-4=10$. Both match.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Find the general term of the decreasing pattern $20, 16, 12, 8, \ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The pattern goes down by 4, so the common difference is $-4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Start with $-4n$. At position 1, $-4(1)=-4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

You need 24 more to reach the first term, 20, so the general term is $-4n+24$.

Check position 1: $-4(1)+24=20$. Check position 3: $-4(3)+24=12$. Both match.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** A pattern has general term $4n+3$. Work out the 15th term.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Put $n=15$ into the rule.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$4(15)+3$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$60+3=63$$

The 15th term is $63$. You do not need to list the first 14 terms.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Which term of the pattern with general term $7n-1$ is $62$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Set the rule equal to 62.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$7n-1=62$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$7n=63 \qquad n=9$$

The 9th term is $62$. Check it: $7(9)-1=63-1=62$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Aoife's plan has general term $3n$ euro. How much does she save in week 15?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The week number is the position.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$3(15)$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$45$$

She saves €45 in week 15. Her saving grows by €3 a week, so after 15 weeks the weekly
amount is $15\times3=45$ euro.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** A pattern starts at 5 and adds 8 each time. Find its general term.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The common difference is 8, so start with $8n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The $8n$ pattern is $8, 16, 24, \ldots$, and each term here is 3 less.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The general term is $8n-3$.

Check position 1: $8(1)-3=5$. Check position 2: $8(2)-3=13$, which is $5+8$. Both
match.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from an earlier section

**9.** Work out $12\times7$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use $12\times7 = 10\times7 + 2\times7$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$70+14$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$84$$

Multiplying by the common difference is the fastest way to move along a linear
pattern, so this skill is worth keeping sharp.

</details>
</details>
</details>

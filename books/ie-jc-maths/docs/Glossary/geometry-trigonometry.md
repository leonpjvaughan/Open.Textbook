# Glossary — Geometry and Trigonometry

**12-hour clock** — a way of telling time that uses a.m. and p.m.

**24-hour clock** — a way of telling time that counts the hours from 00:00 to
23:59.

**Area** — the amount of surface a flat shape covers, measured in square units.

**Capacity** — the amount a container can hold, measured in litres and
millilitres.

**Conversion** — changing a measurement from one unit to another.

**Gram** — a unit of mass; $1000$ grams make one kilogram.

**Kilogram** — a unit of mass equal to $1000$ grams.

**Kilometre** — a unit of length equal to $1000$ metres.

**Length** — the distance from one end of an object to the other.

**Litre** — a unit of capacity equal to $1000$ millilitres.

**Mass** — the amount of matter in an object, measured in grams and kilograms.

**Metre** — the base unit of length.

**Millilitre** — a unit of capacity; $1000$ millilitres make one litre.

**Millimetre** — a unit of length; $1000$ millimetres make one metre.

**Time interval** — the time between two events.

**Timetable** — a table that lists the times at which events happen.

**Time zone** — a region of the world that keeps the same time.

**Tonne** — a unit of mass equal to $1000$ kilograms.

**Volume** — the amount of space a solid occupies, measured in cubic units.

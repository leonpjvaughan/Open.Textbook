# 19.1 Perimeter and area of 2D shapes — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Try these closed-book before checking.

1. Work out $7\times8$.
2. Convert 2 m to centimetres.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Check your answers</summary>

1. $56$. 2. $200$ cm.
</details>

## Practise with help

Attempt each question first. Open the hint, then the next step, when needed.

**1.** Work out the perimeter of a rectangle with length 8 cm and width 3 cm.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Perimeter is the total distance around the rectangle. It has two sides of each length.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Use $P=2l+2w$ and substitute $l=8$, $w=3$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$P=2\times8+2\times3=16+6=22\text{ cm}$.
</details></details></details>

**2.** Work out the area of a rectangle measuring 7 cm by 5 cm.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Area is length multiplied by width.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Use $A=lw$ with $l=7$ cm and $w=5$ cm.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$A=7\times5=35\text{ cm}^2$.
</details></details></details>

**3.** Work out the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and perpendicular height 6 cm.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Triangle area is half the base multiplied by its perpendicular height.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Use $A=\frac12bh$ with $b=10$ cm and $h=6$ cm.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$A=\frac12\times10\times6=30\text{ cm}^2$.
</details></details></details>

Try the remaining questions without opening the full working.

# 19.1 Perimeter and area of 2D shapes — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Try these closed-book before checking.

1. Work out $7\times8$.
2. Convert 2 m to centimetres.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Check your answers</summary>

1. $56$. 2. $200$ cm.
</details>

## Practise with help

Attempt each question first. Open the hint, then the next step, when needed.

**1.** A rectangular flower bed is 11 m long and 6 m wide. Work out its perimeter.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Perimeter is the distance around the edge. This rectangle has two sides of 11 m and two sides of 6 m.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Add one length and one width, then double the total: $2(11+6)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$P=2\times11+2\times6=22+12=34\text{ m}$.
</details></details></details>

**2.** A parallelogram has base 12 cm and perpendicular height 5 cm. Work out its area.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Use the base and the perpendicular height. The sloping side is not the height.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Multiply the base by the perpendicular height: $12\times5$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$A=12\times5=60\text{ cm}^2$.
</details></details></details>

**3.** A trapezium has parallel sides 9 cm and 15 cm, and perpendicular height 6 cm. Work out its area.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

A trapezium's area is the average of its parallel sides multiplied by the perpendicular height.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Average the parallel sides first: $(9+15)\div2=12$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$A=\frac{9+15}{2}\times6=12\times6=72\text{ cm}^2$.
</details></details></details>

Try the remaining questions without opening the full working.

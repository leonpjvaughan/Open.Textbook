# 6.5 Growth, decay and repeated percentage change — Practise with help

Try each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Practise with help

**1.** A quantity falls by 8%. Give its multiplier.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the percentage remaining.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

100% − 8% = 92%, so multiplier 0.92.

</details>

**2.** Find 500 × 1.04³.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use repeated multiplication or a calculator.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

500 × 1.04³ = 562.432, about 562.43.

</details>

**3.** A £700 value decays by 15% yearly for two years.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Choose a multiplier and square it.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

700 × 0.85² = £505.75.

</details>

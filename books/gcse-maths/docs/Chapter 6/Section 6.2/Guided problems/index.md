# 6.2 Percentage change — Practise with help

Try each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out 20% of 80.
2. Write 7% as a decimal multiplier for an increase.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check your answers</summary>

1. 16. 2. 1.07.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Increase 240 by 12%.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the increase multiplier.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

1.12 × 240 = 268.8.

</details>

**2.** A value falls from 80 to 68. Find the percentage decrease.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the change, then divide by the start.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Change 12; 12 ÷ 80 × 100 = 15%.

</details>

**3.** A £90 bill rises by 4%. Find the new bill.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the multiplier 1.04.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

£90 × 1.04 = £93.60.

</details>

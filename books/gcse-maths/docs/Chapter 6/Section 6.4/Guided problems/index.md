# 6.4 Simple and compound interest — Practise with help

Try each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write the multiplier for 6% growth.
2. Work out 10% of £400.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check your answers</summary>

1. 1.06. 2. 40.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find the simple interest on £300 at 5% for 4 years.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find one year’s interest first.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

£300 × 0.05 × 4 = £60.

</details>

**2.** Find the compound amount for £400 at 10% for 2 years.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Apply 1.10 twice.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

£400 × 1.10² = £484.

</details>

**3.** Compare simple and compound totals for £1000 at 2% for 3 years.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Calculate both totals.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Simple: £1,060. Compound: £1,061.21.

</details>

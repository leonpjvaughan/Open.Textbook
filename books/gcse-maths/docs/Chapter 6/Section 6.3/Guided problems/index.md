# 6.3 Reverse percentages — Practise with help

Try each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write the multiplier for a 30% decrease.
2. Work out 120% of 50.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check your answers</summary>

1. 0.7. 2. 60.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A price after a 25% reduction is £60. Find the original.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

What percentage remains?

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

£60 ÷ 0.75 = £80.

</details>

**2.** A number after a 10% rise is 132. Find the start.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The final amount is 110% of the start.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

132 ÷ 1.10 = 120.

</details>

**3.** A ticket costs £48 after a 20% discount. Find its original price.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use 0.8 as the multiplier.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

£48 ÷ 0.8 = £60.

</details>

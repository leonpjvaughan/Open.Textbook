# 3.4 Negative and fractional indices — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $\sqrt{64}$.
2. Write $3^{-2}$ as a fraction.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $8$, because $8^2=64$.
2. $\frac{1}{9}$, because $3^{-2}=1/3^2$.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Work out $81^{1/2}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

An index of $\frac12$ means square root.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$81^{1/2}=\sqrt{81}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$81^{1/2}=\sqrt{81}=9$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Work out $125^{2/3}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Take the cube root, then square the result.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$125^{2/3}=(\sqrt[3]{125})^2=5^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$125^{2/3}=(\sqrt[3]{125})^2=5^2=25$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Work out $8^{-4/3}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find $8^{4/3}$, then take its reciprocal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$8^{4/3}=(\sqrt[3]{8})^4=2^4=16$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$8^{-4/3}=\frac{1}{8^{4/3}}=\frac{1}{16}$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

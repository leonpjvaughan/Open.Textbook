# 3.2 Estimating powers and roots — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $8^2$.
2. Work out $\sqrt{81}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $64$, because $8\times8=64$.
2. $9$, because $9^2=81$.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Estimate $\sqrt{45}$ to the nearest whole number.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the two consecutive square numbers around 45.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$6^2=36$ and $7^2=49$. The midpoint is $42.5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$45$ is between $36$ and $49$, and is above their midpoint, $42.5$. Therefore $\sqrt{45}$ rounds to $7$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Estimate $\sqrt[3]{300}$ to the nearest whole number.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Compare 300 with $6^3$ and $7^3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$6^3=216$ and $7^3=343$. The midpoint is $279.5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$300$ is above the midpoint of $216$ and $343$. Therefore $\sqrt[3]{300}$ rounds to $7$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Estimate $31^2$ by rounding 31 to the nearest ten.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Round 31 first, then square the rounded number.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$31\approx30$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$31^2\approx30^2=900$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

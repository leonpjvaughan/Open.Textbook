# 3.3 Laws of indices — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write $2\times2\times2\times2$ as a power.
2. Work out $5^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $2^4$, because there are four factors of 2.
2. $25$, because $5\times5=25$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Simplify $a^3a^5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write the factors and count them.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$a^3a^5=a^{3+5}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$a^3a^5=a^8$, because the product contains eight factors of $a$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Simplify $(b^2)^4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The whole group $b^2$ appears four times.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$(b^2)^4=b^{2\times4}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$(b^2)^4=b^8$, because there are four groups of two factors of $b$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Write $4^{-2}$ as a fraction.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A negative index means take the reciprocal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$4^{-2}=\frac{1}{4^2}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$4^{-2}=\frac{1}{4^2}=\frac{1}{16}$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

# 3.1 Powers and roots — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $4\times6$.
2. Work out $7^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $24$, because $4$ groups of $6$ make $24$.
2. $49$, because $7^2=7\times7$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Work out $3^4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write four factors of 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

$3^4=3\times3\times3\times3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$3^4=3\times3\times3\times3=81$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Work out $\sqrt{169}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Which positive integer squared gives 169?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

$13^2=169$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\sqrt{169}=13$, because $13^2=169$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Work out $\sqrt[3]{-64}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the number whose cube is $-64$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

$(-4)^3=-64$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\sqrt[3]{-64}=-4$, because $(-4)^3=-64$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

# 9.3 Forming and solving equations — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Write an expression for 5 more than $n$.
2. Solve $2x+3=11$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $n+5$.
2. $x=4$.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 8.1 for writing expressions and Section 9.1 for solving equations.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Solve $3x+2=14$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Undo addition, then multiplication.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $2$, then divide by $3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Subtract $2$: $3x=12$. Divide by $3$: $x=4$.
</details>

**2.** A number plus 9 is 23. Find it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Let the number be $n$ and write an equation.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $n+9=23$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Let it be $n$: $n+9=23$, so $n=14$.
</details>

**3.** Two consecutive integers total 25. Find them.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Let the smaller integer be $n$; the next is $n+1$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $n+(n+1)=25$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Equation $2n+1=25$, so $n=12$. The integers are $12$ and $13$.
</details>

**4.** A rectangle has width $w$ and length $w+2$. Its perimeter is 28 cm. Find its sides.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use perimeter = two widths + two lengths.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $2w+2(w+2)=28$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$2w+2(w+2)=28$, so $4w=24$ and $w=6$. Length $8$ cm.
</details>

**5.** A club charges £4 to join and £3 per visit. The total is £25. Find the visits.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Let $v$ be the visit count.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $4+3v=25$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Equation $4+3v=25$, so $3v=21$ and $v=7$.
</details>

# 9.4 Inequalities on a number line and set notation — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Write $-3$ and $2$ in order, smallest first.
2. Which is greater, $-5$ or $-2$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $-3$, then $2$.
2. $-2$.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 1.1 for ordering numbers and Section 1.3 for negative numbers.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Which is greater: $-2$ or $1$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use their positions on a number line.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Mark both values on a number line.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$1$ lies to the right, so $1>-2$.
</details>

**2.** Write “$x$ is less than 5” in symbols.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use a strict inequality symbol.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $x$ first, then the symbol.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$x<5$.
</details>

**3.** Describe $x\geq2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Say whether 2 is included and which direction works.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Draw a filled circle at $2$ and shade to the right.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

All values at least 2; include 2 and shade right.
</details>

**4.** Show $-1<x\leq3$ on a number line.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Choose an open boundary at $-1$ and a filled one at 3.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Use an open circle at $-1$ and a filled circle at $3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Shade between them; $-1$ is excluded and 3 included.
</details>

**5.** List the integer values in $0\leq x<4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Start at the included lower boundary and stop before 4.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

List the whole numbers from $0$ up to $4$, but not $4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$0,1,2,3$.
</details>

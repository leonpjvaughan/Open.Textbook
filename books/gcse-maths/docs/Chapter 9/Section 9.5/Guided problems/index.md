# 9.5 Solving linear inequalities — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Solve $x+7=12$.
2. On a number line, does $x>2$ use an open or a filled circle?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check the warm-up</summary>

1. $x=5$.
2. An open circle.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 9.1 for solving equations and Section 9.4 for number-line notation.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Solve $x+4>9$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract 4 from both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $4$ to leave $x$ alone.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$x>5$.
</details>

**2.** Solve $2x\leq10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by positive 2.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Divide both sides by $2$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$x\leq5$.
</details>

**3.** Solve $-3x\geq12$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by negative 3 and reverse the sign.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Divide both sides by $-3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$x\leq-4$.
</details>

**4.** Solve $4x-5<7$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add 5, then divide by 4.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Add $5$ to both sides, then divide by $4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$4x<12$, so $x<3$.
</details>

**5.** A number $n$ has triple less than 18. Give the values of $n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Translate “triple” and “less than” into an inequality.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $3n<18$, then divide by $3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$3n<18$, so $n<6$.
</details>

# 9.2 Equations with unknowns on both sides — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Simplify $5x-2x$.
2. Solve $3x=21$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $3x$.
2. $x=7$.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 8.2 for collecting like terms and Section 9.1 for one-step equations.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Solve $2x+5=17$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Collect the constant, then divide.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $5$ to get $2x$ alone.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Subtract $5$: $2x=12$. Divide by $2$: $x=6$.
</details>

**2.** Solve $4x+3=2x+13$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Move one unknown term by subtracting it on both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $2x$ from both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Subtract $2x$: $2x+3=13$. Subtract $3$: $x=5$.
</details>

**3.** Solve $3(x+1)=x+9$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Expand before collecting unknown terms.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Expand the bracket to $3x+3$ first.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$3x+3=x+9$; subtract $x$ and $3$: $2x=6$, so $x=3$.
</details>

**4.** Solve $5x-2=2x+10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Collect unknowns on one side.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $2x$ from both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Subtract $2x$: $3x-2=10$. Add $2$, then divide: $x=4$.
</details>

**5.** Does $2(x+4)=2x+8$ have one, none, or many solutions?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Expand and compare both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Expand the left side, then compare with the right.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Both sides reduce to $2x+8$. Every real value of $x$ is a solution.
</details>

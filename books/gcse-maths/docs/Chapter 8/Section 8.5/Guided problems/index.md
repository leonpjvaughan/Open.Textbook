# 8.5 Substitution into expressions and formulae — Practise with help

Attempt each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Work out $2+3\times4$.
2. Substitute $x=5$ into $x+3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check the warm-up</summary>

1. $14$. Multiply before adding.
2. $8$.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 1.4 for the order of operations and Section 8.1 for substitution.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Find $2a+5$ when $a=3$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Replace $a$ with $3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $2(3)+5$, then multiply before adding.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$2(3)+5=11$.
</details>

**2.** Find $x^2+4$ when $x=-3$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The power applies to the whole value $-3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $(-3)^2+4$ and keep the brackets.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$(-3)^2+4=13$.
</details>

**3.** Find $3p-2q$ when $p=4$ and $q=5$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Two letters need two substitutions.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $3(4)-2(5)$, then work out each product.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$12-10=2$.
</details>

**4.** Find $A=lw$ when $l=8$ and $w=3.5$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

$lw$ means $l$ multiplied by $w$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $A=8\times3.5$, then multiply.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$A=8(3.5)=28$ square units.
</details>

**5.** Find $v=u+at$ when $u=4$, $a=3$ and $t=5$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

$at$ means $a$ multiplied by $t$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $v=4+3(5)$, then multiply before adding.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$v=4+3(5)=19$.
</details>

**6.** Find $3x^2-2$ when $x=-2$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Square $x$ before multiplying by $3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $3(-2)^2-2$ and evaluate the power first.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$3(-2)^2-2=10$.
</details>

**7.** Find $5r-2$ when $r=6$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Replace $r$ with $6$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $5(6)-2$, then multiply before subtracting.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$5(6)-2=28$.
</details>

**8.** Find $P=2l+2w$ when $l=7,w=3$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

There are two terms to substitute.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $P=2(7)+2(3)$, then work out each product.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$P=2(7)+2(3)=20$.
</details>

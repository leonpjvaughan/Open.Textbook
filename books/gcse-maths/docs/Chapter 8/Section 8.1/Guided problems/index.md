# 8.1 Algebraic notation and vocabulary — Practise with help

Attempt each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer from memory before checking.

1. Work out $-8+3$.
2. Work out $3^2$.
<details markdown="1"><summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $-5$. 2. $9$.
If an answer differs, review the earlier method before continuing.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Write $7x$ as repeated addition, then state its coefficient.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Identify the letter, coefficient and constant in the written expression.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $x$ three times as a sum, then name the number multiplying $x$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Seven groups of $x$; coefficient $7$.
</details>

**2.** Write an expression for five more than $n$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Identify the letter, coefficient and constant in the written expression.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Which operation combines $n$ and $5$?
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$n+5$.
</details>

**3.** Evaluate $3x+2$ when $x=3$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Identify the letter, coefficient and constant in the written expression.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Replace $x$ with $3$, then multiply before adding.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$3(3)+2=11$.
</details>

**4.** State the terms and coefficients in $4a+4b$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Identify the letter, coefficient and constant in the written expression.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

List each part separated by a plus sign and identify the number multiplying each letter.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Terms $4a$ and $4b$; each coefficient is $4$.
</details>

**5.** If each item costs £5, write and evaluate the total cost $C=5n$ for $n=6$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Identify the letter, coefficient and constant in the written expression.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Replace $n$ with $6$ in $C=5n$, then attach the currency unit.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$C=5(6)=£30$.
</details>

**6.** Evaluate $2m+1$ when $m=4$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Identify the letter, coefficient and constant in the written expression.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Replace $m$ with $4$ and follow the order of operations.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The expression $2m+1$ becomes $2(4)+1=9$.
</details>

**7.** Write an expression for three fewer than $t$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Identify the letter, coefficient and constant in the written expression.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $3$ from $t$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$t-3$.
</details>

**8.** Is $2m+1=9$ an expression or equation?

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Identify the letter, coefficient and constant in the written expression.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

An equation contains an equals sign. Does this statement have one?
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

It is an equation because it states equality.
</details>

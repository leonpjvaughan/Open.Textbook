# 8.3 Expanding brackets — Practise with help

Attempt each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Simplify $6x+2x$.
2. Work out $-2\times4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check the warm-up</summary>

1. $8x$.
2. $-8$.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 8.2 for collecting like terms and Section 1.3 for multiplying with negative numbers.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Expand $2(x+3)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The $2$ multiplies both $x$ and $3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $2\times x$ and $2\times3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$2x+6$.
</details>

**2.** Expand $4(a-2)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The minus sign applies to the $2$ as well.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $4\times a$ and $4\times(-2)$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$4a-8$.
</details>

**3.** Expand $-3(y+5)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

A negative factor changes the sign of both terms.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $-3\times y$ and $-3\times5$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$-3y-15$.
</details>

**4.** Expand and simplify $2(x+4)+3x$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Expand the bracket first, then collect the $x$ terms.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $2x+8+3x$, then add $2x+3x$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$2x+8+3x=5x+8$.
</details>

**5.** Expand and simplify $5(2p-1)-3p$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Expand the bracket, then subtract $3p$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Write $10p-5-3p$, then work out $10p-3p$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$10p-5-3p=7p-5$.
</details>

**6.** Expand $3(4k-2)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply $3$ by both terms inside.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $3\times4k$ and $3\times(-2)$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$12k-6$.
</details>

**7.** Expand $6(2r+1)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The $6$ multiplies the $2r$ term and the $1$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $6\times2r$ and $6\times1$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$12r+6$.
</details>

**8.** Expand $-2(3w-4)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Both signs flip because the factor is negative.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $-2\times3w$ and $-2\times(-4)$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$-6w+8$.
</details>

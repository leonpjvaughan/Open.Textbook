# 8.4 Factorising with common factors — Practise with help

Attempt each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Expand $3(x+4)$.
2. List the common factors of 12 and 18 that are greater than 1.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $3x+12$.
2. $2$, $3$ and $6$.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 8.3 for expanding brackets and Section 2.1 for factors.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Factorise $6x+12$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Both terms are multiples of $6$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Take $6$ outside: write $6(\quad)$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$6(x+2)$.
</details>

**2.** Factorise $10y-15$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The highest common factor is $5$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Divide $10y$ and $15$ by $5$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$5(2y-3)$.
</details>

**3.** Factorise $8a+12b$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The common factor is a number, not a letter.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Take $4$ outside, then divide each term by $4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$4(2a+3b)$.
</details>

**4.** Factorise $9x^2+6x$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Both terms share a number and an $x$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The common factor is $3x$; divide each term by $3x$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$3x(3x+2)$.
</details>

**5.** Factorise $14m-21mn$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Both terms share $7$ and $m$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Take $7m$ outside, then divide each term by $7m$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$7m(2-3n)$.
</details>

**6.** Factorise $18x+24$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The highest common factor is $6$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Divide $18x$ and $24$ by $6$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$6(3x+4)$.
</details>

**7.** Factorise $12x-8$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Both terms are multiples of $4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Take $4$ outside, then divide each term by $4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$4(3x-2)$.
</details>

**8.** Factorise $15a+25b$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The common factor is $5$; there is no shared letter.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Take $5$ outside, then divide each term by $5$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$5(3a+5b)$.
</details>

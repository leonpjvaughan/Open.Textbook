# 8.6 Rearranging formulae — Practise with help

Attempt each question before opening a hint. Reveal the full working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Solve $x+6=10$.
2. What operation reverses multiplying by 4?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $x=4$.
2. Dividing by 4.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 9.1 for solving equations and Section 1.4 for inverse operations.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Make $x$ the subject of $y=x+7$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The $7$ is added to $x$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $7$ from both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$x=y-7$.
</details>

**2.** Make $x$ the subject of $y=4x$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

$x$ is multiplied by $4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Divide both sides by $4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$x=\frac{y}{4}$. Check: $4(y/4)=y$.
</details>

**3.** Make $b$ the subject of $A=a+b$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

$a$ is added to $b$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $a$ from both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$b=A-a$.
</details>

**4.** Make $r$ the subject of $C=2r$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

$r$ is multiplied by $2$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Divide both sides by $2$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$r=\frac{C}{2}$.
</details>

**5.** Make $h$ the subject of $V=bh/2$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

First remove the division by $2$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Multiply both sides by $2$, then divide by $b$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$h=\frac{2V}{b}$.
</details>

**6.** Make $t$ the subject of $s=t-9$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

$9$ is subtracted from $t$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Add $9$ to both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$t=s+9$.
</details>

**7.** Make $w$ the subject of $P=2l+2w$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Start by removing the $2l$ term.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $2l$ from both sides, then divide by $2$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$w=\frac{P-2l}{2}$.
</details>

**8.** Make $y$ the subject of $x=y+4$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Undo the addition of $4$ on both sides.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract $4$ from both sides of $x=y+4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$x=y+4$ — start with the given equation.

$x-4=y$ — subtract $4$ from both sides.

$y=x-4$ — write the subject on the left.
</details>

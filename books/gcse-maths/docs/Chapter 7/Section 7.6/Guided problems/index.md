# 7.6 Surds and exact calculations — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Simplify $\sqrt{36}$.
2. Work out $3.4\times10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $6$.
2. $34$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Simplify $\sqrt{48}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the largest square factor of $48$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $48=16\times3$ under the root.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\sqrt{48}=\sqrt{16\times3}=4\sqrt3$.
</details>

**2.** Simplify $2\sqrt{12}+\sqrt{27}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Simplify each root before adding.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $\sqrt{12}=2\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt{27}=3\sqrt3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$2(2\sqrt3)+3\sqrt3=7\sqrt3$.
</details>

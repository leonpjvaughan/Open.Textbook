# 7.3 Rounding and error intervals — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Round $4.376$ to 2 decimal places.
2. Round $0.006384$ to 2 significant figures.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $4.38$.
2. $0.0064$.
</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** A length rounds to $7.3$ cm to the nearest $0.1$ cm. Find its interval.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The value is rounded to the nearest $0.1$ cm. Find half of $0.1$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract and add $0.05$ to $7.3$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$7.25\leq L<7.35$ cm.
</details>

**2.** A mass is $460$ g to the nearest $10$ g. Write its interval.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Half of $10$ g is $5$ g.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract and add $5$ g. The upper endpoint is excluded.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$455\leq m<465$ g.
</details>

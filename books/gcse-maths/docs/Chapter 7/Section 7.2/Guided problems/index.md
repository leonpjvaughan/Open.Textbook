# 7.2 Compound measures: speed, density and pressure — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Convert $1.8$ m to cm.
2. Work out $12\times5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $180$ cm.
2. $60$.
</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** A car travels $180$ km in $3$ hours. Find its average speed.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Speed is distance divided by time.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Divide $180$ km by $3$ hours.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$180\div3=60$ km/h.
</details>

**2.** A liquid has density $1.5$ g/cm$^3$ and volume $80$ cm$^3$. Find its mass.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Rearrange density $=\text{mass}\div\text{volume}$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Mass $=\text{density}\times\text{volume}$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$1.5\times80=120$ g.
</details>

**3.** A force of $450$ N acts on an area of $0.15$ m$^2$. Find the pressure.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Pressure is force divided by area.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Divide $450$ N by $0.15$ m$^2$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$450\div0.15=3000$ Pa.
</details>

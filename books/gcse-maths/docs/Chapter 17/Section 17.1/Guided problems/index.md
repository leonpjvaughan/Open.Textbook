# 17.1 Properties of 2D and 3D shapes — Practise with help

Attempt each item before opening its help. Recall ideas from earlier chapters first.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. How many faces and vertices does a cuboid have?
2. How many sides does a hexagon have?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. 6 faces and 8 vertices.
2. 6 sides.

If an answer differs, sketch the shape and count its faces, vertices and sides again.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate

## Try these with help

**1.** A triangular prism has two triangular ends. Name its other faces.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A prism has one rectangular face for each side of its end shape.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The triangular end has three sides.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

It has three rectangular faces.
</details>
</details>

**2.** A cube has square faces. State its number of edges and vertices.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Count the joins and corners.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

A cube has 12 edges and 8 vertices.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

12 edges and 8 vertices.
</details>
</details>

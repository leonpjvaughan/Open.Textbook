# 17.1 Properties of 2D and 3D shapes — Practise with help

Attempt each item before opening its help. Recall ideas from earlier chapters first.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Name a shape with four right angles.
2. What does a scale of $1$ cm to $2$ m mean?
3. What angle is a right angle?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. A rectangle (a square is also correct).
2. Each centimetre on the drawing represents $2$ m in real life.
3. $90^\circ$.
</details>

## Try these with help

**1.** A triangular prism has two triangular ends. Name its other faces.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A prism has one rectangular face for each side of its end shape.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The triangular end has three sides.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full answer</summary>

It has three rectangular faces.
</details>
</details>

**2.** A cube has square faces. State its number of edges and vertices.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Count the joins and corners.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

A cube has 12 edges and 8 vertices.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full answer</summary>

12 edges and 8 vertices.
</details>
</details>

# 17.5 Bearings and scale drawings — Practise with help

Attempt each item before opening its help. Recall ideas from earlier chapters first.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Work out $4\times2.5$.
2. Work out $360-90$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $10$.
2. $270$.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 1.2 for decimal multiplication and
Section 16.1 for angle facts.

</details>
## Try these with help

**1.** Write the bearing for east.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Bearings start at north and turn clockwise.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

East is a quarter-turn clockwise from north.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

East has bearing $090^\circ$.
</details>
</details>

**2.** A map uses $1$ cm for $5$ km. What real distance is represented by $3.2$ cm?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply drawing length by the real distance per centimetre.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$3.2 \times 5 = 16$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$16$ km.
</details>
</details>

# 17.4 Loci — Practise with help

Attempt each item before opening its help. Recall ideas from earlier chapters first.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. What does a perpendicular bisector do to a line segment?
2. What is the distance from the centre of a circle to any point on it called?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. It cuts the segment in half at a right angle.
2. The radius.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 17.3 for the perpendicular bisector and
for drawing circles with compasses.

</details>
## Try these with help

**1.** Describe the locus of points 5 cm from point $A$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use a fixed radius centred at $A$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

A fixed distance from a point traces a circle.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

A circle centred at $A$ with radius 5 cm.
</details>
</details>

**2.** State the shortest distance from a point to a line.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Think about the angle made with the line.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The shortest segment meets the line at a right angle.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The perpendicular distance, measured at $90^\circ$.
</details>
</details>

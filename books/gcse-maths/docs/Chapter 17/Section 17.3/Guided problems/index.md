# 17.3 Constructions with ruler and compasses — Practise with help

Attempt each item before opening its help. Recall ideas from earlier chapters first.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. How many degrees are in a right angle?
2. Which instrument measures an angle in degrees?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $90^\circ$.
2. A protractor.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 16.1 for right angles and measuring angles.

</details>
## Try these with help

**1.** Construct the perpendicular bisector of a 6 cm segment.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Keep the compass wider than half the segment.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Draw equal arcs from both endpoints, then join their crossings.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The line through the two arc intersections is the perpendicular bisector.
</details>
</details>

**2.** Bisect an angle of $80^\circ$ using compasses.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use equal arcs from the two points where an arc meets the arms.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Join the angle vertex to the crossing of the equal arcs.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The bisector divides the angle into two angles of $40^\circ$.
</details>
</details>

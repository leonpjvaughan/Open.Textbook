# 1.3 Negative numbers — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $346 + 78$.
2. Work out $7.5 \times 4$.
3. Which is smaller, $-2$ or $-9$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $424$.
2. $30$.
3. $-9$ (further left).
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Work out: (a) $-5 + 9$; (b) $6 - 10$; (c) $-4 - 3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Picture the number line. Adding moves right; subtracting moves left.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

(a) $4$. (b) $-4$. (c) $-7$.
</details>

**2.** Work out: (a) $3 - (-7)$; (b) $-2 - (-8)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtracting a negative is the same as adding: $a - (-b) = a + b$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

(a) $3 + 7 = 10$. (b) $-2 + 8 = 6$.
</details>

**3.** Work out: (a) $(-4) \times 5$; (b) $(-4) \times (-5)$; (c) $(-24) \div 6$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Decide the sign from the table, then work out the size.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

(a) $-20$. (b) $20$. (c) $-4$.
</details>

**4.** The temperature at midnight was $-6\,^\circ\text{C}$. By 8 am it had risen
by $11\,^\circ\text{C}$. What was the temperature at 8 am?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

"Risen by 11" means add 11.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$-6 + 11 = 5\,^\circ\text{C}$.
</details>

**5.** A bank account has £30 in it. Three payments go out: £45, £12 and £8.
Work out the new balance and explain the sign.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Total the payments first, then subtract from £30.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Payments total $45 + 12 + 8 = 65$. Balance $= 30 - 65 = -£35$. Negative means the
account is overdrawn by £35.
</details>

**6.** In the chapter problem, the stall spent £92.80 and took £90. Write the profit
as a negative number and say what it means.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Profit $=$ taken $-$ spent.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$90 - 92.80 = -2.80$, so a loss of £2.80.
</details>

**7.** Complete: (a) $-3 + \Box = 2$; (b) $4 - \Box = -1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the inverse: if $-3 + \Box = 2$, then $\Box = 2 - (-3)$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

(a) $\Box = 2 - (-3) = 5$. (b) $\Box = 4 - (-1) = 5$.
</details>

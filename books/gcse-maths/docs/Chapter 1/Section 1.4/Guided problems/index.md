# 1.4 Priority of operations and inverse operations — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $52 \div 4$.
2. Work out $(-3) \times (-8)$.
3. Work out $9 - 14$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $13$.
2. $24$.
3. $-5$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Work out $8 + 3 \times 5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiplication before addition.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$3 \times 5 = 15$, then $8 + 15 = 23$.
</details>

**2.** Work out $(8 + 3) \times 5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Brackets first.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$8 + 3 = 11$, then $11 \times 5 = 55$.
</details>

**3.** Work out $20 - 6 \div 2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Division before subtraction.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$6 \div 2 = 3$, then $20 - 3 = 17$.
</details>

**4.** Work out $5 + 2^3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Index first: $2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$2^3 = 8$, then $5 + 8 = 13$.
</details>

**5.** Work out $18 \div 3 + 4 \times 2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Do the division and the multiplication, then add.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$18 \div 3 = 6$ and $4 \times 2 = 8$, so $6 + 8 = 14$.
</details>

**6.** Work out $24 \div 4 \times 3$. Be careful — work left to right.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Division and multiplication have equal priority.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$24 \div 4 = 6$, then $6 \times 3 = 18$.
</details>

**7.** I multiply a number by 5 and subtract 3. The result is 42. Find the number.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Work backwards: first undo the subtraction, then the multiplication.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$42 + 3 = 45$, then $45 \div 5 = 9$. Check: $9 \times 5 - 3 = 42$. ✓
</details>

**8.** Write a single expression for the cost of the stock in the chapter problem
and evaluate it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Cost $= 120 \times 0.65 + 8 \times 1.85$. Multiplication before addition.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$120 \times 0.65 = 78$ and $8 \times 1.85 = 14.80$, so the cost is
$78 + 14.80 = £92.80$.
</details>

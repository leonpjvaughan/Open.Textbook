# 1.2 The four operations — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write $4.06 \times 100$.
2. Write the value of the digit $7$ in $3.27$.
3. Which is larger, $0.5$ or $0.45$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $406$.
2. $0.07$.
3. $0.5 = 0.50 > 0.45$.
</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Work out $146 + 27.5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write $146$ as $146.0$ so the tenths column is visible, then align the columns.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$146.0 + 27.5 = 173.5$.
</details>

**2.** Work out $52.4 - 8.75$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write $52.4$ as $52.40$ so both numbers have two decimal places.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$52.40 - 8.75 = 43.65$.
</details>

**3.** Work out $46 \times 23$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Split $23$ into $20$ and $3$: find $46 \times 20$ and $46 \times 3$, then add.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$46 \times 20 = 920$ and $46 \times 3 = 138$, so $46 \times 23 = 1058$.
</details>

**4.** Work out $3.7 \times 4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Work out $37 \times 4$, then replace the decimal point (one decimal place).
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$37 \times 4 = 148$, so $3.7 \times 4 = 14.8$.
</details>

**5.** Work out $9.6 \div 4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

You can divide the whole number part and the tenths separately: $8 \div 4 = 2$ and
$1.6 \div 4 = 0.4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$9.6 \div 4 = 2.4$.
</details>

**6.** A box of 12 pens costs £8.40. Work out the cost of one pen, and check your
answer.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide: $8.40 \div 12$. Check by multiplying your answer by $12$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$8.40 \div 12 = 0.70$, so one pen costs 70p. Check: $12 \times 0.70 = 8.40$. ✓
</details>

**7.** In the chapter problem, 95 cans are sold at £1.20 each and 6 boxes at
£3.00 each. Work out the total taken.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find each amount separately, then add.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$95 \times 1.20 = 114$ and $6 \times 3.00 = 18$, so the total taken is
$114 + 18 = £132$.
</details>

# Chapter 14: Simultaneous equations and iteration

## The chapter problem: two taxi routes

Two taxi companies charge a fixed fee plus a price for each kilometre. A 4 km trip costs £14 with Company A and £16 with Company B.

An 8 km trip costs £22 with Company A and £24 with Company B.

**First contact:** Which company do you predict charges more per kilometre? Estimate first. You can check your prediction at the end of the chapter.

## By the end of this chapter you will be able to

- solve pairs of linear equations by elimination and substitution;
- find simultaneous solutions from graphs;
- solve a pair containing a linear and a quadratic equation;
- use iteration to find a numerical approximation.

## Where it is used

Two conditions at once appear when two prices or two plans must agree.
Simultaneous equations find the point where two rules give the same value.
Iteration finds an answer to a rule that cannot be solved directly, one step at
a time.

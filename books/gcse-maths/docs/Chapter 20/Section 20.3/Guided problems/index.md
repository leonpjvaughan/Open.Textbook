# 20.3 Angles of elevation and depression — Practise with help

Try each problem first. Open a hint if you need it, then use the working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Work out $\sin 30^\circ$ on your calculator in degree mode.
2. In a right-angled triangle, name the side next to an angle, other than the hypotenuse.

## Practise with help

**1.** A 10 m ramp rises at 30°. Find its vertical rise.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The ramp is the hypotenuse; rise is opposite.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

sin 30° = h/10, so h = 5 m.
</details>

**2.** A 12 m flagpole is viewed at 45°. Find horizontal distance.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Height is opposite and distance is adjacent.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

tan 45° = 12/d, so d = 12 m.
</details>

**3.** A 20 m tower is viewed from 25 m away. Find elevation angle.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Use tangent with opposite and adjacent.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

tan θ = 20/25, so θ ≈ 38.7°.
</details>

Before opening each full working, name the method and explain which measurements led you to choose it.

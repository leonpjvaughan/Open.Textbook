# 20.8 The area of any triangle — Practise with help

Try each problem first. Open a hint if you need it, then use the working to check your method.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Work out $\frac{1}{2}\times10\times4$.
2. Write the sine ratio for an acute angle $\theta$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. $20$. 2. $\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}$.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 19.1 for the area of a triangle and
Section 20.2 for the sine ratio.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Sides 4 and 6 include 30°. Find area.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Use half the product times sine of included angle.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Area = 1/2 × 4 × 6 × sin30° = 6.
</details>

**2.** Sides 5 and 8 include 90°. Find area.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Sine of 90° is 1.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Area = 1/2 × 5 × 8 = 20.
</details>

**3.** Sides 7 and 10 include 60°. Find area exactly.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Substitute sin60° = √3/2.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Area = 35 × √3/2 = 35√3/2.
</details>

Before opening each full working, name the method and explain which measurements led you to choose it.

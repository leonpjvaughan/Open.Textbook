# 21.2 Circle theorems — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. What is the total angle in a triangle?
2. What is the total angle on a straight line?

<details markdown="1"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. $180^\circ$.
2. $180^\circ$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** The angle at the circumference is $27^\circ$. Find the centre angle on the same arc.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

The centre angle is twice the circumference angle.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Calculate $2\times27$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$2\times27^\circ=54^\circ$.
</details>

**2.** An angle in a semicircle is $x$. A second angle stands on the same diameter in the same segment and is $y$. Find both angles.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Recall the fixed angle in a semicircle and the same-segment rule.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Use the diameter for $x$, then use the common chord for $y$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$x=90^\circ$ by the angle in a semicircle theorem. $y=x=90^\circ$ because the angles stand on the same chord in the same segment.
</details>

**3.** Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral are $x^\circ$ and $103^\circ$. Find $x$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Opposite angles total $180^\circ$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Set $x+103=180$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$x=180-103=77^\circ$.
</details>

**4.** Earlier topic: an isosceles triangle has two equal angles of $42^\circ$. Work out its third angle. Which angle fact applies, and why?

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Use the triangle angle sum, not a circle theorem.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract both equal angles from $180^\circ$.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

$180-42-42=96^\circ$. The triangle angle sum applies because the three interior angles form a triangle.
</details>

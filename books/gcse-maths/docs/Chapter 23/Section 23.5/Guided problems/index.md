## Practise with help

**1.** In a group of 25, 14 like tea, 11 like coffee and 6 like both. How many like neither?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract the overlap from each set before adding the exclusive regions.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Tea only is 8; coffee only is 5; both is 6. So 19 like at least one, and $25-19=6$ like neither.

</details>
</details>

**2.** A fair six-sided die is rolled. Find the probability of a result less than 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

List the die results that meet the condition, then divide by six.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The matching results are 1 and 2, so the probability is $\frac26=\frac13$.

</details>
</details>

**3.** Write in words what $A\cap B$ means.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Count the results less than 3, then divide by the six equally likely die results.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

 In both A and B.

</details>
</details>

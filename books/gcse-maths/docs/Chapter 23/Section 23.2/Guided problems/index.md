## Practise with help

**1.** A player scores 18 goals in 30 shots. Find the relative frequency of scoring.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the number of successes by the number of trials.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Relative frequency $=\frac{18}{30}=0.6$.

</details>
</details>

**2.** A fair six-sided die is rolled. Find the probability of a result less than 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

List the die results that meet the condition, then divide by six.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The matching results are 1 and 2, so the probability is $\frac26=\frac13$.

</details>
</details>

**3.** A spinner lands on green 27 times in 60 spins. Find its relative frequency.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the number of green results by the total number of spins.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

 $\frac{27}{60}=0.45$.

</details>
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A fair coin is tossed and a fair die is rolled. How many outcomes are in the possibility space?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Pair each coin result with every die result.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

There are $2\times6=12$ outcomes.

</details>
</details>

**2.** A frequency tree records 45 learners. 27 travel by bus, and 12 bus users bring lunch. Find the number of bus users who do not bring lunch.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract the subgroup count from the branch total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

There are $27-12=15$ bus users who do not bring lunch.

</details>
</details>

**3.** A fair coin is tossed and a fair four-sided spinner numbered 1 to 4 is spun. Find the probability of tails and an even number.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Pair the two tails outcomes with each of four spinner outcomes. Count the even results.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

 There are 8 equally likely pairs. Two have tails and an even number, so the probability is $\frac28=\frac14$.

</details>
</details>

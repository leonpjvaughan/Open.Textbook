# 23.6 Conditional probability — Practise with help

Work each question first. Open the hint if you need it, then reveal the working to check.

## Practise with help

**1.** Of 15 people who walk, 9 carry a bag. Find the probability a walker carries a bag.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the number of walkers as the restricted total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$P(\text{bag}\mid\text{walk})=\frac9{15}=\frac35$.

</details>
</details>

**2.** Of 20 students who cycle, 13 wear helmets. Find the probability a cyclist does not wear a helmet.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The condition restricts the group to cyclists. First find how many cyclists do not wear helmets.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

There are $20-13=7$ without helmets, so the probability is $\frac7{20}$.

</details>
</details>

**3.** In a group, 15 people are in A and 6 are in both A and B. Find the probability a person is in B, given they are in A.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the size of A as the restricted total; the overlap is the matching count.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

 The matching count is 6 and the restricted total is 15, so $P(B\mid A)=\frac6{15}=\frac25$.

</details>
</details>

# 2.3 Systematic listing strategies — Practise with help

Try each question first. Open a hint if needed, then check the full working.

## Warm-up: remember

1. List the factors of 10.
2. Work out $4\times3$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Answers</summary>

1. $1,2,5,10$.
2. $12$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A top can be red or yellow, and a base can be square or round. List every outfit.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Keep the top fixed while listing each base, then move to the other top.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

With red: red-square and red-round. Now do the same with yellow.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The outfits are red-square, red-round, yellow-square and yellow-round. There are four outcomes.
</details>

**2.** Make three-digit numbers using 1, 2 and 3 once each. List them in order.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Group the numbers by their hundreds digit.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Starting with 1 gives 123 and 132. Repeat with 2 and 3.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The list is $123,132,213,231,312,321$. Each first digit has two possible orders for the remaining digits.
</details>

**3.** A lunch has a choice of soup or salad and apple, pear or banana. List the meal choices.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

For each first choice, list every fruit.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Soup pairs with all three fruits; salad also pairs with all three.
</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Soup-apple, soup-pear, soup-banana, salad-apple, salad-pear, salad-banana. There are six choices.
</details>

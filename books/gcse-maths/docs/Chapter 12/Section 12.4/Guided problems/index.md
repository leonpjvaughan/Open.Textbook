# 12.4 Solving quadratic equations by factorising — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Factorise $x^2-x-6$.
2. Solve $x+3=0$.

## Practise with help

**1.** Solve $x^2+2x-8=0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Factorise using two numbers with sum $2$ and product $-8$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Use the hint to write the next algebra line. Then finish the calculation before opening the full working.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$(x+4)(x-2)=0$, so $x=-4$ or $x=2$.
</details>

**Choose a method:** factorise $x^2+7x+10$. Which method applies, and why?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Factorisation applies because the expression is a monic quadratic with integer factors: $x^2+7x+10=(x+5)(x+2)$.
</details>

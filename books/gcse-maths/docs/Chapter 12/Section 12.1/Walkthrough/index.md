# 12.1 Expanding and factorising quadratics — Learn

**Prerequisites:** collecting like terms, multiplying terms, and taking out a common factor.

**Objectives:** expand two brackets and reverse the process to factorise a monic quadratic.

## Expand two brackets

Multiply each term in the first bracket by each term in the second. Then collect like terms.

**Example 1.** Expand $(x+3)(x+5)$.

$$
(x+3)(x+5)=x^2+5x+3x+15
$$

$$
=x^2+8x+15
$$

The middle terms combine because both contain $x$.

## A guided area investigation

A rectangle with width $x+3$ and height $x+4$ splits into four smaller rectangles. The four areas are the four terms of the expansion.

**Predict:** before you move the controls, predict which two areas of the rectangle will combine to give the term $7x$. **Test:** change the two numbers and watch how the four areas change. **Explain:** why do the two middle areas always combine into one term?

## Factorise a quadratic

A quadratic expression has a highest power of $x$ equal to two. To factorise $x^2+bx+c$, find two numbers whose sum is $b$ and product is $c$.

**Example 2.** Factorise $x^2+7x+12$.

The numbers $3$ and $4$ add to $7$ and multiply to $12$.

$$
x^2+7x+12=(x+3)(x+4)
$$

Expanding checks the answer: $x^2+4x+3x+12=x^2+7x+12$.

## Solve by factorising

To solve a quadratic equation, first make one side zero. Then factorise and set each factor equal to zero.

**Example 3.** Solve $x^2+5x+6=0$.

$$
x^2+5x+6=0
$$

$$
(x+2)(x+3)=0
$$

So $x+2=0$ or $x+3=0$. Therefore $x=-2$ or $x=-3$.

> **Watch out.** The factors must have both the correct sum and product. For $x^2+7x+12$, $(x+2)(x+6)$ has the right product, but its middle coefficient is $8$. Expand to check.

## Progress check

The height model for the chapter problem is $h=-t^2+4t+5$. Notice its quadratic term and two other terms. Later, factorising will help identify when the height is zero.

## You should now be able to

- multiply every term in one bracket by every term in another;
- collect like terms after expansion;
- factorise a monic quadratic using the sum and product of two numbers;
- solve a factorisable quadratic equation by setting each factor to zero;
- check a factorisation by expanding it.

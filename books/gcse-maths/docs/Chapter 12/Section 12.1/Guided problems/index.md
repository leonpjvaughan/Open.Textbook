## Practise with help

**1.** Expand $(x+2)(x+6)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply each term in the first bracket by both terms in the second.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Use the hint to write the next algebra line. Then finish the calculation before opening the full working.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$(x+2)(x+6)=x^2+6x+2x+12=x^2+8x+12$.
</details>

**2.** Factorise $x^2+9x+20$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find a pair with sum $9$ and product $20$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Use the hint to write the next algebra line. Then finish the calculation before opening the full working.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The pair is $4,5$, so $x^2+9x+20=(x+4)(x+5)$.
</details>

**Choose a method:** factorise $x^2+7x+10$. Which method applies, and why?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Factorisation applies because the expression is a monic quadratic with integer factors: $x^2+7x+10=(x+5)(x+2)$.
</details>

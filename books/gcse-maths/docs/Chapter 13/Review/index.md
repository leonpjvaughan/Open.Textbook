# Chapter 13: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

The signal crosses its middle value while rising, then reaches a maximum and minimum before repeating. This matches $y=\sin x$ over one cycle, where $0^\circ\leq x\leq360^\circ$.

The maximum occurs at $90^\circ$, the minimum at $270^\circ$, and the pattern repeats every $360^\circ$. A vertical shift changes its middle value; a vertical scale changes its height.

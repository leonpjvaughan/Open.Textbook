# Chapter 13: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

The signal crosses its middle value while rising, then reaches a maximum and minimum before repeating. This matches $y=\sin x$ over one cycle, where $0^\circ\leq x\leq360^\circ$. The maximum occurs at $90^\circ$, the minimum at $270^\circ$, and the pattern repeats every $360^\circ$. A vertical shift changes its middle value; a vertical scale changes its height.

## Mixed practice — choose the method first

For each item, name the graph feature or method you will use before answering, and say why it fits.

1. Sketch the key features of $y=x^3-4x$. Find its intercepts.
2. State the centre and radius of $(x-3)^2+(y+1)^2=16$.
3. Solve $\sin x=0$ for $0^\circ\leq x\leq360^\circ$.
4. Translate $y=x^2$ two units left and three units up.
5. A radius has gradient $4$. Find the tangent gradient and give a reason.
6. State the asymptotes of $y=1/x$ and $y=\tan x$ from $0^\circ$ to $360^\circ$.
7. The point $(2,5)$ lies on $y=f(x)$. Give its image under $y=f(x)-4$.
8. Which family models repeated equal percentage growth? Give a reason.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $x(x-2)(x+2)=0$, so the intercepts are $(-2,0),(0,0),(2,0)$. The ends fall left and rise right.
2. Centre $(3,-1)$; radius $4$.
3. $0^\circ,180^\circ,360^\circ$.
4. $y=(x+2)^2+3$.
5. $-1/4$, because perpendicular gradients multiply to $-1$.
6. Reciprocal: $x=0$ and $y=0$. Tangent: $x=90^\circ$ and $270^\circ$.
7. $(2,1)$.
8. Exponential, because equal input steps multiply the output by a fixed factor.
</details>

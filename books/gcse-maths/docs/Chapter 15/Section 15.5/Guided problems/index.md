# 15.5 Algebraic proof — Practise with help

Work each problem before revealing support.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Expand $(x+2)(x+3)$.
2. Factorise $x^2-9$.
3. Solve $2x+5=13$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $x^2+5x+6$. 2. $(x-3)(x+3)$. 3. $x=4$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Prove that the sum of two consecutive integers is odd.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Represent the integers as $n$ and $n+1$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>First step</summary>

Their sum is $n+(n+1)$. Collect the terms.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$n+(n+1)=2n+1$. This is odd because it is twice an integer plus one.

</details>

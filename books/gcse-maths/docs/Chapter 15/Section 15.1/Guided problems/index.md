## Practise with help

**1.** Find the inverse of $f(x)=4x-7$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write $y=4x-7$, swap $x$ and $y$, then make $y$ the subject.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

After swapping, $x=4y-7$. Add $7$ to both sides.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$x+7=4y$, so $y=\frac{x+7}{4}$. Thus $f^{-1}(x)=\frac{x+7}{4}$.

</details>

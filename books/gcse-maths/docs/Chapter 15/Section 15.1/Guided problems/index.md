# 15.1 Functions: inverse and composite — Practise with help

Work each problem before revealing support.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Expand $(x+2)(x+3)$.
2. Factorise $x^2-9$.
3. Solve $2x+5=13$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $x^2+5x+6$. 2. $(x-3)(x+3)$. 3. $x=4$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find the inverse of $f(x)=4x-7$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write $y=4x-7$, swap $x$ and $y$, then make $y$ the subject.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>First step</summary>

After swapping, $x=4y-7$. Add $7$ to both sides.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$x+7=4y$, so $y=\frac{x+7}{4}$. Thus $f^{-1}(x)=\frac{x+7}{4}$.

</details>

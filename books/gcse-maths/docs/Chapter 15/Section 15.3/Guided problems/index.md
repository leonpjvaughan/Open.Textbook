Work each problem before revealing support.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. Work out $\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{4}$.
2. Factorise $3x+6$.

## Practise with help

**1.** Simplify $\frac{1}{x}+\frac{2}{x+2}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use common denominator $x(x+2)$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$\frac{x+2}{x(x+2)}+\frac{2x}{x(x+2)}$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Combine numerators: $\frac{3x+2}{x(x+2)}$, with $x\neq0,-2$.

</details>

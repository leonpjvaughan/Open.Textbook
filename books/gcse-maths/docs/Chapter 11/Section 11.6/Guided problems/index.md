# 11.6 Gradients and areas under graphs — Practise with help

Try each question before opening its hint.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $5\times4$. 2. Find the area of a rectangle with sides 3 and 2.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Check your answers</summary>

Answers: 20; 6 square units.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Velocity rises from 2 m/s to 10 m/s in 4 seconds. Find the gradient.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Use change in velocity divided by change in time.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Calculate $(10-2)/4$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$(10-2)/4=2$, so the acceleration is 2 m/s².</details>

**2.** A velocity-time graph shows 6 m/s for 5 seconds. Find the distance.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Use the area of a rectangle.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Multiply velocity by time.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$6\times5=30$, so the distance is 30 m.</details>

**3.** Earlier learning: a car travels 60 km/h for 2.5 hours. Find its distance.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Multiply speed by time.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Calculate $60\times2.5$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>150 km.</details>

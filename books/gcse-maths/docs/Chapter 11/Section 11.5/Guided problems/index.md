## Practise with help

**1.** A cyclist travels 18 km in 1.5 hours. Find the constant speed.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Divide distance by time.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Calculate $18\div1.5$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$18\div1.5=12$, so the speed is 12 km/h.</details>

**2.** A car travels at 60 km/h for 2.5 hours. Find its distance.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Distance equals speed multiplied by time.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Calculate $60\times2.5$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$60\times2.5=150$, so the distance is 150 km.</details>

**3.** Earlier learning: a line has gradient 3 km per hour. How far does it represent in 5 hours?

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Multiply rate by time.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Calculate $3\times5$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>15 km.</details>

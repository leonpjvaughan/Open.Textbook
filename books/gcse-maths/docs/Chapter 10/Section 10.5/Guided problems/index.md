## Practise with help

**1.** Continue $4,12,36,\ldots$ by two terms.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide adjacent terms to find the multiplier.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$36\times3=108$, then $108\times3=324$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The next terms are $108,324$.

</details>
</details>
</details>


**2.** Find the common multiplier in $64,32,16,8,\ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide a term by the one before it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$32\div64=\frac12$. Check: $16\div32=\frac12$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The multiplier is $\frac12$.

</details>
</details>
</details>


**3.** A sequence starts at 5 and has multiplier 2. Find term 5.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply by 2 four times after the first term.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$5,10,20,40,80$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Term 5 is $80$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

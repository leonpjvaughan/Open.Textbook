# 10.1 Term-to-term and position-to-term rules — Practise with help

Try each item before opening its hints. Reveal the full working when you want to check your reasoning.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $6+4$.
2. Find the 5th term of $2n$.
3. Write the next term: $10,7,4,\ldots$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check your warm-up</summary>

1. $10$.
2. $10$.
3. $1$; subtract $3$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Continue $2,6,10,14,\ldots$ by two terms.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the change between adjacent terms.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

Add $4$ each time: $14+4=18$, then $18+4=22$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The terms are $18,22$.

</details>
</details>
</details>


**2.** The rule is $5n+1$. Find the 4th term.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Substitute $n=4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

$5(4)+1=20+1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The 4th term is $21$.

</details>
</details>
</details>


**3.** A sequence begins $8,13,18,23,\ldots$. Find the nth term.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The change is $5$, so begin with $5n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>One step</summary>

At position 1, $5n=5$ but the term is 8, so add 3.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The rule is $5n+3$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

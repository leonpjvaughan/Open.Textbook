# 24.5 Time series and line graphs — Learn

**Prerequisites:** coordinates, scales and reading points from a graph.

**Objectives:** read and construct a time-series table and line graph; describe changes over time.

A **time series** is data collected at different times. Put time on the horizontal axis and the measured quantity on the vertical axis.

Choose an even scale, label both axes with units, plot each point, then join points in time order.

**Example.** Rainfall was 4 mm in January, 7 mm in February and 5 mm in March. Describe the change.

From January to February it rises by $7-4=3$ mm.

From February to March it falls by $7-5=2$ mm.

The line graph shows a rise followed by a fall.

When a scale does not begin at zero, show a clear break. The line graph still shows change over time, but the vertical scale affects how steep the changes look.

> **Watch out.** Do not join points in a different order. The horizontal positions represent time, so keep the dates in sequence.
> **Re-check:** read the horizontal axis and confirm that the dates are still in order.

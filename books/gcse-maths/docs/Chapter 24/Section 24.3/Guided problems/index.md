# 24.3 Averages and range — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

**Closed book:** Work out $3+4$ and write $0.25$ as a fraction.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Check your answers</summary>

$3+4=7$. Also, $0.25=25/100=1/4$. If either answer differs, revisit number operations and fractions before continuing.
</details>

### Question 1

Work out $2+5$ and order 4, 1, 3.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Recall addition and ordering.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The sum is 7; ordered values are 1, 3, 4.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The sum is 7; ordered values are 1, 3, 4.
</details>

### Question 2

Find the mean of 2, 4, 6.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Add, then divide by the count.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Mean $=(2+4+6)/3=4$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Mean $=(2+4+6)/3=4$.
</details>

### Question 3

Find the median and range of 2, 3, 3, 8.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Use the middle pair and extremes.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Median $=(3+3)/2=3$; range $=8-2=6$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Median $=(3+3)/2=3$; range $=8-2=6$.
</details>

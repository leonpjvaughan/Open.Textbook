# Chapter 5: Ratio and proportion

## The chapter problem: the model garden

A school is drawing a model of a garden. The path is 18 m long, and a scale drawing must show it accurately.

Before learning the method, predict: would the path fit on a 30 cm page if 1 cm represented 1 m? Estimation is enough for now.

## By the end of this chapter you will be able to

- Write, simplify and use ratios to share amounts.
- Connect ratios with fractions, tables and straight-line relationships.
- Model direct and inverse proportion.
- Use scale drawings and compare lengths, areas and volumes of similar shapes.

## Where it is used

Ratios compare quantities and keep the comparison the same when both change.
Recipes, mixing paint, currency exchange and map scales all use ratios. Direct
and inverse proportion describe how one quantity changes when another changes,
such as pay with hours worked, or travel time with speed.

# Chapter 22: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

Take $A$ as the origin. Let $\overrightarrow{AB}=\mathbf{u}$ and
$\overrightarrow{AD}=\mathbf{v}$. Since opposite sides of a parallelogram have
the same directed vectors, $C$ has position vector $\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}$.

The midpoint of $AC$ has position vector
$\frac12(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v})$. The midpoint of $BD$ has position vector
$\frac12(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v})$ too. The midpoints are the same point, so the
diagonals bisect each other. The prediction from the chapter opener was correct.

## Mixed practice — choose the method first

For each item, name the method before you work it out. Explain why that method applies.

1. Point $P=(-2,4)$ is translated by $\begin{pmatrix}5\\-3\end{pmatrix}$. Give its image coordinates.
2. Find the vector from $A=(3,-1)$ to $B=(-2,5)$.
3. Work out $\begin{pmatrix}2\\-4\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-7\\3\end{pmatrix}$.
4. Work out $2\begin{pmatrix}-3\\5\end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix}4\\-2\end{pmatrix}$.
5. In triangle $PQR$, $\overrightarrow{PQ}=\mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{QR}=\mathbf{b}$. Find $\overrightarrow{PR}$.
6. In parallelogram $ABCD$, $\overrightarrow{AB}=\mathbf{u}$ and $\overrightarrow{AD}=\mathbf{v}$. Find $\overrightarrow{AC}$ and $\overrightarrow{DB}$.
7. Explain how to prove that the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other using vectors.
8. Solve $3x+4=19$ from earlier algebra. Which operation undoes the addition first?
9. A rectangle has width $x$ and length $x+3$. Its perimeter is 30 cm. Form an equation and find its dimensions.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and concise working</summary>

1. $(3,1)$; add the vector components to the coordinates.
2. $\begin{pmatrix}-2-3\\5-(-1)\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-5\\6\end{pmatrix}$.
3. $\begin{pmatrix}-5\\-1\end{pmatrix}$; add matching components.
4. $\begin{pmatrix}-10\\12\end{pmatrix}$; multiply first, then subtract.
5. $\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b}$; add the vectors along the route.
6. $\overrightarrow{AC}=\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}$ and $\overrightarrow{DB}=\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}$.
7. Use position vectors $A=\mathbf{0}$, $B=\mathbf{u}$, $D=\mathbf{v}$ and $C=\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}$. Both diagonal midpoints are $\frac12(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v})$.
8. Subtract 4 first: $3x=15$, then divide by 3, so $x=5$.
9. $2x+2(x+3)=30$, so $4x=24$ and $x=6$. The dimensions are 6 cm by 9 cm.
</details>

# 22.2 Adding and subtracting vectors — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. What movement does $\begin{pmatrix}2\\-3\end{pmatrix}$ describe?
2. Find the vector from $(1,4)$ to $(3,-2)$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. Move 2 right and 3 down.
2. $\begin{pmatrix}3-1\\-2-4\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-6\end{pmatrix}$; subtract start from end.
</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Work out $\begin{pmatrix}4\\1\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-2\\5\end{pmatrix}$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Add the top entries and the lower entries separately.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>$\begin{pmatrix}4+(-2)\\1+5\end{pmatrix}$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$\begin{pmatrix}2\\6\end{pmatrix}$.</details>

**2.** Work out $\begin{pmatrix}7\\-3\end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix}2\\4\end{pmatrix}$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Subtract matching components.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>$\begin{pmatrix}7-2\\-3-4\end{pmatrix}$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$\begin{pmatrix}5\\-7\end{pmatrix}$.</details>

**3.** Work out $3\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\end{pmatrix}$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Multiply both entries by 3.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>$\begin{pmatrix}3(-2)\\3(1)\end{pmatrix}$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$\begin{pmatrix}-6\\3\end{pmatrix}$.</details>

**4.** Work out $2\begin{pmatrix}3\\-1\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-4\\5\end{pmatrix}$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Scale the first vector before adding.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>$\begin{pmatrix}6\\-2\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-4\\5\end{pmatrix}$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\end{pmatrix}$.</details>

**5.** Explain why $\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}=\mathbf{a}+(-\mathbf{b})$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>What vector reverses the direction of $\mathbf{b}$?</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>$-\mathbf{b}$ has the same length and the opposite direction.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>Adding $-\mathbf{b}$ undoes the movement $\mathbf{b}$, so it gives the same result as subtracting $\mathbf{b}$.</details>

**6.** The route from $A$ to $C$ is $\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}$. Write the return vector from $C$ to $A$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Reverse the whole route.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Reverse each movement and reverse the order: $-\mathbf{v}-\mathbf{u}$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$\overrightarrow{CA}=-\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}$, which is the negative of $\overrightarrow{AC}$.</details>

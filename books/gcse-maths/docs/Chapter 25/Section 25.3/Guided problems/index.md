# 25.3 Histograms — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Find the width of the interval $4<x\leq12$.
2. Divide $18$ by $6$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Feedback</summary>

1. Width $8$. 2. $3$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A class has width 5 and frequency 15. Find its frequency density.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Divide frequency by width.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Step</summary>

Use $15/5$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Frequency density $=15/5=3$.
</details>

**2.** A class $10<x\leq30$ has frequency 24. Find its width and density.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract boundaries, then divide the frequency by the width.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Step</summary>

Width $=30-10=20$. Density $=24/20$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Width $20$; frequency density $1.2$.
</details>

**3.** Earlier work: cumulative frequency totals increase by 6, then 9. What are the two class frequencies?

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract consecutive cumulative totals to recover each frequency.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Step</summary>

The first frequency is 6. The next is 9.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The class frequencies are $6$ and $9$.
</details>

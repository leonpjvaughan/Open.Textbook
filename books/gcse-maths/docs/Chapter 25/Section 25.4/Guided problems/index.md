# 25.4 Scatter graphs and correlation — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. In the coordinate $(3,8)$, which value is horizontal?
2. If a graph rises from left to right, is its gradient positive or negative?

<details markdown="1"><summary>Feedback</summary>

1. The first value, $3$. 2. Positive.
</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Study the pairs $(1,4),(2,6),(3,7),(4,10)$. Describe the correlation.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Look at whether the second value tends to rise as the first rises.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The second values generally increase.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The data show positive correlation. Plot the pairs to see the upward pattern.
</details>

**2.** A line of best fit predicts 40 units at $x=0$ and 60 units at $x=5$. Estimate the value at $x=3$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Find the increase per unit of $x$.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

The increase is $20$ over 5 units, or 4 per unit.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

Estimate $40+3\times4=52$ units.
</details>

**3.** Earlier work: a data set has median 12 and another has median 18. Which has the greater median?

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>

Compare the two values.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>

Since $18>12$, the second median is greater.
</details>

<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>

The second data set has the greater median, 18.
</details>

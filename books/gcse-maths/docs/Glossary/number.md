# Glossary — Number

**Decimal place** — a position after the decimal point; the first is tenths, the
second hundredths.

**Estimate** — a quick, approximate answer found by rounding, used to check
whether an exact answer is reasonable.

**Index (plural indices)** — a power, such as the $3$ in $2^3 = 8$.

**Integer** — a whole number, positive, negative or zero.

**Inverse operation** — the operation that undoes another; addition and
subtraction are inverses, as are multiplication and division.

**Loss** — a negative profit: the money taken is less than the money spent.

**Negative number** — a number less than zero, written with a minus sign.

**Order of operations** — the convention (BIDMAS) for the order in which
operations are carried out: brackets, indices, division and multiplication,
addition and subtraction.

**Place value** — the value a digit has because of its position in a number.

**Profit** — money left after costs are subtracted from the money taken.

**Reciprocal** — the result of dividing 1 by a number; the reciprocal of $4$ is
$\frac{1}{4}$.

**Rounding** — replacing a number by a nearby number with fewer significant
figures or decimal places.

**Significant figures** — the digits that carry meaning in a number, counted from
the first non-zero digit.

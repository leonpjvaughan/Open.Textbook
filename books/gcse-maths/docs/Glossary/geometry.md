# Glossary — Geometry

**Adjacent** — next to another side or angle, with a shared boundary.

**Angle of depression** — the angle between a horizontal line and a line of sight below it.

**Angle of elevation** — the angle between a horizontal line and a line of sight above it.

**Arc** — part of a circle's circumference.

**Bearing** — a direction measured clockwise from north and written as a three-digit angle.

**Chord** — a straight line joining two points on a circle.

**Congruent** — having the same shape and size, so matching sides and angles are equal.

**Cyclic quadrilateral** — a four-sided shape whose vertices all lie on a circle.

**Diameter** — a straight line through a circle's centre joining two points on its circumference.

**Enlargement** — a transformation that changes a shape's size using a centre and scale factor.

**Face** — a flat surface of a three-dimensional shape.

**Hypotenuse** — the longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle.

**Locus** — the set of points that follow a stated rule.

**Midpoint** — the point exactly halfway along a line segment.

**Perpendicular** — meeting at a right angle.

**Polygon** — a closed, flat shape made from straight line segments.

**Prism** — a three-dimensional shape with matching parallel ends and a constant cross-section.

**Radius** — a line from the centre of a circle to its circumference.

**Regular polygon** — a polygon with all sides equal and all angles equal.

**Reflection symmetry** — a shape property where a line divides the shape into two matching mirror images.

**Sector** — the part of a circle enclosed by two radii and an arc.

**Segment** — the part of a circle enclosed by a chord and an arc.

**Similar shapes** — shapes with equal matching angles and proportional matching lengths.

**Tangent** — a straight line that touches a circle at one point.

**Transformation** — a change in a shape's position, orientation or size.

**Vertex** — a corner where two or more edges meet.

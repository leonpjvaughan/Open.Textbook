# Glossary — Ratio

**Compound measure** — a measure made by combining two different units, such as metres per second.

**Constant of proportionality** — the fixed number that links two quantities in a proportional relationship.

**Density** — mass per unit of volume.

**Direct proportion** — a relationship where one quantity is a fixed multiple of another.

**Growth factor** — the multiplier that gives a new amount after growth, such as $1.08$ for an increase of $8\%$.

**Inverse proportion** — a relationship where one quantity increases as another decreases, so their product stays constant.

**Lower bound** — the smallest value a rounded measurement could have had.

**Percentage change** — the increase or decrease written as a percentage of the original amount.

**Pressure** — force per unit of area.

**Ratio** — a way to compare amounts by showing how many parts of each amount there are.

**Rate** — a comparison of two quantities measured in different units, such as kilometres per hour.

**Reverse percentage** — a method for finding an original amount from a value after a percentage change.

**Scale factor** — the multiplier that changes a length from one similar shape or drawing to another.

**Scale drawing** — a drawing where every length has the same ratio to the matching real length.

**Simple interest** — interest calculated each time on the original amount only.

**Standard form** — a way to write a number as $a\times10^n$, where $1\leq a<10$ and $n$ is an integer.

**Uncertainty interval** — the range of values that could round to a stated measurement.

**Unitary method** — a method that finds the value of one unit before finding the value of several units.

**Upper bound** — the largest value a rounded measurement could have had.

**Volume scale factor** — the multiplier between the volumes of similar solids; it is the cube of the length scale factor.

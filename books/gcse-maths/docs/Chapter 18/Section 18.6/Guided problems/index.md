# 18.6 Similarity: areas and volumes — Practise with help

Attempt these from memory before opening the answers. Then try each question before opening a hint.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $-3+8$.
2. Translate $(1,2)$ by $\begin{pmatrix}2\\-1\end{pmatrix}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $5$. Starting at $-3$ and moving $8$ places right gives $5$.
2. $(3,1)$. Add $2$ to the horizontal coordinate and subtract $1$ from the vertical coordinate.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Similar shapes have length scale factor $4$. Find the area scale factor.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Square the length factor.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>$4^2=16$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>The area scale factor is $16$.</details>

**2.** Similar solids have length scale factor $3$. Find the volume scale factor.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Cube the length factor.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>$3^3=27$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>The volume scale factor is $27$.</details>

**3.** Earlier topic: solve $3x=18$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Use the inverse operation.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Divide both sides by $3$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$x=6$.</details>

# 18.4 Congruence and congruence criteria — Practise with help

Attempt these from memory before opening the answers. Then try each question before opening a hint.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. Do not look back at the section.

1. State the sum of the angles in a triangle.
2. What does it mean for two lengths to be equal?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $180^\circ$.
2. They have the same length.

If an answer differs, revisit Section 16.3 for triangle angle facts.

</details>
## Practise with help

**1.** Two triangles have three matching side lengths. Name a criterion.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Count the matching sides.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Three sides correspond.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>SSS proves the triangles congruent.</details>

**2.** Two right triangles have equal hypotenuse and one equal shorter side.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Use the special criterion for right-angled triangles.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Both have a right angle, with matching hypotenuse and one leg.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>RHS proves congruence.</details>

**3.** Earlier topic: solve $3x=18$.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Hint</summary>Use the inverse operation.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Next step</summary>Divide both sides by $3$.</details>
<details markdown="1"><summary>Full working</summary>$x=6$.</details>

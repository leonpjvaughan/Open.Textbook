# Chapter 16 Review

## The chapter problem, solved

The pattern can be solved by applying each angle fact in turn. At the straight path, adjacent angles total $180^\circ$. At a crossing, vertically opposite angles match.

Parallel borders let you transfer corresponding or alternate angles, while co-interior angles total $180^\circ$. The triangular tile has interior sum $180^\circ$. State the fact beside every calculation, then check the total fits the shape.

## Mixed practice: choose a method and explain why

For each question, choose the method and give a reason.

1. Two lines cross. One angle is $62^\circ$. Find the other three.
2. A transversal crosses parallel lines. One angle is $73^\circ$. Find its alternate angle and co-interior angle.
3. A triangle has angles $41^\circ$, $x^\circ$ and $(x+19)^\circ$. Find $x$.
4. A quadrilateral has angles $85^\circ$, $95^\circ$, $110^\circ$ and $x^\circ$. Find $x$.
5. Find each interior angle of a regular octagon.
6. An exterior angle of a regular polygon is $40^\circ$. Find the number of sides.
7. Earlier learning: solve $4x+7=31$.
8. Higher only: prove that the exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of its two opposite interior angles.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Check your answers</summary>

1. $62^\circ$, $118^\circ$, $118^\circ$; opposite angles match and adjacent angles form a straight line.
2. Alternate: $73^\circ$; co-interior: $107^\circ$.
3. $41+x+x+19=180$, so $x=60$.
4. $x=70^\circ$, using the quadrilateral sum $360^\circ$.
5. $(8-2)180\div8=135^\circ$.
6. $360\div40=9$ sides.
7. $x=6$.
8. Let the interior angles be $a,b,c$ and exterior angle $e$. Then $a+b+c=180$ and $e+c=180$. Subtracting $c$ gives $e=a+b$.

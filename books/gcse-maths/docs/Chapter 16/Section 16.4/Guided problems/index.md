## Practise with help

**1.** Find the interior angle sum of a pentagon.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write the angle fact that fits the diagram or shape.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

An $n$-sided polygon has interior angle sum $(n-2)\times180^\circ$.

$$
(5-2)\times180=540^\circ
$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The pentagon has $5-2=3$ triangles, so its interior angle sum is $3\times180=540^\circ$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Find each interior angle of a regular pentagon.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Mark the known angles and identify their relationship before calculating.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Find the pentagon's interior sum, then divide by its five equal angles.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The interior sum is $540^\circ$. A regular pentagon has five equal angles.

$$
540\div5=108^\circ
$$

Each interior angle is $108^\circ$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3. Earlier learning.** Work out: $7+5\times3$. Explain which operation you do first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the order of operations.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Calculate $5\times3$ before adding.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$7+5\times3=7+15=22$. Multiplication comes before addition.

# Chapter 16: Angles, polygons and geometrical reasoning

## The chapter problem: a tiled courtyard

A designer plans a pattern with a straight path crossing a triangular tile. Some angles are marked, while others are hidden. How can you find every missing angle and explain why the answers must be correct?

**First contact — estimate or notice.** Imagine a straight line meeting another line. What might the two angles on one side add to? You do not need to calculate anything yet.

By the end of this chapter, you will use angle facts to solve the courtyard problem and explain each step.

## By the end of this chapter you will be able to

- use angles at a point and on a straight line;
- identify corresponding, alternate and co-interior angles in parallel lines;
- use angle sums in triangles and quadrilaterals;
- find and use interior and exterior angle sums in polygons;
- write a geometrical argument with a reason for each step.

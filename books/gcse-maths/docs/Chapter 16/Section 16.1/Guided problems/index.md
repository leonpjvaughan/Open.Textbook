## Practise with help

**1.** Angles $x^\circ$ and $124^\circ$ lie on a straight line. Find $x$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write the angle fact that fits the diagram or shape.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Set up an equation using the straight-line angle total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Angles on a straight line total $180^\circ$.

$$
x+124=180
$$

$$
x=180-124=56^\circ
$$

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Three angles around a point are $85^\circ$, $140^\circ$ and $y^\circ$. Find $y$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Mark the known angles and identify their relationship before calculating.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Angles around a point total $360^\circ$. Write an equation with that total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Angles around a point total $360^\circ$.

$$
85+140+y=360
$$

$$
y=360-85-140=135^\circ
$$

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3. Earlier learning.** Work out: $7+5\times3$. Explain which operation you do first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the order of operations.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Calculate $5\times3$ before adding.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$7+5\times3=7+15=22$. Multiplication comes before addition.

</details>

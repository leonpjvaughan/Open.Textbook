## Practise with help

**1.** Parallel lines give one angle of $64^\circ$. Find and justify the co-interior angle.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Co-interior angles on parallel lines total $180^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Let the co-interior angle be $x$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
x+64=180 \qquad \text{co-interior angles total }180^\circ
$$

$$
x=180-64=116^\circ
$$

The angle is $116^\circ$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A triangle has angles $52^\circ$, $x^\circ$ and $(x+20)^\circ$. Find $x$ and give a reason.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Mark the known angles and identify their relationship before calculating.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The interior angles of a triangle total $180^\circ$. Use this fact to form an equation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
52+x+(x+20)=180 \qquad \text{triangle angles total }180^\circ
$$

$$
2x+72=180
$$

$$
2x=108
$$

$$
x=54^\circ
$$

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3. Earlier learning.** Work out: $7+5\times3$. Explain which operation you do first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the order of operations.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Calculate $5\times3$ before adding.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$7+5\times3=7+15=22$. Multiplication comes before addition.

</details>

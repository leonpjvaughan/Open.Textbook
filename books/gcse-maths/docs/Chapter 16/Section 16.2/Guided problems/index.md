## Practise with help

**1.** Lines $l$ and $m$ are parallel. A corresponding angle is $72^\circ$. Find its partner.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Write the angle fact that fits the diagram or shape.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Corresponding angles are equal when the lines are parallel.

The partner equals $72^\circ$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The corresponding angle fact gives an angle of $72^\circ$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Co-interior angles are $x^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. Find $x$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Mark the known angles and identify their relationship before calculating.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Co-interior angles on parallel lines total $180^\circ$. Write an equation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Co-interior angles on parallel lines total $180^\circ$.

$$
x+67=180
$$

$$
x=180-67=113^\circ
$$

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3. Earlier learning.** Work out: $7+5\times3$. Explain which operation you do first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the order of operations.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Calculate $5\times3$ before adding.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$7+5\times3=7+15=22$. Multiplication comes before addition.

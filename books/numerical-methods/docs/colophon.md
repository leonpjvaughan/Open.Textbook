# About this book

## What this book is

This is a map of problems. You arrive with a system you have modelled and find
the kind of problem that model became. You leave with the techniques that
solve it, and with the ones that look like they should but do not. The classes
are not chapters to work through. They are boxes to look things up in, and one
system often belongs in more than one.

## Where the content comes from

Every claim about a method rests on a textbook or a paper, and the
reference says whether it is the source of a claim or further reading.

The mathematics is standard numerical analysis. Where a claim is contested, or
where a technique's reputation is better than its record, the page says so and
the source for the doubt is the reference below. A reader who needs the
derivation follows the reference rather than expecting it here.

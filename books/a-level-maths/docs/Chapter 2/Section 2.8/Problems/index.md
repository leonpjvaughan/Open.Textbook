# 2.8 Rational expressions and partial fractions — Practise

Work through these on your own. Factorise before you cancel. For partial
fractions, check the degree of the numerator first, and write one term for each
power of a repeated factor. Add your answer back together to check it.

Both the questions and the answers below come from a set that is checked by
computer every time the book is built.

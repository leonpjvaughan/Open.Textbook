# 5.9 The forms r cos(theta +/- alpha) and r sin(theta +/- alpha) — Practise

Work through these on your own. Find $R$ before you do anything else, then place
$\alpha$ in the correct quadrant. Give angles in radians to three decimal places
unless the question asks for an exact value.

Both the questions and the answers below come from a set that is checked by
computer every time the book is built.

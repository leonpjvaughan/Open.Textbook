# 5.3 The sine, cosine and tangent functions: graphs and periodicity — Practise with help

Write down the period, the amplitude and the range before you draw anything. The
shape of the wave tells you most of what a question needs.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Write down the value of $\sin 0$ and $\cos 0$.
2. Write down the value of $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
3. Write down the value of $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $\sin 0 = 0$ and $\cos 0 = 1$.
2. $\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 1$ and $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{2} = 0$.
3. $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{4} = 1$.

If question 2 was hard, revisit the unit circle before you continue.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** State the amplitude, the period and the range of $y = 4\sin x$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Compare the equation with $y = A\sin(Bx + C) + D$. Here $B = 1$ and $D = 0$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The amplitude is the number in front of the sine. The period is
$\dfrac{2\pi}{B}$ with $B = 1$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Amplitude $= 4$. Period $= \dfrac{2\pi}{1} = 2\pi$. The range is
$-4 \le y \le 4$, because the middle line is the $x$-axis.
</details>

**2.** State the amplitude, the period and the range of
$y = 2\cos(4x) + 3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Identify $A$, $B$ and $D$ first. The middle line is $y = D$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Here $A = 2$, $B = 4$ and $D = 3$. The period is $\dfrac{2\pi}{4}$, and the
range runs from $D - |A|$ to $D + |A|$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Amplitude $= 2$. Period $= \dfrac{2\pi}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The middle line is
$y = 3$, so the range is $1 \le y \le 5$.
</details>

**3.** Write down the period and the phase shift of
$y = \sin\left(2x - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Match the bracket with $Bx + C$. The phase shift is $-\dfrac{C}{B}$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Here $B = 2$ and $C = -\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. Work out the period $\dfrac{2\pi}{2}$,
then the phase shift $-\dfrac{-{\pi}/{3}}{2}$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Period $= \dfrac{2\pi}{2} = \pi$. Phase shift
$= -\dfrac{C}{B} = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$ to the right.
</details>

**4.** Sketch one full period of $y = \tan x$. Mark the asymptotes and the
points where the curve crosses the $x$-axis.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The period of tangent is $\pi$. The asymptotes sit where $\cos x = 0$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

On the interval $\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, mark the
asymptotes at the two ends and the crossing at $x = 0$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The curve rises from below through $(0, 0)$ and climbs without limit towards the
asymptote at $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. It comes up from $-\infty$ just after
$x = -\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The same shape repeats every $\pi$. The curve crosses the
$x$-axis at $x = 0$ and at every multiple of $\pi$.
</details>

**5.** The height of a point on a wheel above its centre is
$h = 1.5\sin(0.8t)$ metres, where $t$ is in seconds. Find the greatest height
and the period of the motion.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The greatest height is the amplitude. The period comes from $B = 0.8$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The amplitude is $1.5$, so the highest point is $1.5$ m above the centre. The
period is $\dfrac{2\pi}{0.8}$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Greatest height $= 1.5$ m. Period
$= \dfrac{2\pi}{0.8} = 2.5\pi \approx 7.85$ s. The point returns to the same
height every $2.5\pi$ seconds.
</details>

**6.** The depth of water at a harbour is modelled by
$d = 3 + 2\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi t}{6}\right)$ metres, where $t$ is in hours.
State the greatest and least depth, and the time for one full cycle.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The middle line is $D = 3$ and the amplitude is $A = 2$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The greatest depth is $3 + 2$ and the least is $3 - 2$. The period is
$\dfrac{2\pi}{\pi/6}$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Greatest depth $= 5$ m. Least depth $= 1$ m. Period
$= \dfrac{2\pi}{\pi/6} = 12$ hours. The sea returns to the same depth every
$12$ hours.
</details>

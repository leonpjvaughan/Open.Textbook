# Glossary — Mechanics

**Acceleration** — the rate of change of velocity.

**Apparent weight** — the normal reaction from the floor on a person in a lift;
it is what the person feels.

**Centre of mass** — the point at which the weight of a body acts.

**Coefficient of friction** — the constant $\mu$ in the friction model
$F \leq \mu R$.

**Constant-acceleration formulae** — the four equations that link initial
velocity, final velocity, acceleration, displacement and time when acceleration
is constant.

**Derived quantity** — a quantity built from the fundamental quantities, with a
unit that comes from its formula.

**Displacement** — the change in position; a vector.

**Distance travelled** — the total length of the path, whatever the direction; a
scalar.

**Equilibrium** — the state of a body when the resultant force on it is zero.

**Force** — a push or a pull; a vector measured in newtons.

**Force diagram** — a diagram that shows one chosen body on its own, with an
arrow for every force acting on it.

**Free fall** — motion under gravity alone, with no air resistance.

**Friction** — a contact force between two surfaces that opposes sliding, or the
tendency to slide.

**Fundamental quantity** — a quantity that is defined directly and is not built
from another, such as length, mass or time.

**Mass** — the amount of matter in a body, measured in kilograms; it does not
change with place.

**Moment** — the turning effect of a force about a point, found from the force
multiplied by the perpendicular distance.

**Newton's first law** — a body stays at rest, or keeps moving with a constant
velocity, unless a resultant force acts on it.

**Newton's second law** — the resultant force on a body equals its mass
multiplied by its acceleration, $F = ma$.

**Newton's third law** — if body A exerts a force on body B, then B exerts a
force of the same size on A, in the opposite direction.

**Normal reaction** — the push from a surface at right angles to that surface.

**Principle of moments** — for a body in equilibrium, the sum of the clockwise
moments about a point equals the sum of the anticlockwise moments.

**Projectile** — an object moving under gravity alone, with no air resistance.

**Resultant force** — a single force that has the same effect as all the forces
acting on a body together.

**SI system** — the international system of units used in science, built from the
base units of length, mass and time.

**Sign convention** — the choice of which direction is positive, used to give
signs to vector quantities in kinematics.

**Speed** — the rate at which distance is covered; a scalar.

**Tension** — the pull in a string or rope.

**Terminal velocity** — the constant velocity reached by a falling body when the
resultant force on it is zero.

**Uniform rod** — a rod whose mass is spread evenly, so its centre of mass is at
its midpoint.

**Velocity** — the rate of change of displacement; a vector.

**Weight** — the force of gravity on a body, found from $W = mg$.

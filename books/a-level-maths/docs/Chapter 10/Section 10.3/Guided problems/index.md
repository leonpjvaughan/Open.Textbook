# 10.3 Vector addition and scalar multiplication — Practise with help

Work each one first, closed book. Decide whether the question wants a vector, a
magnitude or a direction. Add or scale the components before you take any square
root.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $3 \times 4$ and $3 \times (-2)$.
2. Work out $(2 + 5, 3 - 1)$.
3. Find the magnitude of $6\mathbf{i} + 8\mathbf{j}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $3 \times 4 = 12$ and $3 \times (-2) = -6$.
2. Add the matching positions: $(2 + 5, 3 - 1) = (7, 2)$.
3. $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{100} = 10$.

If question 2 was hard, revise adding directed numbers before you continue.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Add $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j}$ and
$\mathbf{b} = 5\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the coefficients of $\mathbf{i}$ together, and the coefficients of
$\mathbf{j}$ together.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The $x$-component is $2 + 5$ and the $y$-component is $3 + (-1)$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} = (2 + 5)\mathbf{i} + (3 + (-1))\mathbf{j} = 7\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j}.
$$

The resultant is $7\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j}$.
</details>

**2.** Add $\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$ and
$\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} - 3\mathbf{j} + 4\mathbf{k}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

There are three rows in each column. Add each row on its own, and keep the signs
with the numbers.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

For the $\mathbf{k}$ row, work out $-1 + 4$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} = (1 + 2)\mathbf{i} + (2 - 3)\mathbf{j} + (-1 + 4)\mathbf{k} = 3\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}.
$$
</details>

**3.** Multiply $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - 3\mathbf{j}$ by $4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The scalar multiplies both components, not only the first.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $4 \times 2$ and $4 \times (-3)$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
4\mathbf{a} = 4(2\mathbf{i} - 3\mathbf{j}) = 8\mathbf{i} - 12\mathbf{j}.
$$

The result is parallel to $\mathbf{a}$ and four times as long.
</details>

**4.** For $\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j}$ and
$\mathbf{b} = 3\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j}$, find $2\mathbf{a} + 3\mathbf{b}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Do the two multiplications first, then add. Keep the two answers separate until
the end.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$2\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j}$ and
$3\mathbf{b} = 9\mathbf{i} - 3\mathbf{j}$. Now add matching components.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
2\mathbf{a} + 3\mathbf{b} = (2\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j}) + (9\mathbf{i} - 3\mathbf{j}) = 11\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j}.
$$
</details>

**5.** For $\mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j}$ and
$\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j}$, find $3\mathbf{a} - 2\mathbf{b}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtracting $2\mathbf{b}$ means adding $-2\mathbf{b}$. Change the sign of both
components of $2\mathbf{b}$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$3\mathbf{a} = 6\mathbf{i} - 3\mathbf{j}$ and
$2\mathbf{b} = 2\mathbf{i} + 6\mathbf{j}$, so $-2\mathbf{b} = -2\mathbf{i} - 6\mathbf{j}$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
3\mathbf{a} - 2\mathbf{b} = (6\mathbf{i} - 3\mathbf{j}) + (-2\mathbf{i} - 6\mathbf{j}) = 4\mathbf{i} - 9\mathbf{j}.
$$
</details>

**6.** Two forces $\mathbf{F}_1 = 5\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j}$ N and
$\mathbf{F}_2 = -2\mathbf{i} + 6\mathbf{j}$ N act at a point. Find the resultant,
its magnitude and its direction from the positive $x$-axis.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

This is two jobs. Add the forces to get the resultant, then use Section 10.2 for
the magnitude and the direction.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$\mathbf{F}_1 + \mathbf{F}_2 = 3\mathbf{i} + 8\mathbf{j}$. Now work out
$\sqrt{3^2 + 8^2}$ and $\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{8}{3}\right)$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
\mathbf{F}_1 + \mathbf{F}_2 = 3\mathbf{i} + 8\mathbf{j}.
$$

$$
|\mathbf{F}_1 + \mathbf{F}_2| = \sqrt{3^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{73} \approx 8.54 \text{ N}.
$$

Both components are positive, so the direction is
$\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{8}{3}\right) = 69.4^\circ$ to $1$ d.p.
</details>

**7.** Two forces $\mathbf{A} = 4\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j}$ N and
$\mathbf{B} = -\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j}$ N act at a point. Find the force
$\mathbf{C}$ that keeps the point still.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

"Keeps the point still" means the three forces are in equilibrium. Their sum
must be the zero vector.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Add $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$, then change the sign of the result.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B} = 3\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j},
\qquad
\mathbf{C} = -(3\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j}) = -3\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j} \text{ N}.
$$
</details>

**8.** A ship sails displacement $6\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j}$ km, then
$-2\mathbf{i} + 5\mathbf{j}$ km. Find the single displacement from the start,
and the distance of the ship from the start.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the two displacements first. The distance is the magnitude of that single
displacement.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The total is $(6 - 2)\mathbf{i} + (-2 + 5)\mathbf{j}$. Then square and add the
components.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
(6\mathbf{i} - 2\mathbf{j}) + (-2\mathbf{i} + 5\mathbf{j}) = 4\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j}.
$$

$$
\text{distance} = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5 \text{ km}.
$$
</details>

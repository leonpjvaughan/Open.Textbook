# 10.5 Vectors in pure mathematics and in context — Practise

Work through these on your own. In a proof, write the two vectors you are
comparing and name the shared point. In a context question, sketch the
situation, choose axes, and interpret your answer with its units.

Both the questions and the answers below come from a set that is checked by
computer every time the book is built.

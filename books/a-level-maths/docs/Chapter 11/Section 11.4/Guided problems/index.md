# 11.4 Scatter diagrams, regression lines and correlation — Practise with help

Work each question closed book first. Describe the correlation in words before
you use the line.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Find the gradient of the line through $(2, 3)$ and $(6, 11)$.
2. Work out $3x + 4$ when $x = 7$.
3. Is the point $(5, 12)$ above or below the line $y = 2x + 1$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $\dfrac{11 - 3}{6 - 2} = \dfrac{8}{4} = 2$.
2. $3(7) + 4 = 25$.
3. On the line, $y = 2(5) + 1 = 11$. The point has $y = 12$, so it lies above
   the line.

If question 1 was hard, revise gradients from Section 3.1.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A scatter diagram plots the mass of a parcel against the cost of posting
it. The points rise from left to right, close to a straight line. Describe the
correlation.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Say whether the pattern is positive, negative or none, and then say whether it
is strong or weak.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The points rise, so the correlation is positive. They lie close to a straight
line, so it is strong.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The correlation is positive and strong. Heavier parcels tend to cost more, and
the points lie close to a straight line.
</details>

**2.** A line of best fit is $y = 5x + 20$, where $x$ is the number of weeks of
practice and $y$ is the score in a test. Interpret the gradient and the
intercept.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The gradient is the number multiplying $x$. The intercept is the number added.
Decide what each one means in the context.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The gradient is 5 and the intercept is 20. Write one sentence about each in the
context of weeks and score.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The gradient is 5, so each extra week of practice is associated with about 5
extra marks. The intercept is 20, so a student with no practice has a predicted
score of 20. Both numbers describe the sample, and neither proves that practice
causes the score to rise.
</details>

**3.** Using the line $y = 5x + 20$, predict the score for 8 weeks of practice.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Substitute $x = 8$ into the equation of the line.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $5(8) + 20$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
y = 5(8) + 20 = 40 + 20 = 60.
$$

The predicted score is 60. The value $x = 8$ lies inside the range of the data,
so this is an interpolation.
</details>

**4.** A scatter diagram of income against age has one group of part-time
workers and one group of full-time workers. Explain why a single line of best
fit is not appropriate.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Compare the levels of the two groups.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Ask where a single line would sit relative to each group.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The two groups are distinct sections of the population, and the full-time group
sits at a higher income than the part-time group. A single line would pass
between the two groups and fit neither of them well. Each group should be
described with its own line, and the groups should be compared separately.
</details>

**5.** A study finds a strong positive correlation between the number of ice
creams sold and the number of drowning accidents. Explain why this does not
prove that ice cream causes drowning.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Think of a variable that could affect both numbers.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Consider the weather. Hot weather increases both ice-cream sales and the number
of people swimming.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The hot weather is a lurking variable. It increases ice-cream sales, and it also
increases the number of people who swim, which increases the number of drowning
accidents. The two variables move together because both depend on the weather.
Correlation does not imply causation.
</details>

**6.** A line of best fit from data with $x$ between 0 and 10 is $y = 2x + 3$.
A student uses it to predict $y$ when $x = 50$. Explain why the prediction should
not be trusted.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Compare the value $x = 50$ with the range of the data.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Say whether the prediction is an interpolation or an extrapolation, then explain
what can go wrong.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The prediction is an extrapolation, because $x = 50$ is far outside the range
0 to 10. The straight-line trend may not continue outside the data. The
relationship may curve, level off or reverse, so the prediction of $103$ is not
reliable.
</details>

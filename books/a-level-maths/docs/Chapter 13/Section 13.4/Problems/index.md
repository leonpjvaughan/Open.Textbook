# 13.4 Links to the binomial, histograms and points of inflection — Practise

Work through these on your own. Check $np > 5$ and $n(1-p) > 5$ before you
approximate, and use the continuity correction. For a general Normal variable,
$\mu - \sigma$ and $\mu + \sigma$ are the points of inflection.

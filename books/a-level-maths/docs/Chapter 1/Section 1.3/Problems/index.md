# 1.3 Proof by exhaustion — Practise

Work through these on your own. For every item, write the list of cases first,
then check them. Say in words why the list is complete, or explain what is
missing from a list that is not. The last item asks you to choose the method: say
which one fits and why before you calculate.

Both the questions and the answers below come from a set that is checked by
computer every time the book is built.

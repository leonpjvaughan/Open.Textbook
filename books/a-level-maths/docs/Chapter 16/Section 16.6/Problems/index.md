# 16.6 Resultant forces and dynamics in a plane — Practise

Work through these on your own. Choose one axis along the acceleration and one
perpendicular to it. Use the perpendicular direction for the normal reaction and
the other direction for the acceleration. Take $g = 9.8$ m s$^{-2}$. Where a
decimal is wanted, give the number of decimal places the question names.

computer every time the book is built.

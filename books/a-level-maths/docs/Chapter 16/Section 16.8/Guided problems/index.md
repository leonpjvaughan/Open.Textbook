# 16.8 Moments in static contexts — Practise with help

Work each one first, closed book. Draw the beam or rod and mark every force with
its distance from the pivot. Take moments about a point that removes an unknown
force. Take $g = 9.8$ m s$^{-2}$.

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $196 \times 2$.
2. Solve $4x = 1053.5$.
3. A uniform rod has length $4.0$ m. How far from one end is its centre of mass?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. $196 \times 2 = 392$.
2. $x = \dfrac{1053.5}{4} = 263.375$.
3. The centre of mass is at the midpoint, $2.0$ m from either end.

If question 3 was hard, remember that a uniform rod balances at its midpoint.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** A force of $25$ N acts at right angles to a lever at a distance of
$0.4$ m from the pivot. Find the moment of the force about the pivot.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The force is at right angles, so the distance given is the perpendicular
distance.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Multiply the force by the distance.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
\text{moment} = 25 \times 0.4 = 10 \text{ N m}.
$$
</details>

**2.** A force of $40$ N acts at the end of a rod of length $1.2$ m. The force
makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with the rod. Find the moment of the force about
the other end.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The perpendicular distance is $1.2\sin 60^\circ$, not the length of the rod.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Work out $1.2\sin 60^\circ$ first, then multiply by $40$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
\text{moment} = 40 \times 1.2 \times \sin 60^\circ \approx 40 \times 1.039 = 41.57 \text{ N m}.
$$
</details>

**3.** A child of mass $40$ kg sits $1.5$ m from the pivot of a seesaw. A second
child of mass $60$ kg sits on the other side. How far from the pivot must the
second child sit to balance the seesaw?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

For balance, the two moments about the pivot are equal.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Set $40g \times 1.5 = 60g \times d$. The $g$ cancels.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
40 \times 1.5 = 60d, \qquad 60 = 60d, \qquad d = 1 \text{ m}.
$$
</details>

**4.** A uniform beam $AB$ has length $5$ m and mass $20$ kg. It rests on
supports at $A$ and $B$. A load of mass $30$ kg rests $2$ m from $A$. Find the
reaction at each support.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The beam's weight acts at its midpoint, $2.5$ m from $A$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Take moments about $A$: $5R_B = 196 \times 2.5 + 294 \times 2$. Then the
reactions add to the total load.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
5R_B = 490 + 588 = 1078, \qquad R_B = 215.6 \text{ N}.
$$

$$
R_A = (196 + 294) - 215.6 = 274.4 \text{ N}.
$$
</details>

**5.** A uniform beam $AB$ has length $3$ m and mass $15$ kg. It rests on
supports at $A$ and $B$. A load of mass $25$ kg rests $1$ m from $A$. Find the
reaction at each support.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The beam weight is $147$ N at $1.5$ m from $A$, and the load is $245$ N at
$1$ m.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Take moments about $A$: $3R_B = 147 \times 1.5 + 245 \times 1$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
3R_B = 220.5 + 245 = 465.5, \qquad R_B \approx 155.17 \text{ N}.
$$

$$
R_A = (147 + 245) - 155.17 \approx 236.83 \text{ N}.
$$
</details>

**6.** A uniform rod $AB$ has mass $10$ kg and length $3$ m. It is hinged at $A$
and held horizontal by a vertical string at $B$. Find the tension in the string.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Take moments about the hinge, so the hinge force does not appear in the
equation.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The weight is $98$ N at $1.5$ m from $A$. Set $3T = 98 \times 1.5$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
T = \frac{98 \times 1.5}{3} = \frac{147}{3} = 49 \text{ N}.
$$
</details>

**7.** A non-uniform rod $AB$ has length $2$ m and mass $5$ kg. It is pivoted at
its centre and balances when a mass of $1$ kg is placed at $B$. Find the distance
of the centre of mass of the rod from $A$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The $1$ kg mass is $1$ m from the pivot. Its moment must be balanced by the rod's
weight acting on the other side of the pivot.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Set $5g\,x = 1g \times 1$, so $x = 0.2$ m from the pivot. Then subtract from
$1$ m.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
5x = 1, \qquad x = 0.2 \text{ m}.
$$

The centre of mass is $1 - 0.2 = 0.8$ m from $A$.
</details>

**8.** A uniform plank $AB$ has length $6$ m and mass $30$ kg. It rests on
supports at $A$ and $B$. A person of mass $60$ kg stands $4$ m from $A$. Find the
reaction at each support.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The plank's weight acts $3$ m from $A$. The person's weight acts $4$ m from $A$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Take moments about $A$: $6R_B = 294 \times 3 + 588 \times 4$.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
6R_B = 882 + 2352 = 3234, \qquad R_B = 539 \text{ N}.
$$

$$
R_A = (294 + 588) - 539 = 343 \text{ N}.
$$
</details>

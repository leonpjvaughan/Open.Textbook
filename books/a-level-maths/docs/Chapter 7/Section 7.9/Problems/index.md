# 7.9 Implicit and parametric differentiation — Practise

Work through these on your own. For an implicit relation, differentiate both
sides with respect to $x$ and collect the $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ terms. For a
parametric curve, remember that the gradient is
$\dfrac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}$.

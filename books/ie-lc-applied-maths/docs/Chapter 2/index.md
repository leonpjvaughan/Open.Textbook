# Chapter 2: Networks and graphs

Networks as models of connection — vertices, edges, weights — and the graphs, matrices and algorithms used to reason about them.

## The chapter problem

**Wiring the new building.** You will connect a set of rooms with the least total length of cable, then find the shortest route between two of them. By the end of the chapter you can say which algorithm answers which question.

**Where it is used:** Transport, delivery routes, cabling, broadband and utility networks.

**Your turn to connect it:** Which journey you take, or which network reaches your area, is decided by a problem like this one.

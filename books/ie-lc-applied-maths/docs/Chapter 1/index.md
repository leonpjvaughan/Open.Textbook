# Chapter 1: What mathematical modelling is

The problem-solving cycle: formulating a problem, translating it into mathematics, computing a solution, evaluating it, and going round again.

## The chapter problem

**How long is the queue?.** You will model a queue — how fast people arrive, how fast they are served — and predict how long the wait gets. By the end of the chapter you can say which assumption your answer depends on most.

**Where it is used:** Every other chapter in this book, and the 20% modelling project.

**Your turn to connect it:** What queue or delay in your own week would you like to be able to predict?

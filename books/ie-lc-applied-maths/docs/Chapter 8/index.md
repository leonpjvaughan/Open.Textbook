# Chapter 8: Modelling a changing world

Difference and differential equations as models of change — growth, decay, recurrence and feedback.

## The chapter problem

**Will the lake ever clear?.** A pollutant enters a lake and is washed out at a rate proportional to how much is there. You will model it, solve the model, and say how long the lake takes to reach a safe level — and what the model assumes that might not hold.

**Where it is used:** Population, disease, pollution, cooling, radioactive decay and finance.

**Your turn to connect it:** What changes in your life or your area at a rate set by how much of it there already is?

# 4.6 Solving linear equations — Learn

**Prerequisites:** Section 4.5 — simplifying and substituting.

**Objectives:** solve a linear equation with the unknown on one side or both;
check a solution by substitution; solve the break-even equation.

By the end of this section you can find the value of the unknown that makes an
equation true, and prove that it is right.

## An equation is a balance

An **equation** says that two expressions are equal. The equals sign is the pivot
of a balance, and whatever you do to one side you must do to the other.

$$4x + 5 = 17$$

The value of $x$ that makes the two sides equal is the **solution**. To find it,
undo the operations one at a time, working backwards.

$$4x + 5 = 17$$

Subtract $5$ from both sides:

$$4x = 12$$

Divide both sides by $4$:

$$x = 3$$

**Example 1.** Check the solution in the original equation.

$$4(3) + 5 = 12 + 5 = 17 \quad\text{Correct.}$$

## The unknown on both sides

When the unknown appears on both sides, collect it on one side first.

**Example 2.** Solve $3x - 7 = 2x + 5$.

Subtract $2x$ from both sides:

$$x - 7 = 5$$

Add $7$ to both sides:

$$x = 12$$

Check: the left side is $3(12) - 7 = 29$ and the right side is $2(12) + 5 = 29$.
Both sides agree.

**Example 3.** Solve $240 + 4n = 10n$.

The unknown is on both sides, so subtract $4n$:

$$240 = 6n$$

Divide by $6$:

$$n = 40$$

At $40$ candles the cost and the income are equal. This is the break-even point,
and it is the exact answer to the estimate read from the graph in Section 4.2.

**Example 4.** Solve $5(x - 2) = 3x + 4$.

Expand the bracket first:

$$5x - 10 = 3x + 4$$

Subtract $3x$: $2x - 10 = 4$. Add $10$: $2x = 14$. Divide by $2$: $x = 7$.

> **Watch out.** A term that moves across the equals sign changes sign, because
> you subtracted it from both sides. Also divide **every** term by the same
> number, not only the first one. After solving, substitute the answer back into
> the original equation: that one line catches almost every mistake.

## Progress check

Back to the chapter problem. Solve the break-even equation

$$240 + 4n = 10n$$

step by step, and check your answer by substituting it back into both sides.

Then work out the cost and the income at that value of $n$, and confirm they are
equal. Say in one sentence what the answer means for the business.

## You should now be able to

- solve a linear equation by undoing the operations in reverse order;
- collect the unknown on one side when it appears on both;
- expand a bracket before solving;
- check a solution by substitution.

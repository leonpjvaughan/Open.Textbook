# 4.1 Generating expressions from patterns — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out $7 \times 6 + 5$.
2. Work out $240 + 4 \times 10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. Multiply first: $42 + 5 = 47$.
2. Multiply first: $240 + 40 = 280$. This is the cost of $10$ candles, and the
   multiplication happens before the addition.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find the next two terms of $6, 11, 16, 21, \dots$ and state the gap.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract each term from the one after it.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$11 - 6 = 5$ and $16 - 11 = 5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The gap is $5$ every time, so the next two terms are $26$ and $31$.

A constant gap means the sequence is built by adding the same amount at each
step.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Write an expression for the $n$th term of $6, 11, 16, 21, \dots$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The gap is the coefficient of $n$. Then compare at $n = 1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The gap is $5$, so start with $5n$. At $n = 1$, $5n = 5$, but the first term is
$6$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$5n + 1$$

Check at $n = 3$: $5(3) + 1 = 16$. Correct.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Find the twelfth term of $4, 9, 14, \dots$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the expression first, then substitute $n = 12$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The gap is $5$, and at $n = 1$, $5n = 5$ is one too many, so the expression is
$5n - 1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$5(12) - 1 = 59$$

The twelfth term is $59$. Check with the gap: $4 + 11 \times 5 = 4 + 55 = 59$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A tile pattern has $2$ tiles in the first shape, $5$ in the second and $8$
in the third. Find an expression for the number of tiles in the $n$th shape, then
find the number in the twentieth shape.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The gap is the coefficient. Compare at $n = 1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The gap is $3$, and at $n = 1$, $3n = 3$, but the first shape has $2$ tiles, so
subtract $1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$3n - 1$$

The twentieth shape has $3(20) - 1 = 59$ tiles.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** A plumber charges a call-out fee of €50, plus €30 for each hour of work.
Write an expression for the cost of $n$ hours, then work out the cost of $4$
hours.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Which amount is paid once, and which is paid for every hour?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

€50 is paid once, so it is the constant. €30 is paid each hour, so it is the
coefficient.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$50 + 30n$$

For $4$ hours: $50 + 30(4) = 50 + 120 = 170$, which is €170.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Explain the difference between the coefficient and the constant in an
expression such as $4n + 7$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Which number changes when $n$ changes?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The coefficient multiplies the variable; the constant stands alone.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

In $4n + 7$ the coefficient is $4$. It multiplies the variable $n$, so it sets
how fast the value grows. The constant is $7$. It does not depend on $n$, so it
is the value when $n = 0$.

Changing $n$ from $1$ to $2$ raises $4n + 7$ by $4$, the coefficient. It never
changes the constant.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from Chapter 2

**7.** A price of €240 is increased by $10\%$. Work out the new price.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

An increase of $10\%$ multiplies the price by $1.10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$240 \times 1.10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$240 \times 1.10 = 264$$

The new price is €264.

The €240 here is the same fixed cost as in the chapter problem. A percentage
change and a fixed change behave differently, and this chapter uses both.

</details>
</details>
</details>

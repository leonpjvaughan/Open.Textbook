# 4.9 Linear inequalities — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Solve $3x = 12$.
2. Is $-2 > -5$ true or false?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $x = 4$.
2. True. On the number line $-2$ is to the right of $-5$, so it is the greater
   number. Multiplying by a negative is what reverses an order like this.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Solve $2x + 3 < 11$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract $3$, then divide by $2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$2x < 8$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$x < 4$$

Dividing by $2$, which is positive, leaves the sign unchanged. Any number below
$4$ satisfies the inequality.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Solve $5x - 2 \geq 13$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add $2$ to both sides first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$5x \geq 15$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$x \geq 3$$

The circle at $3$ is filled, because $3$ itself satisfies the inequality:
$5(3) - 2 = 13$, which is equal to $13$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Solve $-2x > 8$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Dividing by a negative reverses the sign.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$x < -4$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The sign reverses because $-2$ is negative. Check with $x = -5$:
$-2(-5) = 10$, and $10 > 8$. Correct.

If the sign had been left as $x > -4$, then $x = 0$ would appear to be a
solution, but $-2(0) = 0$, which is not greater than $8$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Solve $240 + 4n < 10n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract $4n$ from both sides.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$240 < 6n$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$n > 40$$

The business makes a profit when it sells more than $40$ candles. At exactly
$40$ candles the cost and income are equal, so the profit is zero and the
inequality is strict.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Solve $3(x - 1) \leq 12$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide both sides by $3$, or expand first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$x - 1 \leq 4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$x \leq 5$$

The circle at $5$ is filled, because $5$ is included. The arrow points left.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Solve $7 - x \geq 2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract $7$, then divide by $-1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$-x \geq -5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Dividing by $-1$ reverses the sign:

$$x \leq 5$$

Check with $x = 5$: $7 - 5 = 2$, which equals $2$. Correct. Check with $x = 0$:
$7 - 0 = 7 \geq 2$. Correct.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from Chapter 1

**7.** Solve $|x| < 4$ and describe the solution set.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The absolute value is the distance from zero.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Which numbers are less than $4$ units from $0$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The solutions are the numbers between $-4$ and $4$:

$$-4 < x < 4$$

The endpoints are not included, because the inequality is strict. Section 4.10
solves inequalities with a modulus written as $|x - a| < b$, which shifts this
interval along the number line.

</details>
</details>
</details>

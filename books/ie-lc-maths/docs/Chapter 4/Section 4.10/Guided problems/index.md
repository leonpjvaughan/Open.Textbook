# 4.10 Inequalities and the modulus — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Solve $x^2 - 4 = 0$.
2. Work out $|-3|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $(x - 2)(x + 2) = 0$, so $x = 2$ or $x = -2$. These are the boundary values
   of the quadratic inequality.
2. $|-3| = 3$. The modulus is a distance, and a distance is never negative.

</details>

:::figure warm-up-gate


## Practise with help

**1.** Solve $x^2 - 4 < 0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the boundary values, then decide where the parabola is below the axis.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The roots are $-2$ and $2$, and the parabola $y = x^2 - 4$ opens upward.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Between the two roots the parabola is below the horizontal axis, so

$$-2 < x < 2$$

Check with $x = 0$: $0^2 - 4 = -4$, which is less than $0$. Correct.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 > 0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Factorise, then decide where the upward parabola is above the axis.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$(x - 2)(x - 3) = 0$, so the roots are $2$ and $3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The parabola opens upward, so it is above the axis outside the roots and below it
between them:

$$x < 2 \quad\text{or}\quad x > 3$$

Check with $x = 0$: $0 - 0 + 6 = 6 > 0$. Correct. Check with $x = 2.5$:
$6.25 - 12.5 + 6 = -0.25 < 0$, so $2.5$ is not a solution.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Solve $|x - 3| < 2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

This asks for the numbers less than $2$ from $3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$3 - 2 < x < 3 + 2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$1 < x < 5$$

The endpoints are not included, because the inequality is strict.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Solve $|x - 1| > 3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

This asks for the numbers more than $3$ from $1$. There are two ranges.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$x < 1 - 3$ or $x > 1 + 3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$x < -2 \quad\text{or}\quad x > 4$$

Check with $x = 5$: $|5 - 1| = 4 > 3$. Correct. Check with $x = 0$:
$|0 - 1| = 1$, which is not greater than $3$, so $0$ is not a solution.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Solve the profit inequality $-0.1n^2 + 10n - 240 > 0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Multiply by $-10$ to clear the decimal, and reverse the sign.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$n^2 - 100n + 2400 < 0$, with roots $40$ and $60$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The parabola opens upward, so the expression is negative between the roots:

$$40 < n < 60$$

The business makes a profit when it sells more than $40$ and fewer than $60$
candles.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Explain why $|x - a| > b$ gives two ranges while $|x - a| < b$ gives one.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Think about points near $a$ and points far from $a$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

"Less than $b$ from $a$" is a band around $a$. "More than $b$ from $a$" is
everything outside that band.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The numbers less than $b$ from $a$ form a single band, from $a - b$ to
$a + b$. That is one interval.

The numbers more than $b$ from $a$ are everything outside that band. There is one
range to the left of $a - b$ and another to the right of $a + b$, so the solution
is two ranges:

$$x < a - b \quad\text{or}\quad x > a + b$$

The sign decides whether the answer is the band or everything outside it.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from Chapter 1

**7.** Solve $|x - 2| = 5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

$x$ is $5$ units from $2$, in either direction.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$x - 2 = 5$ or $x - 2 = -5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$x = 7 \quad\text{or}\quad x = -3$$

An equation with a modulus gives the two endpoints of an inequality with the same
modulus. Those endpoints are what this section works between.

</details>
</details>
</details>

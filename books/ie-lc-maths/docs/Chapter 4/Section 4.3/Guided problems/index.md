# 4.3 Finding a formula from a pattern — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Find the gap in the sequence $7, 12, 17, \dots$
2. Work out $4 \times 25$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $12 - 7 = 5$, and $17 - 12 = 5$, so the gap is $5$.
2. $4 \times 25 = 100$. This multiplication is the middle step when a formula
   such as $240 + 4n$ is evaluated at $n = 25$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find a formula for the $n$th term of $7, 11, 15, 19, \dots$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the gap, then step back to $n = 0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The gap is $4$, so the formula starts with $4n$. Stepping back from $7$ gives
$7 - 4 = 3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4n + 3$$

Check at $n = 4$: $4(4) + 3 = 19$. Correct.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Find a formula for $20, 17, 14, 11, \dots$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The gap is negative, because the sequence decreases.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The gap is $-3$, so the formula starts with $-3n$. Stepping back from $20$ gives
$20 - (-3) = 23$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$-3n + 23$$

Check at $n = 4$: $-3(4) + 23 = 11$. Correct.

A decreasing sequence has a negative coefficient. That is allowed, and the
constant is still found by stepping back to $n = 0$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A table gives the values below. Find the formula.

| $n$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | $6$ | $13$ | $20$ | $27$ |

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The gap is the coefficient, and the $n = 0$ column is the constant.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The gap is $7$, and the value at $n = 0$ is $6$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$y = 7n + 6$$

Check at $n = 3$: $7(3) + 6 = 27$. Correct.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A pattern starts at $12$ and adds $7$ each time. Write the formula in words
and in symbols.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Which amount is paid once, and which is added every time?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

At $n = 0$ the value is $12 - 7 = 5$, so the constant is $5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

In words: **value = $5$ + $7$ × the term number**.

In symbols: $T_n = 7n + 5$.

Check at $n = 1$: $7 + 5 = 12$. Correct.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Use $C = 240 + 4n$ to find the cost of $25$ candles.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Substitute $n = 25$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$4 \times 25 = 100$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$C = 240 + 100 = €340$$

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Use $C = 240 + 4n$ to find the number of candles that cost €300.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Set the formula equal to $300$ and solve.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$240 + 4n = 300$, so $4n = 60$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$4n = 60 \quad\Rightarrow\quad n = 15$$

So $15$ candles cost €300.

Check: $240 + 4(15) = 240 + 60 = 300$. Correct.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from Chapter 2

**7.** A jacket costs €80. In a sale the price falls by $25\%$. Work out the sale
price.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

A fall of $25\%$ leaves $75\%$ of the price.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$80 \times 0.75$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$80 \times 0.75 = €60$$

The sale price is €60. A percentage fall and a fixed fall behave differently, and
a formula records which one a situation uses.

</details>
</details>
</details>

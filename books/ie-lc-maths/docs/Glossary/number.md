# Glossary — Number

**Absolute value** — the distance of a number from zero, written $|x|$.

**Area** — the amount of surface a flat shape covers, measured in square units.

**Average rate of change** — the change in one quantity divided by the change in
another, taken across an interval.

**Circumference** — the distance around a circle.

**Compound interest** — interest worked out each time on the original amount and
on the interest already added.

**Conjugate** — the expression formed by changing the sign between two terms,
used to rationalise a denominator.

**Cost price** — the amount paid for an item before any mark-up.

**Cube** — a solid with six square faces of equal size.

**Cuboid** — a solid with six rectangular faces.

**Curved surface area** — the area of the curved part of a solid, such as the
side of a cylinder.

**Deduction** — an amount taken from gross pay before it is paid.

**Depreciating** — falling in value over time.

**Diameter** — a straight line through a circle's centre joining two points on
its circumference.

**Discount** — an amount taken off a price.

**Discounting** — finding the present value of a future amount.

**Gross pay** — the total pay before any deductions.

**Height** — the perpendicular distance from the base of a shape to its highest
point.

**Hemisphere** — half of a sphere.

**Higher rate** — the rate of income tax paid on income above the standard rate
cut-off.

**Income tax** — tax taken from pay by the government.

**Index (plural indices)** — a power, such as the $3$ in $2^3=8$.

**Integers** — the whole numbers, positive, negative or zero.

**Irrational number** — a number that cannot be written as a fraction of two
integers.

**Loss** — a negative profit: the money taken is less than the money spent.

**Margin** — profit written as a percentage of the selling price.

**Mark-up** — the amount added to the cost price to set the selling price.

**Multiplier** — the single number by which you multiply to apply a percentage
change.

**Natural numbers** — the counting numbers, starting at 1.

**Net** — a flat pattern that folds up to make a solid.

**Net pay** — the pay left after tax and other deductions.

**Order of magnitude** — the nearest power of ten to a number.

**Percentage change** — the increase or decrease written as a percentage of the
original amount.

**Percentage error** — the difference between a measured value and a true value,
written as a percentage of the true value.

**Perimeter** — the total distance around the edge of a flat shape.

**Power** — a way of writing repeated multiplication, such as $2^3$.

**Present value** — the amount that would grow to a future sum at a given rate
of interest.

**Principal** — the original amount of money borrowed or invested.

**Prism** — a solid with matching parallel ends and a constant cross-section.

**Profit** — money left after costs are subtracted from the money taken.

**Radius** — the distance from the centre of a circle to its edge.

**Rationalised** — written with no surd in the denominator.

**Rational number** — a number that can be written as a fraction of two
integers.

**Real numbers** — all the numbers on the number line, rational and irrational
together.

**Recurring decimal** — a decimal in which a digit or a block of digits repeats
for ever.

**Scale factor** — the multiplier that changes a length from one similar shape
to another.

**Scientific notation** — a way to write a number as $a\times10^n$, where $1\leq
a<10$ and $n$ is an integer.

**Selling price** — the amount a customer pays for an item.

**Significant figure** — a digit that carries meaning in a number, counted from
the first non-zero digit.

**Similar** — having the same shape but not necessarily the same size.

**Simple interest** — interest calculated each time on the original amount only.

**Standard rate** — the rate of income tax paid on income up to the standard
rate cut-off.

**Surd** — a root that cannot be simplified to a whole number, such as
$\sqrt{2}$.

**Surface area** — the total area of all the faces of a solid.

**Tax credit** — an amount that reduces the income tax you pay.

**Terminating decimal** — a decimal that stops after a fixed number of digits.

**Tolerance** — the range of values within which a measurement is accepted.

**Total surface area** — the sum of the areas of every face of a solid.

**Triangular prism** — a prism whose ends are triangles.

**Universal Social Charge** — a charge on income in Ireland, paid in addition to
income tax.

**Volume** — the amount of space a solid occupies, measured in cubic units.

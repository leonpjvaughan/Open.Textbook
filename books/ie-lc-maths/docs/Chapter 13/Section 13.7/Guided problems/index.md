# 13.7 Dividing a line segment in a given ratio — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory before you start.

1. Find the midpoint of $(0, 0)$ and $(8, 6)$.
2. Work out $\frac{2}{5} \times 10$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. The midpoint is $(4, 3)$. This is the section formula with the ratio $1 : 1$.
2. $\frac{2}{5} \times 10 = 4$. A ratio of $2 : 3$ splits the segment into fifths,
   and this is the fraction you move along it.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Find the point that divides the segment from $(2, 4)$ to $(8, 10)$ in the
ratio $1 : 2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use $\frac{n x_1 + m x_2}{m + n}$ with $m = 1$ and $n = 2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The denominator is $m + n = 3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$P = \left(\frac{2 \times 2 + 1 \times 8}{3}, \frac{2 \times 4 + 1 \times 10}{3}\right)
= \left(\frac{12}{3}, \frac{18}{3}\right) = (4, 6)$$

The point is $(4, 6)$. It is one third of the way from $(2, 4)$ to $(8, 10)$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** A bus stop divides the main road from $A(-1, 1)$ to $B(7, 7)$ in the ratio
$2 : 3$. Find its coordinates.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Here $m = 2$ and $n = 3$, so the denominator is $5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$A$ is multiplied by $3$ and $B$ is multiplied by $2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$P = \left(\frac{3 \times (-1) + 2 \times 7}{5}, \frac{3 \times 1 + 2 \times 7}{5}\right)
= \left(\frac{11}{5}, \frac{17}{5}\right)$$

The bus stop is at $\left(\frac{11}{5}, \frac{17}{5}\right) = (2.2, 3.4)$.

It is closer to $A$, which matches the ratio $2 : 3$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Find the point that divides the segment from $(0, 0)$ to $(6, 9)$ in the
ratio $2 : 1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Here the first part of the ratio is the larger one.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$m = 2$, $n = 1$, so $P = \left(\frac{1 \times 0 + 2 \times 6}{3},
\frac{1 \times 0 + 2 \times 9}{3}\right)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$P = \left(\frac{12}{3}, \frac{18}{3}\right) = (4, 6)$$

The point is $(4, 6)$, two thirds of the way along. It is closer to the second
endpoint, which is what the larger first part of the ratio means.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Find the two points that divide the segment from $(0, 0)$ to $(9, 6)$ into
three equal parts.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the ratios $1 : 2$ and $2 : 1$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The first point is at $\left(\frac{9}{3}, \frac{6}{3}\right)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

First point, ratio $1 : 2$: $\left(\frac{2 \times 0 + 1 \times 9}{3},
\frac{2 \times 0 + 1 \times 6}{3}\right) = (3, 2)$.

Second point, ratio $2 : 1$: $\left(\frac{1 \times 0 + 2 \times 9}{3},
\frac{1 \times 0 + 2 \times 6}{3}\right) = (6, 4)$.

The two points are $(3, 2)$ and $(6, 4)$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** The point $P(3, 5)$ divides the segment from $A(1, 1)$ to $B$ in the ratio
$1 : 2$. Find $B$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

$AP$ is one part and $PB$ is two parts, so $AB$ is three times $AP$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$\vec{AP} = (3 - 1, 5 - 1) = (2, 4)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$B = A + 3\,\vec{AP} = (1 + 3 \times 2, 1 + 3 \times 4) = (7, 13)$.

Check the ratio: $AP = \sqrt{2^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{20}$ and
$PB = \sqrt{4^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{80}$, so $PB = 2\,AP$ and the ratio is $1 : 2$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Find the ratio in which $(2, 3)$ divides the segment from $(0, 0)$ to
$(8, 12)$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

What fraction of the way along is the point?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Compare the $x$-coordinates: $\frac{2}{8}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$\frac{2}{8} = \frac{1}{4}$ and $\frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}$, so the point is one
quarter of the way along. Then $AP : PB = 1 : 3$.

The same fraction in both coordinates is a good check. If the two fractions
disagree, the point is not on the segment.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from Chapter 4

**7.** Divide €120 in the ratio $2 : 3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

There are five parts in total.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

One part is $120 \div 5 = 24$, which is €24.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

One part is €24, so the shares are $2 \times 24 = 48$ and $3 \times 24 = 72$, which are €48 and €72.

The section formula does the same thing with two coordinates instead of one
amount. Both are weighted averages.

</details>
</details>
</details>

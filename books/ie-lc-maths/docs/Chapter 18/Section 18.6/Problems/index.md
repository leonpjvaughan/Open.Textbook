# 18.6 Geometric applications of vectors — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Write each side as a vector before you decide what to compare. A right angle is a zero dot
product, an equal pair of opposite sides makes a parallelogram, and parallel displacements
with a shared point make three points collinear.

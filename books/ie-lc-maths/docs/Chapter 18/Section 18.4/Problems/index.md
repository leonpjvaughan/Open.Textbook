# 18.4 Perpendicularity and the angle between vectors — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Use the dot product for every right-angle test. For an angle, use
$\cos\theta = \dfrac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|}$, and give a
special angle exactly.

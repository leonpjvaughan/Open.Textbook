# 2.5 Present value — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Work out €2,000 × $1.05^2$.
2. Write $6\%$ as a decimal.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $2\,000 \times 1.05^2 = 2\,205$. The answer is €2,205.
2. $6\% = 0.06$. The divisor in a present-value calculation uses the decimal.

Both answers come from Section 2.4, and both are used again here.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** €4,200 is due in one year. Money earns $5\%$ a year. Work out the present
value.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the future amount by $1.05$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{4\,200}{1.05}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{4\,200}{1.05} = 4\,000$$

The present value is €4,000. Invested today at $5\%$, it becomes €4,200 in a year.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** €5,618 is due in 2 years. Money earns $6\%$ a year. Work out the present
value.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by $1.06$ once for each year, so divide by $1.06^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{5\,618}{1.06^2}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{5\,618}{1.06^2} = 5\,000$$

The present value is €5,000. The divisor is $1.06^2$ and not $1.12$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** You may take €5,000 now or €5,400 in one year. Money earns $5\%$ a year.
Work out the present value of the €5,400 and say which offer is better.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Bring the €5,400 back to today, then compare it with the €5,000.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{5\,400}{1.05}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{5\,400}{1.05} = 5\,142.86$$

The €5,400 in a year is worth €5,142.86 today, to the nearest cent. That is more
than €5,000, so the later payment is the better offer.

Compare the present values, not €5,400 with €5,000.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** A loan of €8,000 is repaid as a single payment of €8,988.80 after 2 years.
Work out the annual rate.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide the repayment by €8,000, then take the square root.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{8\,988.80}{8\,000} = 1.1236$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$(1 + i)^2 = 1.1236$$

$$1 + i = \sqrt{1.1236} = 1.06$$

So $i = 0.06$, and the rate is $6\%$ a year. Halving the total change would give
$6.18\%$, and that is wrong.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** An investment will be worth €9,261 in 3 years. Money earns $5\%$ a year.
Work out its value today.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide by $1.05^3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{9\,261}{1.05^3}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{9\,261}{1.05^3} = 8\,000$$

The investment is worth €8,000 today. The difference of €1,261 is the reward for
waiting three years.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## The chapter problem

**6.** Niamh can pay €11,070 now or €12,000 in 2 years. Her money earns $3\%$ a
year. Work out the present value of the €12,000.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Divide €12,000 by $1.03^2$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$\frac{12\,000}{1.03^2}$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{12\,000}{1.03^2} = 11\,311.15$$

The present value is €11,311.15 to the nearest cent. That is more than €11,070, so
paying now is cheaper.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**7.** Work out how much cheaper paying €11,070 now is than paying €12,000 in 2
years, in today's money.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use the present value you worked out in question 6.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$$11\,311.15 - 11\,070$$

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$11\,311.15 - 11\,070 = 241.15$$

Paying now is cheaper by €241.15 in today's money.

Check it by looking forward: $11\,070 \times 1.03^2 = 11\,744.16$, which is
€255.84 short of the €12,000. Both routes give the same decision.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**8.** Explain why a sum of money received in the future is worth less today.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

What could you do with the money if you had it now?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Money held today can earn interest, so it grows into a larger amount later.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Money you hold today can earn interest. At $5\%$ a year, €1,000 today becomes
€1,050 in a year. So €1,000 today and €1,050 in a year are equivalent, and a
promise of €1,050 in a year is worth €1,000 today.

To find a present value, divide the future amount by the growth factor once for
each year:

$$P = \frac{F}{(1 + i)^t}$$

Dividing brings a future amount back to today, which is the reverse of compound
interest.

</details>
</details>
</details>

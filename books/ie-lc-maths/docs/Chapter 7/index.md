# Chapter 7: Differentiation

The derivative as a rate of change, the rules of differentiation, and what the derivative tells you about a graph.

## The chapter problem

**When is the tank filling fastest?.** You will model the amount in a tank as a function of time, differentiate it, and use the derivative to answer a question about the rate. By the end of the chapter the derivative is a tool you reach for, not a procedure you remember.

**Where it is used:** Rates, optimisation, motion, growth and any quantity that changes.

**Your turn to connect it:** Where would knowing how fast something is changing right now be more useful than knowing its value?

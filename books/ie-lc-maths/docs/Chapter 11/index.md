# Chapter 11: Statistics

Collecting, representing and analysing data, and the sampling variability that limits what a sample can tell you.

## The chapter problem

**The poll that got it wrong.** A poll predicted one result and the vote gave another. You will work out how a sample of that size could be that far out, and by the end of the chapter you can say what a margin of error does and does not promise.

**Where it is used:** Surveys, polls, quality control, and every claim made from a sample.

**Your turn to connect it:** Where have you seen a percentage from a survey used to argue for something?

# 16.3 Similar triangles — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Two angles of a triangle are $35^\circ$ and $90^\circ$. Find the third angle.
2. A triangle has sides of $3$, $4$ and $5$ cm. A second triangle has sides of $6$, $8$
   and $10$ cm. Is the second triangle similar to the first?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $180 - 35 - 90 = 55^\circ$. The three angles of a triangle add to $180^\circ$.
2. Yes. Each side of the second triangle is twice the matching side of the first, so the
   scale factor is $2$.

If either answer felt uncertain, look again at Section 15.4 and Chapter 3 before you carry
on.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR$. Given $|AB| = 6$ cm, $|BC| = 9$ cm and
$|PQ| = 4$ cm, find $|QR|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the scale factor from $\triangle ABC$ to $\triangle PQR$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$AB$ matches $PQ$, so the scale factor is $\frac{4}{6}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{|PQ|}{|AB|} = \frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}$$

The side $QR$ matches $BC$, so
$$|QR| = \frac{2}{3} \times 9 = 6 \text{ cm}.$$

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** In $\triangle ABC$, $D$ is on $AB$, $E$ is on $AC$, and $DE \parallel BC$. Given
$|AD| = 3$ cm, $|DB| = 2$ cm and $|AE| = 4.5$ cm, find $|EC|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The parallel line cuts the two sides in the same ratio.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $\frac{|AD|}{|DB|} = \frac{|AE|}{|EC|}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{3}{2} = \frac{4.5}{|EC|}$$

$$3|EC| = 9 \qquad |EC| = 3 \text{ cm}$$

Check: $\frac{3}{2} = 1.5$ and $\frac{4.5}{3} = 1.5$, so the two ratios agree.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** A post $2$ m tall casts a shadow $3$ m long. At the same time a tree casts a shadow
$12$ m long. Find the height of the tree.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The post and its shadow make a triangle similar to the tree and its shadow.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Put the heights over the shadows.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The two triangles are similar by the AA rule, because the right angles are equal and the
Sun makes the same angle with each shadow.

$$\frac{h}{2} = \frac{12}{3}$$

$$h = 2 \times 4 = 8 \text{ m}$$

The tree is $8$ m tall.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle A'B'C'$, with $|AB| = 5$ cm and $|AC| = 7$ cm.
Given $|A'B'| = 15$ cm, find $|A'C'|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the scale factor from the two sides you know.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$AB$ matches $A'B'$, so the scale factor is $3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\frac{|A'B'|}{|AB|} = \frac{15}{5} = 3$$

$$|A'C'| = 3 \times |AC| = 3 \times 7 = 21 \text{ cm}$$

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of $8$ cm and $12$ cm. A second side
of the smaller triangle is $10$ cm. Find the matching side of the larger triangle.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The scale factor is the ratio of the two corresponding sides you know.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$\frac{12}{8} = 1.5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The scale factor from the smaller triangle to the larger is $1.5$, so the matching side is

$$1.5 \times 10 = 15 \text{ cm}.$$

Check: $\frac{15}{10} = 1.5$, which agrees with $\frac{12}{8}$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Prove the AA rule: if two angles of one triangle are equal to two angles of
another, then the two triangles are similar.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

You need the third pair of angles.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Use the angle sum of a triangle.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Let the first triangle have angles $A$ and $B$, and let the second have matching angles
$A'$ and $B'$, with $|\angle A| = |\angle A'|$ and $|\angle B| = |\angle B'|$.

The angles of a triangle add to $180^\circ$, so

$$|\angle C| = 180^\circ - |\angle A| - |\angle B| = 180^\circ - |\angle A'| - |\angle B'| = |\angle C'|.$$

The third angles are equal too, so all three pairs of angles match and the triangles are
similar.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from Chapter 14

**7.** A triangle has sides of $4$, $5$ and $7$ cm. It is enlarged by a scale factor of
$3$. Find the sides of the image, and say whether the image is similar to the original.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

An enlargement multiplies every length by the scale factor.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Multiply each side by $3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The sides of the image are

$$3 \times 4 = 12, \qquad 3 \times 5 = 15, \qquad 3 \times 7 = 21 \text{ cm}.$$

The image is similar to the original. An enlargement keeps every angle the same, so the
two triangles have equal angles, and the sides are all multiplied by the same factor.

</details>
</details>
</details>

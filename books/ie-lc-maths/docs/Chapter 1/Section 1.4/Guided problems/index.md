# 1.4 Absolute value — Practise with help

## Warm-up: remember

1. Solve $x + 3 = 5$.
2. Which number is larger, $-8$ or $-3$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers and feedback</summary>

1. $x = 2$.
2. $-3$ is larger. On the number line, $-3$ is to the right of $-8$.

</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Work out $|-9|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

How far is $-9$ from $0$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Distance is not signed.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$|-9| = 9$. The number $-9$ is $9$ units from $0$ on the number line.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**2.** Work out $|2 - 7|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Work out the subtraction inside the bars first.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$2 - 7 = -5$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$|2 - 7| = |-5| = 5$$

Take the absolute value after the subtraction, never before.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**3.** Solve $|x| = 8$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Which numbers are $8$ units from $0$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

There is one on each side of $0$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$x = 8 \quad \text{or} \quad x = -8$$

Both are $8$ units from $0$. A modulus equation with a positive right-hand side
has two solutions.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**4.** Solve $|x - 3| = 4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The distance from $x$ to $3$ is $4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Split into $x - 3 = 4$ and $x - 3 = -4$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$x - 3 = 4 \quad \text{or} \quad x - 3 = -4$$

So $x = 7$ or $x = -1$.

Check: $|7 - 3| = 4$ and $|-1 - 3| = |-4| = 4$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**5.** Work out the distance between $-2$ and $5$ on the number line.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The distance is $|a - b|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$|5 - (-2)| = |7|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$|5 - (-2)| = |7| = 7$$

The gap is $7$ units. Subtracting in the other order gives $|-7| = 7$ as well.

</details>
</details>
</details>

**6.** Solve $|x| < 3$ and write the answer as an interval.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Which numbers are less than $3$ units from $0$?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

The answer runs from $-3$ to $3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$-3 < x < 3$$

The endpoints are not included, because the inequality is strict. Every number in
this interval is less than $3$ units from $0$.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## A question from Section 1.2

**7.** Simplify $\sqrt{12}$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the largest square factor of $12$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$12 = 4 \times 3$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$\sqrt{12} = \sqrt{4 \times 3} = 2\sqrt{3}$$

Absolute value and surds both take a size and remove something else: the sign in
one case, a square factor in the other.

</details>
</details>
</details>

## The chapter problem

**8.** The correct quote is €533.937096 and the estimator's quote is €449.064.
Work out $|533.937096 - 449.064|$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract, then take the absolute value.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

$533.937096 - 449.064 = 84.873096$.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$|533.937096 - 449.064| = 84.873096$$

The error is about €84.87. The profit the job was meant to make was about €48.54,
so the error is larger than the profit.

</details>
</details>
</details>

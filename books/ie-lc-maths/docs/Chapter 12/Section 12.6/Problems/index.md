# 12.6 The sine and cosine rules — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Label the triangle before you start, so each side sits opposite its matching angle. Then
ask what you know: a complete side-and-angle pair means the sine rule, and two sides with
the angle between them means the cosine rule.

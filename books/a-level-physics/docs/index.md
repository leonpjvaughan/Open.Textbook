# A-level Physics

Welcome. This is a complete, interactive A-level Physics book for England,
covering the core physics common to the three awarding bodies. Everything here is
free to read online, and there is nothing to install.

## How to use this book

Each section has three pages:

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Learn** | The teaching: ideas, worked examples, and something to try |
| **Practise with help** | Questions with hints you reveal only if you need them |
| **Practise** | Independent questions, with answers at the end |

Each chapter opens with a problem you will be able to solve by the end, and ends
with a **Review** that mixes the chapter's ideas together.

## The data and formulae booklet

A **Formulae** button sits at the bottom right of every page. It opens a
searchable booklet of the equations and data you are given in the exam. Choose
your exam board, and it shows only what that board provides.

## Checked questions

The questions and the answers in every **Practise** set are printed from the same
source, so they cannot disagree. A computer checks the arithmetic and the units
of each calculation when the book is built. It cannot check the physics, so read
the reasoning in **Learn** as well.

## Practical work

All three boards assess twelve set practical activities. Your teacher records
these in the **Practical Endorsement**, which is separate from the written exams
and carries no marks in them.

Practical skills are still examined. At least 15% of the written marks assess
practical knowledge and skills, so measurements, uncertainties, graphs and the
choice of apparatus are part of the assessment.

## The exam boards

This book follows the content common to the three awarding bodies:

- AQA 7407 and 7408
- Pearson Edexcel 9PH0
- OCR H556 (Physics A)

The content areas are the same. The papers and their weightings differ, so the
book stays board-neutral.

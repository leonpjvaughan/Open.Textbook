# Glossary — Materials

**Elastic limit** — the point on a force-extension graph beyond which a material
does not return to its original length when the load is removed.

**Elastic potential energy** — the energy stored in a stretched or compressed
material because of its change of shape.

**Hooke's law** — the extension of a spring or a wire is proportional to the
force applied to it, provided the limit of proportionality is not passed. In
symbols, $F = k\Delta L$.

**Limit of proportionality** — the point on a force-extension graph beyond which
the extension is no longer proportional to the force, so the graph stops being a
straight line.

**Plastic deformation** — a permanent change of shape that remains after the
load is removed.

**Spring constant** — the force needed to produce unit extension. It is the
gradient of a force-extension graph, and its unit is $\text{N}\,\text{m}^{-1}$.

**Strain** — the extension of a material divided by its original length. Strain
has no unit.

**Stress** — the force acting per unit cross-sectional area. The unit is the
pascal, $\text{Pa}$.

**Tensile** — describing a force or a stress that tends to stretch a material.

**Young modulus** — the ratio of tensile stress to tensile strain for a
material. The unit is the pascal.

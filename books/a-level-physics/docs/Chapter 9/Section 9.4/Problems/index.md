# 9.4 Electromagnetic induction — Practise

Work through these on your own. Find the change in flux linkage before you
divide by the time, and use Lenz's law to check the direction of every induced
current. Write the unit of every answer. In the questions that ask for a reason,
write your answer in full sentences.

The questions and the answers below come from a set that is checked by computer
every time the book is built.

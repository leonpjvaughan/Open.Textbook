# 9.3 Magnetic fields — Practise

Work through these on your own. Decide whether each question needs the force on
a wire or the force on a moving charge, and use Fleming's left-hand rule to
check every direction. Write the unit of every answer. In the questions that ask
for a reason, write your answer in full sentences.

The questions and the answers below come from a set that is checked by computer
every time the book is built.

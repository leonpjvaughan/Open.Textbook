# 9.1 Gravitational fields — Practise

Work through these on your own. Measure every distance from the centre of the
planet, keep the minus sign on every gravitational potential, and write the unit
of every answer. In the questions that ask for a reason, write your answer in
full sentences.

The questions and the answers below come from a set that is checked by computer
every time the book is built.

# 9.2 Electric fields — Practise

Work through these on your own. Change microcoulombs to coulombs and
nanometres to metres before you substitute, keep the signs of the charges, and
write the unit of every answer. In the questions that ask for a reason, write
your answer in full sentences.

The questions and the answers below come from a set that is checked by computer
every time the book is built.

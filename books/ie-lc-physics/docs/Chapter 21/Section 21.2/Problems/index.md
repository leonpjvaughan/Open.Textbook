# 21.2 Binding energy and the mass defect — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Take $1\ \text{u} = 1.66 \times 10^{-27}$ kg, $c = 3.0 \times 10^8$ m/s and
$1\ \text{MeV} = 1.6 \times 10^{-13}$ J unless a question says otherwise. A proton
has a mass of 1.0073 u and a neutron 1.0087 u. Give every numerical answer with its
unit and to the number of significant figures the data support.

# 6.3 Gravitational potential energy in a field — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Decide first whether the distance is small enough for $E_p = mgh$, or large enough that
$g$ changes and you need the field form. Keep the minus sign on any potential energy you
calculate, and remember that an **energy to lift** is a positive difference.

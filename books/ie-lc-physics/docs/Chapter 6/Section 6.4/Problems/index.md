# 6.4 Orbits and satellites — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Take $G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\,\text{N}\,\text{m}^{2}\,\text{kg}^{-2}$ and
$M = 5.97 \times 10^{24}\,\text{kg}$ for the Earth unless a question says otherwise.
Every radius is measured from the **centre** of the Earth, so add the height to the
radius before you start.

# 13.4 Electric field strength and potential — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Give every answer with its **unit**, and to the number of significant figures the data
justify. Take $k = 9.0 \times 10^{9}$ N m$^{2}$ C$^{-2}$. Decide first whether the
field is uniform, where $E = \dfrac{V}{d}$, or the field of a point charge, where
$E = \dfrac{kQ}{d^2}$.

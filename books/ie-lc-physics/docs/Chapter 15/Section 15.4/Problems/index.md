# 15.4 The force on a moving charge — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

Give every numerical answer with its **unit**, and to the number of significant
figures the data justify. Before you substitute into $F = Bqv$, write down which three
of the four quantities you know and which one you want. Keep the powers of ten tidy,
and take the charge on an electron as $1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ C.

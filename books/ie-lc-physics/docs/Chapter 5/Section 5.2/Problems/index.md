# 5.2 The spring constant and combinations of springs — Practise

Work independently. The questions and answers are shown together at the end.

For every combination, check your answer against the size of the individual spring
constants before you write it down: a series answer must be smaller than the smallest,
and a parallel answer larger than the largest.

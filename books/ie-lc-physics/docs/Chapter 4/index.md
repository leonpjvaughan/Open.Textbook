# Chapter 4: Work, energy and power

Lift a box onto a shelf and you do work. Push a car and you do work. In each case
energy moves from you to the object. In this chapter you learn to count that energy,
follow it from one store to another, and say how quickly it moves.

## The chapter problem

**The station that stores energy.** A pumped-storage station stores electricity by
pumping water up to a high reservoir. The upper reservoir is 300 m above the lower
one. When the station generates, $4.0 \times 10^5$ kg of water passes through the
turbines every minute, and the electrical output is 16 MW. When it pumps, the pumps
take 24 MW from the grid and lift the same mass of water back up each minute.

By the end of the chapter you can work out the work done lifting the water, the energy
it stores, the speed it reaches at the turbines, and the power and efficiency of the
station. You can also explain why the station gives back less energy than it takes in.

## By the end of this chapter you can

- calculate the work done by a force, and say when a force does no work;
- calculate kinetic energy and gravitational potential energy, with units;
- use conservation of energy to follow energy from one store to another;
- account for energy transferred to the surroundings as thermal energy;
- calculate power and efficiency, and explain why efficiency is less than 100%;
- choose between the work-energy model and the equations of motion.

## Where it is used

Lifting and carrying, braking a car, a falling stone, a bouncing ball, a cyclist on a
hill, a wind turbine, and every machine that turns one kind of energy into another.
The same ideas return when you stretch a spring in Chapter 5, study gravity in
Chapter 6, and measure heat in Chapter 8.

## Your turn to connect it

Think of something that stores energy and gives it back later. A dam, a trampoline, a
phone battery, a bungee rope, a pumped-storage station, or the water tank in a roof.

Choose one. Write down where the energy comes from, what it is stored as, and what it
becomes when it is released. You will be able to put numbers on it by the end of the
chapter.

# 5.2 Interactions of electromagnetic radiation with matter — Practise with help

Work each problem first. Open a hint only if you are stuck, and the full working
only to check.

## Warm-up: remember

Answer these from memory. They use ideas from Section 5.1.

1. Which group of the spectrum lies between infrared and ultraviolet?
2. Use $v = f\lambda$ to find $f$ when $v = 3.0 \times 10^8\,\text{m/s}$ and
   $\lambda = 0.50\,\text{m}$.
3. Write 10 μm in metres.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Visible light.
2. $f = \dfrac{v}{\lambda} = \dfrac{3.0 \times 10^8}{0.50} = 6.0 \times 10^8\,\text{Hz}$.
3. $1.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\text{m}$.

If question 2 went wrong, look again at rearranging the wave equation. If
question 3 went wrong, remember that micro means $10^{-6}$.
</details>

## Practise with help

**1.** Ultraviolet radiation has a frequency of $1.5 \times 10^{15}\,\text{Hz}$.
Calculate its wavelength.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

This is a wave equation calculation. You know the speed and the frequency, and
you want the wavelength.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Write $\lambda = \dfrac{c}{f}$ and put in
$f = 1.5 \times 10^{15}\,\text{Hz}$.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda &= \frac{c}{f} \\
        &= \frac{3.0 \times 10^8}{1.5 \times 10^{15}} \\
        &= 2.0 \times 10^{-7}\,\text{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

This is 200 nm, which is in the ultraviolet range.
</details>

**2.** A black metal plate and a shiny metal plate are placed in bright sunshine.
Explain which plate warms up faster, and why.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The radiation that reaches both plates is the same. The difference is what each
surface does with it.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Say which surface absorbs more infrared radiation, and what happens to that
absorbed energy.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The black plate warms up faster. A black, matt surface absorbs infrared radiation
well and reflects very little. The absorbed energy is transferred to the thermal
store of the plate, so its temperature rises quickly. The shiny plate reflects
most of the infrared radiation, so it absorbs less energy and warms up slowly.

This answer is a model answer for you to compare with your own wording.
</details>

**3.** A ray of light hits a plane mirror. The angle between the incoming ray and
the mirror is 30°. Work out the angle of reflection, and give a reason.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

The angle of incidence is measured from the normal, not from the mirror. The
normal is at right angles to the mirror.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Subtract 30° from 90° to find the angle of incidence. Then use the law of
reflection.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

The normal is at 90° to the mirror, so the angle of incidence is
$90^\circ - 30^\circ = 60^\circ$. The angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence, so the
angle of reflection is 60°.

The reason is the law of reflection: the angle of reflection equals the angle of
incidence.
</details>

**4.** A ray of light passes from air into a glass block. Describe what happens
to the ray, and explain why.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Compare the speed of light in air with its speed in glass. Most of the light
enters the glass, but a little does not.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

Say which way the ray bends, then link the bend to the change of speed at the
boundary.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Most of the light passes into the glass and bends towards the normal. A small
part of the light is reflected at the surface. Light travels more slowly in glass
than in air. The change of speed at the boundary is what makes the ray change
direction.
</details>

**5.** A hospital sterilises sealed packs of equipment with gamma radiation, and
images broken bones with X-rays. Explain why each kind of radiation is suitable
for its job.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Hint</summary>

Think about how far each kind of radiation travels through matter, and what it
does when it is absorbed.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Next step</summary>

For the gamma rays, say why they can reach inside a sealed pack. For the X-rays,
say how bone and soft tissue interact differently with the radiation.
</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Full working</summary>

Gamma radiation is very penetrating, so it passes through the sealed pack and
kills the microorganisms inside it. This sterilises the equipment without
opening the pack.

X-rays pass through soft tissue but are absorbed by denser bone. The bone
therefore casts a shadow on the detector, and the image shows the shape of the
bone.

This answer is a model answer for you to compare with your own wording.
</details>

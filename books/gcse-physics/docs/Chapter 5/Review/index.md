# Chapter 5: Review

## The chapter problem, solved

The observatory uses radio waves, infrared radiation and visible light. Take the
speed of electromagnetic waves in a vacuum as $c = 3.0 \times 10^8\,\text{m/s}$.

**1. The radio signal.** The signal has a frequency of 150 MHz.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda &= \frac{c}{f} \\
        &= \frac{3.0 \times 10^8}{150 \times 10^6} \\
        &= 2.0\,\text{m}
\end{aligned}
$$

The radio signal has a wavelength of 2.0 m.

**2. The infrared radiation.** The camera detects radiation with a wavelength of
10 μm.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f &= \frac{c}{\lambda} \\
  &= \frac{3.0 \times 10^8}{10 \times 10^{-6}} \\
  &= 3.0 \times 10^{13}\,\text{Hz}
\end{aligned}
$$

**3. The visible light.** The image uses light with a wavelength of 600 nm.

$$
\begin{aligned}
f &= \frac{c}{\lambda} \\
  &= \frac{3.0 \times 10^8}{600 \times 10^{-9}} \\
  &= 5.0 \times 10^{14}\,\text{Hz}
\end{aligned}
$$

**4. The order of the three.** Put them in order of increasing frequency.

| Radiation | Wavelength | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Radio | 2.0 m | $1.5 \times 10^8\,\text{Hz}$ |
| Infrared | 10 μm | $3.0 \times 10^{13}\,\text{Hz}$ |
| Visible | 600 nm | $5.0 \times 10^{14}\,\text{Hz}$ |

All three travel at the same speed, so the longest wavelength has the lowest
frequency. The order is radio, then infrared, then visible light.

**5. The projected picture.** The picture is 2.0 cm tall, and the image is
0.30 m tall. Change 0.30 m to 30 cm.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{magnification} &= \frac{30\,\text{cm}}{2.0\,\text{cm}} \\
                     &= 15
\end{aligned}
$$

The image is 15 times taller than the picture.

**6. The white walls.** A white surface reflects most of the infrared radiation
that reaches it and absorbs little. Less energy is transferred to the thermal
store of the building, so the inside stays cooler than it would with black
walls. A white surface is also a poor emitter, so the walls radiate little
energy back.

## What you can now do

- list the groups of the electromagnetic spectrum in order of wavelength and
  frequency;
- state that all electromagnetic waves travel at the same speed in a vacuum;
- use $v = f\lambda$ for any electromagnetic wave;
- describe absorption, reflection, transmission and refraction;
- give a use and a danger of each group of electromagnetic waves;
- draw a ray diagram for a convex lens, and describe real and virtual images;
- calculate the magnification of a lens;
- explain the colour of an object in terms of absorption and reflection;
- explain how temperature affects the radiation an object emits.

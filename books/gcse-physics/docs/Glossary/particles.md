# Glossary — Particle model of matter

**Change of state** — a physical change from one state of matter to another, such
as melting, freezing, boiling, evaporating, condensing or sublimating. The mass
is conserved, and the material gets back its original properties if the change is
reversed.

**Internal energy** — the total kinetic energy and the total potential energy of
all the particles in a system. Heating a system increases its internal energy.

**Latent heat** — the energy needed to change the state of a substance. It does
not change the temperature of the substance.

**Particle model** — a model that describes matter as tiny particles, and
explains the properties of solids, liquids and gases from the arrangement and
motion of those particles.

**Specific latent heat** — the energy needed to change the state of one kilogram
of a substance, with no change in temperature. It is measured in joules per
kilogram. The specific latent heat of fusion is the value for a change from solid
to liquid; the specific latent heat of vaporisation is the value for a change
from liquid to vapour.

# Glossary — Energy

**Closed system** — a system that no energy can enter or leave. In a closed
system the total energy stored does not change.

**Conservation of energy** — the principle that energy is never created and never
destroyed; it is only transferred between stores.

**Dissipation** — the transfer of energy to the surroundings in a way that is not
useful, usually to the thermal store of the surroundings.

**Efficiency** — the useful output energy transfer divided by the total input
energy transfer. It has no unit and can be written as a decimal or a percentage.

**Elastic potential store** — the energy stored in an object that is stretched or
squashed, such as a spring.

**Energy store** — a way in which energy is held in a system, such as the kinetic,
gravitational potential, elastic potential, thermal, chemical, nuclear, magnetic
or electrostatic store.

**Energy transfer** — the movement of energy from one store to another, by
heating, by work done by a force, or by work done when a current flows.

**Gravitational potential store** — the energy an object has because of its
height above the ground.

**Kinetic store** — the energy an object has because it is moving.

**Lubrication** — putting oil or another slippery substance between moving
surfaces to reduce friction and the energy transferred to the thermal store.

**Non-renewable resource** — an energy resource that is used up faster than it is
replaced, such as coal, oil, gas or nuclear fuel.

**Power** — the rate at which energy is transferred, measured in watts. One watt
is one joule per second.

**Renewable resource** — an energy resource that is replaced as it is used, so it
will not run out, such as wind, hydroelectricity or the Sun.

**Specific heat capacity** — the energy needed to raise the temperature of one
kilogram of a substance by one degree Celsius.

**System** — an object, or a group of objects, chosen for study.

**Thermal conductivity** — a measure of how quickly a material transfers energy
by conduction. A material with a higher thermal conductivity transfers energy
faster.

**Thermal store** — the energy held by the particles of a warm object because of
their motion.
